## Choices made while you slept (the careful pick each time)
**Wheels**
- Start closes a wheel and pauses.
- In Hold, triggers and bumpers do what they do today, nothing. Your "the wheel closes, the attack happens" applies to Tap and Toggle.

**Admin tools**
- Ban IP works with Permanent only, because only staff could lift a timed one.
- Nobody can kick, ban, warn, mute, bring or level someone ranked above them.
- Staff still read a muted player, marked.
- A player isn't told about an item spawn.
- A timed ban on a name ZeD has never seen is refused until that player has appeared once.

**Safehouses and factions**
- Names 3 to 14 characters, titles up to 30, tags saved in capitals.
- An invitation leaves a belt note and a toast too.
- Accepting and ending a war ask in the foot first, which the game doesn't do.

**Logs**
- Anyone who can answer tickets can read the log. Steam IDs and IP addresses stay with those who can ban.
- Your own car writes no lines except "drove off".
- The log keeps 2,000 lines. A busy server may want more.

**Community**
- Moderators can post news but can't edit the rules.
- A removed or moved rule doesn't ask people to agree again.
- Deleting a news post asks first.
- A report brings 5 chat lines from each side of the one you picked.
- Staff are asked to agree like everyone else.

**Extras**
- Safehouse "lot" protection reaches 5 tiles past the walls, because the game keeps no lot lines a server can read.
- Standing by a car is enough to claim it, no key needed.
- "Others" means everyone but the owner, faction included.
- New words beyond your drawing: a car at someone else's safehouse can't be claimed, and "what's built there" replaces "anything".
- New log wording is mine, such as "was refused taking parts from %1".

## What it can't do, so you can decide how to say it
- Join rules and timed bans are enforced by the player's own game. A modified game can get past them. Staff get a note if a banned client stays.
- The container log is reported by each player's own game, and its lines say so.
- Driving a claimed car can last up to 2 seconds before the engine is shut off.
- Past about 1 MB the game's own ticket list stops answering, and any modified client can cause that. ZeD's queue keeps working. This should go to The Indie Stone with the other report.
- A ticket lost in a crash with no save comes back with its kind but not its words. That's the cost of keeping reports private.
- The player can be bitten while reading the join screen, because the world runs on behind it.
