# GAMEPAD_SPEC — flat free-nav

**Status:** LOCKED (S113, April 26 2026 — design director). Reconfirmed in words Oct 11 2026: "D-pad is the most accurate way to navigate to items. A/B are left and right mouse. X/Y are E/F and Tab/I. Chords are excellent. Predictable and logical."
**Supersedes:** the ring model (S97–S112; see `docs/archive/GAMEPAD_SPEC_20260416.md`) and the stack model before it.
**Source of truth in code:** `Input/ActionMap.lua` (bindings), `Input/FocusManager.lua` (cone-nav, tap/hold timers, F9 overlay), `Input/BumperChord.lua` (zoom chord when no window is focused), `Input/InputModeDetector.lua`.
**Principle:** the gamepad is a mouse with a thumb. Every focusable element has the same callbacks for both input modes; focus paints exactly like hover; there is no gamepad-only state.

## Focus rules

1. Tabs switch on **A**, never on focus. Sub-tab pills (Journal, Animals) still live-switch on focus — open item, should match.
2. Disclosures, stack chevrons, card open/close and grid scroll are **A on the element**; there are no window-level D-pad intercepts. Pseudo-focusable surfaces (maps cursor, animals zone cursor, grid-scroll regions) take `onDpadDir(w, el, dir)` first and `return false` to fall through to cone-nav.
3. **A on an inventory row toggles the card**: open · live-swap to another row · close.
4. Focus visual = the player's **Controller highlight** setting (director, Oct 26 2026; `COHESION_SPEC.md` section 6), drawn by `ZeD.Focus.mark` in an overlay pass after the window's content: **Pointing hand** (default: the hover wash plus a pixel hand at the element's left edge), **Corner marks**, **One wash** (0.55 accent, words in `T.textOnFocus`) or **Marching ants**. The mark shows only in the focused window and zone (`Focus.isFocused`) and only while the controller is in use: any mouse travel or click hides it, the next pad press brings it back on the same element. No glow, no soft shadow; the title-bar zone underline is gone.
5. Scroll-follow fires on focus **change** (delta gate), never on input mode, because `InputModeDetector.getMode()` oscillates between frames (Q99).
6. Zones (`charTabBar`, `charContainerStrip`, `charMainInv`, `charTabContent`, `charItemCard` and the World mirrors) are storage only; dispatch flattens them. Builders keep calling `setRingZoneElements(win, zone, elements)`.
7. (Oct 23 2026, the director's first hands-on pad test.) **Entering a window** (LB, RB, Y, or Left / Right past a window's edge toward the other window) lands on the last focused element while it exists, else the item list's first row (`Focus.ensureEntry`, the window's `_focusEntryZone`); never on nothing lit. **Landing on a column from the side** lands on what it shows: a tab strip on the active tab, a container strip on the container on show, the item list on the row focus last had (`zone.landOn`). **A on a container** shows it and moves focus to its first item. **A on a row** opens the card with focus on its primary action (`isPrimary`: the card's CTA); in the card Up goes to the action strip in one press, Down to the next control, then reads on; B closes and returns to the row. **After an action** (card closed, menu closed, item moved) focus returns to the start, else its neighbour in the same panel. **The belt** is never entered from an open window; on the belt a direction with nowhere to go leaves it, as B does.

## D-pad wheels

With no window holding the pad (in the world), a tap on a D-pad direction opens a wheel and it stays up on its own: Left Character, Right World, Up Social, Down Here and now; Select opens the ZeD wheel (theme, accent, text size, UI scale, moodles, action bar, reset windows, ZeD setup, safe mode). The left stick keeps walking, and the right stick alone picks: past the engage radius a slice lights, and when the stick drops under the release radius the lit slice fires and the wheel closes (the angle on the release frame is never read). A flick onto a slice with the corner triangle opens that ring in place; flick there to take, B backs out one level. The same direction, or B at the top level, closes it with nothing taken; another direction swaps wheels; with nothing pointed at, it closes after 3 seconds. A, X and Y do nothing with a wheel up and don't reach the game. RT or LT closes the wheel, takes nothing and the aim or the swing goes through (in a vehicle the triggers stay the pedals). LB or RB tapped closes it and opens the windows on that side; held with a face button it assigns the chord and opens nothing, so the bumper acts on its release. Holding a bumper keeps the highlight while the thumb leaves the stick. Whichever way a wheel closes, the map above is back. ZeD setup, Controls: Wheel open (Tap, Hold), Wheel commit (Flick-release, Stick click), Wheel toggle mode (the stick or the D-pad moves the highlight, A takes, B closes, no timer), Wheel stick dead zone (0.20 to 0.60). Hold is the earlier way: up while the direction is held, letting go takes, inner wheels on A; with Flick-release, letting the stick go takes too. The feel constants are `ZeD.DpadWheels.tune`. The gesture is RADIAL_WHEELS_SPEC.md; the code is `ZeD_WheelGesture.lua`. With a window holding the pad the D-pad stays the window's (nothing above changes). The driver's seat changes nothing either: the dashboard takes the pad only from World > Vehicle > Dashboard, and B gives it back (director, Oct 24 2026). Paused: only Here and now and ZeD open. The whole design is DPAD_WHEELS_SPEC.md; the code is `ZeD_DpadWheels.lua`; QA `zedqa_dpad_wheels.lua`.

## What "parity" means for QA

For every surface in both windows: reach it by D-pad · see focus on it · A does what left-click does · X does what right-click does · B backs out of anything that has a back · the result is readable at both text sizes in all three themes. A surface that fails any of these is not done.
