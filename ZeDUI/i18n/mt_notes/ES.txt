Spanish (Spain), informal "tu" as the game's own menus (Pulsa, Elige, Haz clic). Spain terms: ordenador, archivo, ratón, coche, mechero, frigorífico, bombona, surtidor, cristal. LatAm differs: computadora, carro/auto, encendedor, refrigerador, tanque/garrafa, bomba, vidrio, "tomar" for coger, desempacar, "hacer clic".
Loadouts = "Kits" (invented; no game word); Outfits = "Conjuntos" (game word); Restock = "Reponer"; Unpack = "Desempaquetar" (LatAm: desempacar); Survey (map style) = "Catastro"; Full / Simple = "Completo" / "Simple"; belt = "cinturón"; drawer = "cajón"; card = "ficha"; wheel = "rueda"; Safe mode = "Modo seguro".
Preset names kept in English as names: Vanilla, Vanilla Plus, Vanilla Access, Plus, Access, Hardcore, Casual. ZeD picks, Skill Recovery Journal strings (Transcribe Into Journal, Bound Journal) and Module.Name left as written.
"Moodles" = "estados de ánimo" and "Sandbox" = "mundo abierto" (game's own ES). Sandbox option names inside the help texts use the game's ES labels (Horas para la reaparición del botín, Corte de agua, etc.). XP = EXP (game). "Setup" = "Ajustes de ZeD".
Action bar verbs are gerunds in capitals with accents/Ñ (pixel face supports them). Judgement calls to check against 14 chars: ECHANDO COMB., QUITANDO SEÑ., SACANDO LADRI., TENDIENDO RED, ARMANDO TRAMPA, MEDICÁNDOSE (pills), DESCUARTIZANDO (butchering), TOMANDO HUEVOS.
Hutch egg hatch = "tapa" (Tapa abierta / Cerrar tapa); hutch = "Gallinero" (game). Rancho / Comedero / Abrevadero follow the game. Pen and animal genetics: "Ansioso, Cobarde, Glotón" etc. are mine.
Short slot codes (849-871: Braz, Bols, Cint, Zap...) are guesses; no length limit given. 604 "< Volver" (game uses Volver for BACK). 1445/1413 kept AM / PM.
Tiles = "baldosas" (game glossary; "casillas" is more natural in Spain), shortened "bald." where the limit is tight. Month and day abbreviations: Ene Feb Mar Abr May Jun Jul Ago Sep Oct Nov Dic; VI LU SA DO JU MA MI on the watch.
The About text (1408) is rewritten in the same messy voice with deliberate missing accents and vosotros ("Chupaos", "podeis"); a LatAm version needs ustedes.
Key names: "Escape" kept as the English writes it; Return is written "Intro"; Up/Down in Ctrl+Up are "Arriba/Abajo". Flyer = "Folleto", Brochure = "Tríptico" not used (only Flyer appears).
Several narrow labels were cut to fit: Prog. 1:30, Desconoc., Sig. nivel, %1/%2 sabidas, Cint. I/D, Princ./Secund., Ext., Cond.
