Finnish (FI) notes for the native checker
1. Tone: imperative as in the game's own FI menus (Lue, Tallenna); "sinä" forms only where needed (Sinulla on, kätesi).
2. Action bar (pixel face, capitals, 14 max): action nouns (TANKKAUS, SYTYTYS, OTTO); Ä and Ö used, the pixel faces draw them.
3. Item = esine; tile = ruutu (game glossary says Laatat); container = säilö; zone = alue (karja-alue); Ranch tab = Tila.
4. 5. Preset names kept in English (Vanilla, Vanilla Plus, Vanilla Access, Plus, Access, Hardcore, Casual); "ZeD picks" kept as the helper mod's name.
6. "ZeD setup" rendered as ZeD-asetukset; Moodles = moodlet (as the game's FI); Electricity skill = Sähkötyöt.
7. Case endings avoided on placeholders ("Esineitä: %1", "kohteen %2 jälkeen", "Modi %1 ..."); counts put in parentheses where plural grammar was unclear.
8. Hair/beard: Trimmaa, Ajele, Muotoile, Sido; "Cut" = Leikkaa.
9. Month abbreviations are 3 letters (Tam Hel Maa Huh Tou Kes Hei Elo Syy Lok Mar Jou); compass letters P I E L KO KA LO LU; weekdays MA TI KE TO PE LA SU.
10. About text (line 1408): rough draft with deliberate typos kept in spirit ("ydnin" for kernel, no capital after full stops).
11. Date lines use "%2. %1 %3, %4" with a short month, no case endings.
12. Shortened for limits: Heikko hedelm., Latausjärj., Karttajärj., Päivitä (for Reload), Seur. lataus, Käsiv., Vedenkest., Seur. taso.
13. Doubtful: Hinaaja / Hinaa (Towed by / Towing), Kehä (circular rule), "mukaan siirretty" (carried, mod manager pins), Ruudukko for Grid.
