Survival Guide: both looks on one topic

The game's Skill Books topic in the Simple look (a ZeD window, laid out like the Crafting, Vehicle and Cooking tabs) and the Full look (the ZNIX manual, as in the gallery). The topic list and text are the game's own; the numbers under The rules in numbers are read from the game's files (42.22). Click topics, open and close sections, type in Find, and turn the pages in the book.

Look

What is the game's, what is new

  • The game's: the 15 sections, their topics, each topic's text and clip, the Show the guide on startup box (it still opens by itself on a new game).
  • New, under each section: The rules in numbers, read from the game's files and this world's sandbox, never from fan sites. Here: what each skill book volume covers and multiplies.
  • New sections at the foot of the list: ZeD, First days, Build 41 habits, Your controls, What's new in ZeD (topics shown, pages still to write).
  • The clip shown is the game's own (its inventory menu and skills panel). Clips with the game's menus get remade with ZeD's; the yellow tag marks one.

The two looks

  • Simple is the default: list left, topic right, Find and the start-up box in the band, Previous and Next at the right. The controller: D-pad in the list, A opens, RB and LB turn topics, B closes.
  • Full is the manual: the topic on the left page with the clip taped in, the rules on the right page, his notes and a sticky. Sections are the thumb tabs on the page edge; LB and RB turn the page.
  • Both read the same pages, so a topic written once shows in both.
