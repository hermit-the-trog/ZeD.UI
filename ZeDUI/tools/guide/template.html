Survival Guide: both looks on one topic

The game's Skill Books topic in the Simple look (a ZeD window, laid out like the Crafting, Vehicle and Cooking tabs) and the Full look (a printed manual, new from the bookstore, set like a 1990 newsstand survival magazine). The topic list and text are the game's own; the numbers under The rules in numbers are read from the game's files (42.22). Click topics, open and close sections, type in Find, and turn the pages in the book.

Look

What is the game's, what is new

  • The game's: the 15 sections, their topics, each topic's text and clip, the Show the guide on startup box (it still opens by itself on a new game).
  • New, under each section: The rules in numbers, read from the game's files and this world's sandbox, never from fan sites. Here: what each skill book volume covers and multiplies.
  • New sections at the foot of the list: ZeD, First days, Build 41 habits, Your controls, What's new in ZeD (topics shown, pages still to write).
  • The clip shown is the game's own (its inventory menu and skills panel). Clips with the game's menus get remade with ZeD's; the yellow tag marks one.

The two looks

  • Simple is the default: list left, topic right, Find and the start-up box in the band, Previous and Next at the right. The controller: D-pad in the list, A opens, RB and LB turn topics, B closes.
  • Full pages are set like Chapter 1 of the 1983 bookstore guide: a chapter's first topic opens with Chapter n, the chapter name in heavy italic capitals, a halftone photo with an italic caption and a bold Title Case line under it (try Movement); later topics carry their own title in the same face. The rules pages keep the 1990 magazine's furniture: a kicker over a heavy headline, an italic deck and a byline between rules, two justified columns, the clip as a boxed halftone, a grey-tint department bar, bold run-in subheads, charts in a ruled box with the caption inside. No marks of any kind. LB and RB turn the page.
  • The manual opens on its title page and contents (from a 1983 bookstore survival guide): numbered chapters, a line on each, dotted leaders to the page. F1 with nothing to jump to opens here; F1 from a tab opens its topic.
  • Both read the same pages, so a topic written once shows in both.
