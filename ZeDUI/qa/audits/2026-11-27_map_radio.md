# Radio tab: map for the gamut sweep (QA, Nov 27 2026)

A read-only map of ZeD's Radio tab (the radios, walkie-talkies, ham sets, the CD player and the TVs, in both windows) and the surfaces it shares its verbs with (the car radio up close, the dash's head unit, the face pop-out and the D-pad wheels' radio slices), set against the game's own radio window (B42: ISRadioWindow and its RWM modules, ISRadioAction and the device timed actions). Written before the sweep scripts. Line refs are as read on Nov 27. Nothing was run.

**The tab lives in both windows.** RadioTab.lua registers one renderer into the Character window (tab id "radio", ZeD's key UI_ZeD_Char_TabRadio, CW:57, 864-868) and the World window (vkey IGUI_ItemType_Radio, group 2 before Vehicle, WW:141-154, 1910). The drawing is the same; only the list differs: the Character window lists radios on the player, the World window radios and TVs in the 3 x 3 around him plus one pinned device (RT:5-10). The focus zone is charTabContent or worldTabContent (RT:383). The game's window never shows while ZeD runs outside safe mode (RW:83-94, OCR:32-41).

## 1. What the tab does

**Getting there:**
- The tab strip of either window, shown only while its list has a device (CW:864-868, WW:151-153). When the last one goes the window falls back to Loot (CW:3635-3645; WW:2167 via _tabLeft and lootDrawerFallback WW:92).
- The game's radio window is routed: every caller of ISRadioWindow.activate goes through RW:87-93 to WR.radio (WR:311-330). That covers a left-click on a set within 1.5 tiles (OCH:225-233), the world menu's Device Options on a TV, house radio or dropped radio (RTM:3-42), the inventory menu's Device Options on a radio in hand or on the back (IPCM:896-899), the controller's Interact prompt with a radio in hand (BP:312, 610-621) and the car wheel's Device Options slice for a device part that is not "Radio" (VMN:151-158, passed on by OCR:35).
  - A radio item: the Character window's Radio tab on that radio; a radio in a bag (not hands or back) is pinned as "Carried" (WR:231-260, RT:232-240).
  - A car's "Radio" part from a front seat: the car radio up close (WR:299-306, CR:860-887).
  - Any other device (a TV, a placed set, another car part): with faces on (ST.devicesSimple false) a set with a face pops out on its own (FP:360-370) instead of the tab; else the World window's Radio tab on that device, pinned while within 10 tiles on the same floor (WR:263-296, 140-147). A device on another floor opens nothing (WR:269, 324).
  - Split screen: a window owned by another player declines and the game's window opens (WR:65-69).
- Equipping a radio no longer opens anything: the game's onEquip auto-open is removed (RW:45-58, 75-78); ZeD refreshes the Character window's list so the tab shows at once (RW:102-125).
- D-pad wheels: the Character wheel's Radio tab slice ("No radio on you", DW:1018-1020); the World wheel's Radio slice ("No radio in reach", named Television with a TV showing, DW:1136-1144); the Use wheel's Radio sub-wheel on the first equipped radio: Turn on or off, Next station (RO.seek), the Radio tab (DW:1088-1089, 1569-1585).
- The car's own radio: the dash's head unit (DB:629-656) and the car radio up close (CR). These share RO with the tab and are in this map's cases, but they are not the tab.
- Safe mode: the onEquip auto-open comes back and every route opens the game's window (RW:80, 88; OCR:33, 57 puts a car radio away); FP.on is false (FP:68-76).

**Data:**

- World list (buildWorldRadios RT:251-314): IsoWaveSignal objects on the 9 squares of his floor (his own included), each neighbor through canReachTo, in MP also SafeHouse.isSafehouseAllowLoot (RT:263-307); sorted by distance; then the pinned device (WR.addPinned WR:179-215, tagged "Floor" or "Vehicle"). - Per frame: the face's reading (RTV.read RTV:404-446 or TWF.read TWF:180-205 or RO.read RO:185-230 for the car). The fill blocks under the face are read every 500 ms (RT:694).
- Heard lines: the game's OnDeviceText per device key, the last 8, cleared on a channel change or tape start or stop (RTV:352-380).
- Kept between frames: _radioIdx and _radioFollow (the tab follows the device while the list re-sorts, RT:849-852); RO's short-lived state per device key: flash, scan, lamp, the volume before MUTE, a remembered Stop (RO:141, 157, 462-467).

## 2. Against the game

The game's window (IRW:111-123) stacks modules a device enables (IRW:219-252): General (name, channel name, frequency, range, two-way, strength; not for a NoTransmit set, GEN:28-73), Power (battery sets: on/off, a battery drop box, the charge bar, POW:54-110), Grid power (mains sets: on/off and Power nearby, GRID:36-86), Signal (the wave and, high tier, the last distance, SIG:47-82), Volume (speaker mute, a slider, the headphone box on portable non-TV sets only, VOL:49-140), Microphone (two-way only, MIC:24-50), Media (an LCD, a drop box, Play / Stop, MED:45-200), Channel (radios: a preset list, Tune in, Add, Edit, Delete with a name and a free frequency at 0.2 MHz, CH:88-160, 210) and TV Channel (a preset list and Tune in, TVC:46-105). Every button queues an ISRadioAction or a device action after doWalkTo: an adjacent walk for a set in the world, nothing for an item and only from inside the car for a car part (PAN:43-56). A TV remote skips the walk (canPlayerRemoteInteract, POW:56). It closes past 10 tiles; closing on an item no longer in the hands or on the back also turns the radio off (IRW:125-159). Pad: A power, Y volume, X mic, RB walks the modules (IRW:271-285).

## 3. Cases

## 4. Suspected bugs (no runs)

- **B1** suspected: no ZeD control puts a battery into or takes one out of any two-way set (the walkies, the ManPack, the three ham sets, all UsesBattery in RIS). The two-way faces have no battery key (PX hits), PF reads a two-way battery only (PF:291-292) and nothing else in ZeD queues ISDeviceBatteryAction (RTV:760-764 only). With ZeD on, a walkie whose battery dies can't be fed.
- **B2** suspected: headphones are offered where the game never allows them and missing where it does. RTV.phonesKey (RTV:832-844) and the fill's Headphones row (RT:669-672) work on every non-two-way device, TVs and car parts included; the game allows headphones on portable non-TV sets only (VOL:123), walkies included; ZeD's two-way faces have no phones key.
- **B3** suspected: verbs on a pinned world device act from up to 10 tiles with no walk. RO.run performs at once (RO:248-261) and the battery, media and headphone actions are queued with no walkAdj (RTV:755-769); the game walks adjacent first (PAN:43-56). The same holds for a car part pinned from outside the car (WR:263-296), where the game's buttons do nothing. A stakes question for the director rather than a crash.
- **B4** suspected: with two devices on one square, a queued battery or media action can land on the other device. RTV.queue fills a.deviceData only when the parameter gave none (RTV:756), but the parameter is the square and IsoGridSquare:getDeviceData answers with one of them (DBA:33-50); the comment at RTV:756 says it handles this.
- **B5** suspected: the fill's "Spare xN" counts every Base.Battery including dead ones (getCountTypeRecurse, RT:657-664), while the battery key only takes one with charge left (RTV:819-827): "Spare x1" next to a press that answers "No batteries on you".
- **B6** suspected: the CD player's fill shows a Channels block, a Heard block and a "Frequency range 88.0-108.0 MHz" row (RT:630-647, 674-677 test only for a TV); the game hides General, Signal and Channel for a NoTransmit set (GEN:31, SIG:48, CH:211).
- **B7** suspected: the pop-out of a two-way radio carried in a bag closes on its first reach check. RT offers the button for any face (RT:898), TWV.inReach asks TWF.inReach (TWV:190), which accepts only the hands and the back (TWF:360-365); RTV.inReach accepts the bag (RTV:950-958).
- **B8** suspected: pad focus on a two-way face lands on the radio's tab, not on VOL. The two-way builder adds the tab stops first (RT:1212) and seeds focus with elements[1] (RT:1238); the ordinary and plain builders put the face first (RT:1118-1148, 1162-1179). The comment at RT:1214 says the full view button comes first.
