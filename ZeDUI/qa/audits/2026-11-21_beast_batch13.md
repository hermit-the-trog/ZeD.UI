# Beast day, batch 13 (Nov 22 2026, QA): what the batch 11 proof found in B11-2's fix

Found by the writer of ZeDUI/tools/qa/vx11_craft.lua while proving batch 11 on c77b5b0fc (its stubbed run: 23 of 24
checks pass on HEAD; the one fail is B13-1), each confirmed by QA reading the code (lines below).

### B13-1 The Build tab's metal and welding rows still blink and the Build list still re-sorts every scan (B11-2 half done)

- What the player meets: on the Build tab, a recipe that needs learning and is not learned (Metal floor and the other
  metal and welding entities) still flips between unlocked and locked every scan, and the Build list is sorted again
  every ~1.2 s with nothing changing (vx11_craft's 6.5 s watch on Metal floor).
- Cause: BuildingTab.lua:246 sets status = hasSkill and "unlocked" or "locked" BEFORE the new learned block (:248-255),
  so learned reaches CC.refreshRow (:256) but never the status. refreshRow writes "unlocked" (CraftingCore.lua:575-588:
  carry needs a craftable or missing row); the scan then says locked (:522 info:isValid() false, and :526 rd.learned ==
  false) and reports a change (:528); the next 1 s refresh writes unlocked again.
- Fix: compute learned first, then local status = (hasSkill and learned) and "unlocked" or "locked", as CraftingTab does
  (CraftingTab.lua:284-289).
- Smaller, same tab: the Build catalog's entries carry no needLearn, so Show: Known lists unlearned metal recipes (the
  Known filter reads entry.needLearn, CraftingCore.lua:1121-1127); set needLearn from recipe:needToBeLearn() when the
  catalog is built.

### B13-2 With a locked recipe's card open, its row flips and the Craft list re-sorts every scan

- What the player meets: open the card of a recipe he has not learned or lacks the skill for (from a search, an item
  card or Show: All): its row's square flips between red (locked) and gold or green every ~1.2 s and the whole list
  re-sorts each time. (Code reading; vx11_craft deliberately never opens a locked card for this reason.)
- Cause: CraftingTab gives the row locked (CraftingTab.lua:284-289) and refreshRow writes it with fromLogic nil
  (CraftingCore.lua:586); in the same frame the open card's gatherDetail overwrites it (CraftingCore.lua:1360-1362: if
  rd.status ~= "station" and not rd.fromLogic then rd.status = missing or craftable); the scan writes locked back (:526)
  and reports a change (:528); the next refresh cannot carry (its status is locked, :578-580) and the cycle repeats.
- Fix: leave a locked row alone there: if rd.status ~= "station" and rd.status ~= "locked" and not rd.fromLogic then.
