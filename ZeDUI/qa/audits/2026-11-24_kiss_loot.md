# KISS test: looting and inventory (Nov 24 2026, QA)

The loot pass of the gamut sweeps. The director, Nov 23 2026: every micro QA pass also gets a KISS test. The player
in mind picks the game up for half an hour, reads nothing and wants to loot and get back to playing.

How it was done: a read of the code as it stands on main (ef70cc050), job by job, against the game's own Lua, after
the Nov 22 audit (2026-11-22_kiss_bugs_K1.md, _fixes_K2.md, _calls_K3.md) and the fixes built since. QA checked
every bug below in the code itself. The loot sweep (lv_move.lua, lv_items.lua) ran beside it in the game.

ZeD paths are under 42/media/lua/client/ZeD/. Game paths are under the game's media/lua/client/ISUI/.

## Steps per job (ZeD against the game)

| Job | Mouse, ZeD | Mouse, game | Pad, ZeD | Pad, game |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Loot all at a crate | 2 | 1 to 2 | 3 (Y, Left, X) | 4 or more |
| 2 Take one thing | 1 | 1 | 3 | 3 |
| 3 Part of a stack (Grab half) | 2 | 2 | 5 or more | 4 or more |
| 4 Transfer all | 2 | 1 | about 5 | 5 or more |

| 6 Switch containers | 1 | 1 | 3 | 1 (RB) |
| 7 Bag to bag | 1 | 1 | about 5 | about 4 |
| 8 Drop | 1 to 2 | 1 to 2 | 4 to 5 | about 5 |
| 9 Car (trunk, glove box) | 2 | about 2 | about the same | about the same |
| 10 A zombie's body | as a crate | as a crate | as a crate | as a crate |
| 11 Worn thing into a bag | 2 | 2 | about 4 | about 4 |
| 12 Find one thing | Filter in the heading | no filter | L3 sorts | sort only |

Mouse counts are level or one step over, where the game's loot page is always on screen and ZeD's World window
opens on a click. The pad is behind on switching containers (RB turns tabs in ZeD) and one press behind on stacks
and bag to bag.

## Bugs (QA checked each in the code)

1. "Put in Container (X)" in the item menu fills the game's hidden loot page's container, not the one ZeD shows.
   The game builds the row from getPlayerLoot(player).inventory (ISInventoryPaneContextMenu.lua:719, 928, 2870).
   ZeD takes the page's container into the World window (ZeD_WorldWindow.lua:7058-7094) but never the other way, so
   after the player picks another container in the World window the row still names and fills the old one. Seen in
   the game on Nov 24: a body near the crate, the World window on crate A, the row read "Put in Container (Corpse)"
   and the battery went into the body (lv_move case 11). The same page feeds the Move to submenu's loot entry
   (ISInventoryPaneContextMenu.lua:719).
2. Things land in different bags depending on how they were taken. Loot all and pad X on a row go to the bag on show
   (ContainerControls.lua:790, 375-376; the director's Nov 24 call). A double-click goes to the main inventory
   (InventoryRenderer.lua:1099), as do the card's Grab (ItemCard.lua:1080), pad X on a strip button
   (ZeD_WorldWindow.lua:354) and the item menu's Loot all (Overrides/ItemMenu.lua:226). The game's double-click
   goes to the bag on show (ISInventoryPane.lua:1159, 1190).
3. Seated with the window shut, To floor stays lit and does nothing, with no word why (ContainerControls.lua:803-809).
   The game grays Drop and says why (ISInventoryPaneContextMenu.lua:641-648).
4. The setting tip for worn and held things still says "at the top" (UI.json:2572; the comment at
   InventoryRenderer.lua:2454 too). They stand last since Nov 23.
5. Lite drops Same type and Same type nearby on the ground that the game lacks them (ContainerControls.lua:630-635).
   The game has them: InventoryWindow/Handlers/TransferSameType.lua and LootWindow/Handlers/TakeSameType.lua.

## Design calls (for the director)

1. On a pad nothing at the row Y lands on says how to reach Loot all (Left, then X), and Up goes to Pick all.
2. On a pad RB turns tabs; in the game it goes to the next container (ISInventoryPage.lua:876-879).
3. A second Y goes back to the old crate's row instead of the crate he now faces.
4. On a narrow card Grab moves into More (ItemCardOrganism.lua:1211-1235).
5. Grab half on a pad takes one more press than in the game (A for the card, then A for the menu).
6. No room says nothing for a pad X or for the floor.
7. The Filter dims rows that don't match instead of hiding them (InventoryFilter.lua:8-10).

Not a finding: Transfer all with the World window shut drops on the floor under the same word, as the director
called it on Nov 24 ("as vanilla does").
