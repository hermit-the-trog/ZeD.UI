### C1. A key changed in the Keyboard map is put back by the next Apply in Options

- Where: ZED/ZeD_KeyboardMap.lua:172-189 (KM.setKey) and 192-204 (KM.resetAll)
- Steps: in a game, window menu, Keyboard map. Click the box for Toggle Inventory, press K. Close. Pause, Options,
  change anything (a volume), Apply.
- What goes wrong: the action is back on its old key, in the game and in keysB42.ini. Before the Apply, the Key
  bindings page of Options already shows the old key, not K.
- Why: setKey tells Core and writes the file, but never the game's own list that Options keeps in memory:
  - GAME/client/OptionScreens/MainOptions.lua:2187 `keyTextElement.keyCode = tonumber(v.key) or 0` (filled once,
