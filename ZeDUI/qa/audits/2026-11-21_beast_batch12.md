# Beast day, batch 12 (Nov 21 2026, QA): the controller wheels' balloon tail

From the zoom check of the retaken B7-3 pictures; traced by one agent and attacked by a second (workflow

### B12-1 On every ZeD wheel the balloon's tail points at the top slice, not the slice it names (a look regression from beta 10)

- What the player meets: with a controller (and with the mouse), the balloon above any ZeD wheel names the lit slice
  correctly, but its tail always drops onto the 12 o'clock slice. B7-3: "Sit on: Green Armchair / 2 tiles" points at
  Sit on ground while the armchair tile at 4 o'clock is lit; "Sleep / Single Bed with Black Frame" points at slice 1 while
  the bed tile at 6 o'clock is lit (tail tip x 1920, y 987 and 944 at 4K; lit tiles centered at 1961,1103 and 1919,1171).
  Also visible, smaller, in the live 1.0 gallery picture upload_v1/00b_on_a_controller.jpg ("Belt" lit at about 11
  o'clock, the tail on the top tile). Not QA staging: W.qaPoint, the real stick latch (ZeD_WheelGesture.lua:502), toggle
  mode (:265), pad game wheels (ZeD_Wheel.lua:465) and the mouse (:467) all only set menu._zedPoint, which the placement
  never reads.
- Cause: W.draw builds the words from the pointed slice (ZeD_Wheel.lua:384-406) but places the balloon from a fixed
  point, the ring's apex (cx, apexY, :408 and :416); drawContextBalloon makes that a 2 x 1 px anchor (Molecules.lua:4575).
  The approved wheel design is a fixed balloon CENTERED above the ring with a small nub on the center line
  (ZeDUI/mockups zed_wheels_v1.html:72-73, :111, :263-277, :334; zed_dpad_wheels_v2.html:404-419), a caption, not a
  pointer. Beta 10's Balloon Help (2eadf8fca) gave every balloon a 10-cell tail placed from its tip with the box's
  corner 6u to the left (Balloon.lua:447-449, :492-500), whose contract is that the tail points at its control
  (Balloon.lua:9-14; handoff "tails on target", HANDOFF_design_tooltips_balloon_20261108.md:134). That commit never
  touched ZeD_Wheel.lua (the wheel is not in the handoff's table, :16-24; state.snug at ZeD_Wheel.lua:410 is read
  nowhere), so the wheel's caption became a pointer at slice 1, and its box now hangs 130-150 px right of the ring's
  center (box x 1909-2193 and 1909-2228 at 4K, ring center 1920).
- Fix (back to the approved look): no tail on the wheel's balloon, and the box centered on the ring.
  1. Balloon.lua:751: if not opts.noTail then drawTail(p, _box, pl, ox, oy) end (document noTail near :735; the frame's
     opening is drawn only by drawTailRaw, :554-556, so the box stays closed).
  2. Molecules.lua:4580: pass noTail = state.noTail into Bn.draw's opts; and when state.noTail is set, center the box on
     centerX: a.x = x0 + centerX - floor(Bn.layout(c).w / 2) + T.px(Bn.INSET) - 1 (B.layout is public), optionally
     lowered by the tail's depth so it sits just above the ring.
  3. ZeD_Wheel.lua:410: state.noTail = true in place of the dead state.snug.
  This covers the title-only balloon and the under-the-ring fallback (:411-415) too. A tail moved onto the lit tile
  instead would be a new look for the director (it collides with neighbor tiles and the crumb tag at 6 o'clock).
- QA after the fix: a wheel check that with slice k > 1 pointed the balloon draws no tail and its box is centered on the
  ring (B.draw opts and the box's x), at 4K and 1280x800; the B7-3 pictures again.
