# KISS audit, batch K1 (Nov 22 2026, QA): the plain bugs

From the KISS audit (Keep It Super Simple): nine agents walked the everyday jobs against vanilla (147 findings), one
skeptic per family tried to refute each (86 confirmed, 59 confirmed but milder, 2 refuted), and a ranker merged them
into 61 themes. These 15 are plain bugs: ZeD does the wrong thing by itself, so they need no call from the director.
The design calls (looks and behavior) go to the director as choices first. Ranked by harm, then by score
(frustration x frequency). Lines marked "read from code" want one look in game; QA will prove each fix in game.

### K1-1 The Medicine chord pulls a good bandage off before it treats a fresh wound

- Findings: survive-2, pad-4 (theme #1, score 15).
- What the player meets: After a fight he presses RB + A, the default Medicine chord: it walks the body in the game's order, hands first, and takes the first thing offered there, which on a bandaged part is Remove bandage, so yesterday's clean forearm bandage comes off while the bite on his leg keeps bleeding. With two fresh wounds the second press takes off the bandage the first press put on, and the Medicine list on D-pad Left puts pills first and stops at ten lines.
- Fix: Build the Medicine list and its pick worst wound first, in the Health tab's order (HealthTab.lua:704-709), not the body's index order, which also takes parts that are only bandaged (ZeD_DpadWheels.lua:1306-1310); within a part take the care order (glass or bullet out, disinfect, then a clean bandage), leave Remove bandage out of the pick unless that bandage is dirty, never pick a dirty bandage while a clean one is carried and list treatments before pills so the cut at ten drops pills, not wounds (C.medicine at ZeD_DpadWheels.lua:933-940, C.medicinePick at 944-948), as docs/specs/QUICK_CHORDS_SPEC.md:42 already says.

### K1-2 The item card's big button drinks bleach and gasoline, and its Eat skips the game's checks

- Findings: survive-1, survive-5 (theme #2, score 12).
- What the player meets: He clicks his disinfectant to clean a cut and the card's one bright button reads Drink: it drinks the whole bottle of rubbing alcohol, and bleach and a 10 liter gas can get the same button. On a pad, holding A on the row drinks it too, though the Controller map teaches that hold as the way to mark rows, and gasoline is a deadly poison in the game. The same card's Eat runs on a can that needs opening without opening it and looks ready when he is stuffed but does nothing, while a double-click on the row still runs the game's careful version.
- Fix: Drink: offer it on the card and on the pad's hold only where the game does, a beverage not known to be tainted in a container of 3 L or less (vanilla ISInventoryPaneContextMenu.lua:437-443, 2318-2322; ISInventoryPane.lua:1039), where today any unsealed fluid container gets it (ItemCardOrganism.lua:346, 442-446; InventoryRenderer.lua:687-696, 769-771) and it drinks all of it (ItemCard.lua:964-965); bleach keeps its Drink in the right-click menu only. Eat: run the opening recipe first, as the quick wheel's eat already does (ZeD_QuickWheel.lua:145-161), and gray it with the game's Too full to eat more when he is stuffed (vanilla ISInventoryPaneContextMenu.lua:499-503), on the card (ItemCard.lua:943-946) and on the pad's hold (InventoryRenderer.lua:674-682). QA confirms in game what a sealed can does today.

### K1-3 Quick Eat picks raw chicken first, and quick Drink never checks for tainted water

- Findings: survive-3, pad-13 (theme #3, score 8).
- What the player meets: He carries a can of beans and fresh raw chicken and presses LB + A: the pick is whatever spoils soonest, so he eats the whole raw chicken, which the Eat wheel lists first with a green Fresh. LB + B drinks water first without asking whether it is tainted, and a clean and a tainted bottle of the same kind fold into one line, so the tainted one can be the pick while clean water sits in his bag.
- Fix: Give the chord and the Eat and Drink lists the game's double-click test (vanilla ISInventoryPane.lua:990-995, 1063): skip burnt food, known poison and raw food the game marks dangerous uncooked (isFood, ZeD_QuickWheel.lua:69-76) and known-tainted water (isWater, ZeD_QuickWheel.lua:83); keep clean and tainted water apart in byKind (121-131); and tag the wheel bubble and the hover card Dangerous uncooked (ZeD_DpadWheels.lua:776-781; ZeD_LootCard.lua:76-83). Rotten food last is the written spec (docs/specs/QUICK_CHORDS_SPEC.md:19), so leaving it out entirely is the director's call.

### K1-5 On a pad, RB + B draws whichever weapon the bag lists first, and can put away the one in hand

- Findings: fight-6, pad-18 (theme #5, score 6).

### K1-6 Hide the interface sits in every window's menu with no way back for a mouse

And when the game shows its UI again after Escape and Resume (vanilla MainScreen.lua:1717-1719; ISUIHandler.lua:5-27), bring back the belt, watch and moodles too, which wait on ISUIHandler.allUIVisible (ZeD_Belt.lua:902; ZeD_Watch.lua:733; ZeD_Moodles.lua:322).

### K1-7 Craft stays gray with every row green, and the line under it blames a workbench

- Findings: craft-2 (theme #23, score 8).
- What the player meets: He opens a card, every Required row is green, yet Craft stays gray and a line says Needs a workbench or station nearby; by hand that line only ever shows on recipes that need no bench. The real cause is one the rows can't show, a spool with too few uses of thread or no table within two squares for a recipe made on any surface, so he goes looking for a workbench he does not need.
- Fix: When every row reads green but the game says no, ask the game which input is short (getRecipeData():getDataForInputScript(input):isInputItemsSatisfied(), as fillPickSlot reads it at CraftingCore.lua:2521-2527) and mark that row red, since the rows count whole items and a uses-based input keeps amount 1 (822-837); word the line by cause: a new Needs a table or counter nearby line when the recipe is any-surface and CC.craftSurface finds none (221-232), the workbench line (UI.json:594) only for a bench recipe (benchLine at CraftingCore.lua:2921-2925, drawn at 3162-3167).

### K1-9 The hood stays open after a look, and E at the hood opens the tab again

### K1-10 An item's Craft misses recipes, and the search box keeps old words

- Findings: craft-7, craft-11 (theme #28, score 6).
- What the player meets: He right-clicks a tool and picks Craft, and the Craft tab opens with the tool's name typed in the search and no recipe open; the search matches only the first item each ingredient slot lists, so a recipe his tool fits is missed when the tool isn't that first item, and an old Category pick can leave Nothing matches. When he is done the words stay in the box, which keeps Show from applying, and there is no clear X, so he selects them and deletes them.
- Fix: Match a search against every type an ingredient slot takes (validTypes, already built at CraftingCore.lua:792-819) instead of the first item's name only (CC.getInputName at 89-96, used at 786 and 1102-1107); an item's Craft resets the category as a recipe open does (Overrides/Stations.lua:28-61 sets activeCat only when a recipe is named); and turn on the text box's own clear button in ZeD_SearchField (Molecules.lua:808-834), as the game's recipe filter does (vanilla ISWidgetRecipeFilterPanel.lua:22).
