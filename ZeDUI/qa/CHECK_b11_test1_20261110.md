# Beta 11 tests 1 and 2: the in-game checks (Mac, Nov 10 2026)

Builds: `0.3.0-beta11-test1` (8028ca63) and `0.3.0-beta11-test2` (test 1 plus 114, the desk's sticky note), from
`claude/keen-meitner-optdjj` (PR #3). Test 2 replaces test 1: run test 2. Install the zip into
`~/Zomboid/mods` (it holds ZeD-UI and ZeD_Picks), replacing any ZeD-UI folder there, and unsubscribe from the Workshop
item while testing. The dev tools are in (the `-debug` QA bridge), as for every test build.

Each item's own Build paragraph in ZeDUI/PUNCH_LIST.md says what changed and why; the checks are copied here. Every
item passed its sim on Linux; none has been seen in the game yet. Report each as ok, wrong (what you saw, a picture),
or not run.

## 127. PZ Teal is the default accent
- Delete `accentColor` from `~/Zomboid/Lua/ModOptions.ini` (or use a clean cachedir) and start: the main menu, a window
  and a balloon are PZ Teal.
- Set Orange in the options, restart: still Orange.

## 138. Menu marks and the new icons
At 1x and 1.75x, in Dark, Light and High contrast:
- The strip, a wheel and the list menu: blue on Equip, Take, Zoom in; red on Unequip, Put in, Drop, Dispose's lid and
  the strike of an "off" (Turn off light).
- The pointed cell or row has no color left: the whole icon in one color.
- The list menu's shades are the theme's own (lighter blue and red on Dark and High contrast, deep blue and rust on
  Light); the strip and the wheels keep the paper's blue and red.
- Eat (and Eat all, half, quarter) shows the figure with a fork; Sneak in the wheel and the emotes shows the creeping
  figure, Stop sneaking with its x top left; the three safehouse rows show the boarded-up house (claim with a blue
  plus, release with a red minus); Disassemble's screwdriver has its handle up.
- The 24 px plus sits centered (Equip in a wheel at 1.75x).

## 133. The strip pages by whole icons
- A menu with 15 or more options (a crate of mixed things, a car): click the right arrow 3 times, then the left 3
  times, at 1x and 1.75x. No icon is cut at either end; each click shows the next 10, none skipped or repeated; you
  land back on the first page.
- The arrow keys and the D-pad still move one cell at a time.

## 130. A changed role shows on the Player card (two players on a local server)

  inventory past 2 s): the list moves no more than 4 px (it slid 57).

## 114. The desk's sticky note moves and closes (test 2)
- Drag the note by its top strip to the desktop's middle; quit to the desktop and start again: it is where you left it.
- Drag it past each edge: it stops on the desktop, under the menu bar.
- Close it with the x box at the strip's right end; ZeD menu > Show note: back where it was (Show note is gray while
  the note shows).
- On a pad: select the note; A (picked up: its shadow lengthens), the D-pad a few times, A (put down, kept); A, D-pad,
  B (back where it was); X (the keyboard comes up); Y (closed). The foot's prompts name each.
- With the keyboard, Enter on the note still puts the caret in it.
- At 1x and 1.75x.
