Loot hover card

The card a looting player sees the moment the pointer lands on a row, built from ZeD's own parts: the balloon (surface 2, the strong border, the hard shadow, square corners, the stepped tail), the item's name in the heading face, status tags, the five-cell condition bar, and two or three figures for its kind. Holding Alt swaps in the game's full tooltip, so lines other mods add are one key away. The item card stays a click away. Example items and figures; the drawing follows ZeD's sizes at the scale you pick.

How it behaves

  • At once, as vanilla's tooltip: no pause. It follows the pointer from row to row in the same frame, in the list and the grid.
  • It stands beside the list, its tail on the hovered row, so it never covers the rows below the pointer. Without room on the right it goes to the left.
  • It goes while items are dragged, while a selection box is drawn, while a right-click menu is open, while that item's card is open, and on a controller.
  • Kinds: a weapon shows damage and reach (a gun its ammo); food its hunger, thirst and freshness; clothing bite and scratch protection; a bag its capacity, what it holds and its weight reduction; a book its progress. Every card: name, tags, condition when the item has one, weight.
  • The help line under the list says Alt shows the game's full tooltip.
