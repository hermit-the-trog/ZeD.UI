### 149. The belt's hand rings in segments
The director: "you divide a ring like a pie or a clock from the center to the edge."
- Items in both hands at about 92%, 55%, 10% and broken: five, three, one and no lit cells, green, yellow, red.
- The gaps run along the radius like a clock's five marks (twelve, and every fifth of the way round), each lit cell
  ending on its gap; no flat horizontal or vertical bars across the ring.
- At 1x and 1.75x, in Dark, Light and High contrast; Status colors set to Color blind: the colour-blind colours.
- (Corrected after the run: a liquid's ring in ten blue cells comes only with Item Condition Restored on, from its
  meter; without it the ring shows the container's own condition, as a saucepan's five green cells did.)

### 150. The inventory narrower where a full card would shift the window (1280 x 800)
- Reset both windows (or a fresh profile): both inventories open narrower than before; the Cond column is gone.
- Open Equip's full card, then Health, then close them: the Character window grows left as its drawer opens, as
  always, but its right edge and the list never shift (the 4 px push and the 53 px squeeze are gone).
- The World window's full card (Crafting or the vehicle's): the same, nothing moves.
- If the rig can, 1366 x 768: narrower too, the same. At 1920 x 1080 and 4K: the windows as before (the usual width,
  the Cond column there).

### 148. The Health tab's percent
- The window from the picture (1920 x 1080, 1x, Dark, 700 tall): Health with injuries, the rows scrolled into view:
  each row's percent whole, "%" and all. Again at 1.75x, and at the default height scrolled down.

### 135. The hint strip and the belt's card
- Hover a held item on the belt: while ZeD's card is up the "Right Click More" hint strip steps aside; it comes back
  when the pointer leaves.
