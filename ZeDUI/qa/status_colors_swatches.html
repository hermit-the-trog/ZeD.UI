ZeD status colors

The five-cell status bars from punch list 143, drawn with ZeD's bar geometry and theme colors. On the left, today's green, yellow and red. On the right, the proposal: good and bad take the menu marks' blue and red-orange, and warning gets lighter (darker in Light) so it stands apart from red by brightness. Each row puts the same panels through a color-blindness simulation (Machado 2009, full strength).

How far apart the three colors are

Each cell gives the color difference (CIELAB ΔE) for good–warning, good–bad and warning–bad. As a rough guide: under 10 reads as the same color, 10 to 20 is hard to tell apart at a glance, 20 and over is clear.

What's on each panel

The inventory's Cond column at 100, 95, 72, 55, 38, 21, 12 and 0 percent, which crosses every color boundary. A Mechanics part list with its colored percentages. An animal's three stat dots, the one place where color is the only cue. Last, a 10-cell amount bar in the accent beside a healthy status bar, to show whether the two can be told apart. Text colors are moved toward light or dark only as far as they need to read at 4.6 : 1, the way ZeD builds its status text shades. Bars and dots keep the plain colors.
