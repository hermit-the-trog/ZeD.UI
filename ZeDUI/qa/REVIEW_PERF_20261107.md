# Outside performance review of the shipping Lua (Nov 7 2026)

Pasted by the director on Nov 7 2026: a read-only review of `42/media/lua` from a cloud checkout. Paths are relative to `42/media/lua/client/ZeD/`. "Bridge call" = a Lua-to-Java call; each `pcall(function() ... end)` also allocates a closure.

**QA spot-check (main at 9f37a8a5):** confirmed in the code: Restock's whole-building walk keyed on the player's square (`ZeD_Restock.lua:265-275`), `MODEL_REFRESH = 30` frames (`InventoryRenderer.lua:1093`), `wrapText` with no memo (`Atoms.lua:1542`), `logic:setRecipes` in the scan (`CraftingCore.lua:335`), `hooksNear` (`AnimalsTab.lua:1892`), the moodles' per-frame `getMoodleLevel` (`ZeD_Moodles.lua:150`). Line numbers in the review are off by 0-4 on today's main. Not measured in game yet (no ms numbers of our own).

**Not for beta 8.** Each of these touches a hot path; any of them needs a full regression after. Proposed for the build after the Nov 8 upload, with `suite_perf.lua` numbers before and after.

## Already fine (the review checked, no action)
AnimalsAlertPoller (`cell:getAnimals()` once a game minute); measure / `ZeD.tr` / `truncateText` / colour memos; TextScale (only `drawTextRight`/`drawTextCentre` measure uncached, 3 sites); Belt; main-menu Mods and ModScan; the OnTick / OnRenderTick / OnPreUIDraw handlers; Craft zone and element tables reused.

## Findings, ranked

1. **Restock walks every square of the building once a second while a loadout is applied (HIGH, conditional).** `ZeD_Restock.lua:265-290` `R.scan` loops x/y/z over the BuildingDef; per square `getGridSquare`, `getBuilding`, `scanSquare` (`:164-188`: `lootAllowed` closure, `R.inReach`, `getObjects`, per object containers and water). `EL.plan` (`ZeD_EquipLoadout.lua:161-199`) recomputes once a second from `foldSummary` / `barParts` / `EL.drawSection`, even with the fold shut; `ZeD_RestockPin.lua:61-80` too. The cache key is the player's square, so any step forces a rescan. A 20x20x2 house: ~800 squares x 10-20 calls, a 10-40 ms hitch each second. Fix: cache the building's containers and taps per `R.bkey(b)` (~10 s, or on OnContainerUpdate / OnObjectAdded / OnObjectAboutToBeRemoved); only the 3x3 reach part follows the square. Or time-slice the walk (as `MS.step`).
2. **`CC.logicScan` hitch every 1.2 s on Craft and Build (HIGH while shown).** `CraftingCore.lua:309-372`: `CC.buildContainers` (closure + `getCategory` + `isEquipped` per item); a new ArrayList of every recipe whenever `win[dKey] ~= data` (in effect every scan, ~541 adds, because of 3); `logic:setRecipes` makes Java recompute every recipe; then a closure per recipe for `getCachedRecipeInfo`. Callers `CraftingTab.lua:541`, `BuildingTab.lua:401`. Fix: key the list on the recipe source; call setContainers / setRecipes only when a cheap signature changes (container count, item counts, capacity weight, bench, perk levels); `pcall(logic.getCachedRecipeInfo, logic, r)`.
3. **Craft and Build rebuild every recipe row every 30 frames (MED-HIGH).** `CraftingTab.lua:215-283` `gatherData`: per recipe `getPerkLevel`, a closure for `isRecipeKnown`, `ZeD.textCase` x2, a new table; `CC.carryStatus` name map (`CraftingCore.lua:378-389`); `BuildingTab.lua:206-243` the same; `CraftingTab.lua:550-557` a linear search for the selection every frame. ~1,000+ bridge calls, 541 closures and tables per refresh. Fix: mutate rows in place (as `JournalTab.gatherRecipeData`, `:420-440`); perk levels once into a map; cache `stationTag` / `boundShort`; an ms interval of 1-2 s; a name index.
4. **Inventory list model rebuilt and re-sorted every 30 frames per open column (MED-HIGH, big containers).** `InventoryRenderer.lua:1097`, `:1261-1265`, `buildItemModel` `:1117-1236`: per item `heldByPlayer` 2 closures, `groupOf`, `catKey`; the comparators (`makeSorter` `:70-121`) call `getName`, `lower`, `catKey`, `getUnequippedWeight` inside every comparison. A 300-item crate: ~2,500 comparisons x 2-4 calls, ~twice a second per column, columns in step. Fix: decorate-sort-undecorate; one equipped/worn set per build; a time-based refresh (~1 s) staggered by column.
5. **World window container refresh every 30 frames, with radios and vehicles (MED).** `ZeD_WorldWindow.lua:2817-2905`: `buildWorldContainers` (`:2544+`) clears and refills `_floorContainer` from 9 squares, per-square `canReachTo` and safehouse, corpses with `getText` + 2 closures; the same tick runs `RadioTab.buildWorldRadios` (`RadioTab.lua:149-208`) and `MechanicsTab.refresh` (every vehicle in the cell, `MechanicsTab.lua:166-202`) even with those tabs shut. Fix: 300-500 ms, rebuild at once on a square change or OnContainerUpdate; skip the refill when per-square object counts match; radios and vehicles only on their tab or every 1-2 s.
6. **Animals ranch zone view: per frame and per row, uncached (MED).** `AnimalsTab.lua:2130-2135` every frame: `getZoneByName` (`:190-201`) walks every zone with a pcall each, then `setSeeDesignationZone`, `resetSelectedZonesForHighlight`, `addSelectedZoneForHighlight`. `drawAnimalRow` (`:1628-1710`) per row: `AD.getConditionTags` (7 checks + tables), `AD.resolvePortrait` (lower, gsub, getters, `getTexture`), `getAgeText`, health/hunger/thirst, 3 `getText`, `tagWidth`, measures: ~300 calls and 50 tables a frame at 10 rows. Fix: a row model per animal on the existing 500 ms `SORT_CACHE_MS`; Java `DesignationZoneAnimal.getZoneByName` (already at `:499`) or a cache; re-highlight only when `state.zoneName` changes.
7. **Animals `hooksNear`: a 41x41 scan every 2 s (MED hitch).** `AnimalsTab.lua:1890-1918`: 1,681 squares, `getObjects` + `instanceof` per object; the sort calls `o:getSquare()` and the player's x, y per comparison. ~5-8k calls in one frame. Fix: distance once per hook before sorting; spread the walk or a widening ring that stops at 4 hooks; or refresh on object add/remove.
8. **`ZeD.Atoms.wrapText` not memoized (MED, broad).** `Atoms.lua:1542-1564`: gmatch, a words table, a new prefix string per word (O(n^2)), each measured. Every frame from `Molecules.drawDetailCard` (`:5020/5030`), `drawNeedsLine` / `drawEmptyLine` (`:4954/4973`), the help balloon (`:4405`), the moodle note, `ZeD_RestockPin:prerender`, `ZeD_Belt:_cardH`; ~128 call sites. Every prefix also lands in the measure cache (`MC_MAX = 3000`, `shared/ZeD/Scale.lua:732`), churning real entries out. Fix: the two-generation memo `truncateText` has (font -> maxW -> text, cleared on `ZeD.Text.measureEpoch()`), returned read-only.
9. **The item card rebuilds its model every frame (MED, while a card shows).** `ItemCard.lua:1513-1522` -> `ItemCardOrganism.lua:1199+` `renderBody` -> `ItemFactsPanel.factsFor(item)` at `:990` and `:1068` (`ItemFactsPanel.lua:395-405`: dozens of `safeGet` closures, format, `ZeD.tr`, tables), plus `headerVerbsForContext`, `readMarks`, `topStatsForItem`. Fix: memoize per item for 250-500 ms; clear on `setItem`, an action start or finish (`ItemActionManager`), a fold toggle.
10. **Always-on HUD, moodles (LOW-MED).** `ZeD_Moodles.lua:532-536` OnPreUIDraw -> `_place` (`:249-320`) -> `M.active` (`:150-165`): ~26 `MoodleType` lookups and 26 pcall'd `getMoodleLevel` + a table per moodle, every frame, per player; bottom mode `M.backing` (`:167-183`) 4 pcalls per moodle for the highlight colours; `_noticeTick` (`:422-458`) a table a frame. Fix: compute on OnTick or every 100-250 ms into a reused list; cache the two colours.
11. **Always-on HUD, the watch (LOW-MED).** `ZeD_Watch.lua:547-650` prerender: `_wornWatch` (`:247-262`) walks worn items through a closure at `:581` and again via `W.beltWatch` (`:276-283`) at `:594`, plus the belt's layout: 2-3 walks a frame; closures for `isAlarmSet` / `getHour`; `_hitsFrom` (`:529-540`) new tables a frame. Fix: cache the worn watch per player (frame or OnClothingUpdated); reuse hit tables.
12. **Frame-count throttles scale with FPS and line up (SYSTEMIC).** Craft / Build / Journal recipes 30; `MODEL_REFRESH` 30; World and Character container timers 30 (`ZeD_WorldWindow.lua:2817`, `ZeD_CharacterWindow.lua:3356`); `Stations.SCAN_EVERY` 30 (`Stations.lua:34`); `ObjectCards` REFRESH 15 / BROWSE_EVERY 60 (`:55-56`); Health, Skills, Equipment, Journal data 15 (`HealthTab.lua:1359`, `SkillsTab.lua:962`, `EquipmentTab.lua:1236/1344`, `JournalTab.lua:597`); Skills training 30 (`:1037`); `CC.gatherDetail` CONTAINER_INTERVAL 90 (`CraftingCore.lua:50`). At 144-240 Hz they run 2.4-4x as often; timers made together fire together. Fix: `getTimestampMs()` deltas with a per-window phase offset. Also: `SkillsTab.gatherTrainingData` (`:402-447`) walks the inventory and ~100+ books even with the training section folded (gate it); `HealthTab.gatherData` (`:446-534`) ~17 parts x ~20 getters per refresh.
13. **`Stations.scan` every 30 frames in some craft views (LOW-MED).** `Stations.lua:194-247`: 81 squares, per object `getMasterObject`, `ST.classify` (up to 4 pcall'd `getComponent`), `CanOpenWindowFor`; while a hand-mode station recipe's detail draws (`CraftingCore.lua:2317` `nearestFor`) or the station dropdown is open; `ST.entryFor` forces one (`:457`). Fix: ~1 s, and on a square change.
14. **The World window rebuilds its focus list every frame, also with mouse and keyboard (LOW-MED).** `ZeD_WorldWindow.lua:4898-4925`: `_buildWorldPanelFocusables` (`:1013`) for Craft, Build, Animals, Maps, Radio, Vehicle, Furniture; Craft walks ~541+ zones and allocates `occRow` / `occSec` (`CraftingCore.lua:3283-3312`). Fix: skip while the input mode is keyboard and mouse; rebuild on `ZeD_InputModeChanged`. Check the Character window for the same.
15. **Small:** `CookingTab.lua:653/664` re-groups and sorts ingredient rows every frame (sort when `ingCache` changes); `ZeD_Pins.stats` (`:124+`) 2 closures per pin per frame from both windows; `FlyerMap.checkVisits` (`:47-70`) walks `PrintMediaDefinitions.MiscDetails` with concatenation each second (precompute rectangles); `Atoms.truncateText` keys on a fractional `maxW`, so drawer and resize animation miss every frame and flood the measure cache (floor `maxW` first).
