# QA script audit: animals, hutches, farming, troughs, butchering, fishing (Nov 12 2026)

Read only. Sources: ZeDUI/tools/qa (scripts, manifests, git log), ZeDUI/qa/regression (every run row that names these
scripts), QA_169_animals_round3_20261112.md, B8_OVERNIGHT_20261108.md, AUDIT_DESIGN_AND_COVERAGE_20261111.md, PUNCH_LIST 167/169.

The QA home every animal and farm script stages at is the Irvington drag strip (925,13040): asphalt and open ground,
no barn, no pasture, no water. zedqa_animals.lua builds a hen house, a trough, a butcher hook and a dead cow there and
leaves them ("more each run"), so the strip has slowly become a cluttered fake farm. That clutter is why a `_far` copy,
a restore script and a far-cleanup script exist. The fishing scripts are the exception: they go to a real shore.

"Runs" are rows in qa/regression reports. WARN means the runner saw the script's own FAIL lines.

## Worst offenders

1. **zedqa_animals_quick + _more + _far (punch list 169).** About 24 in-game runs in 25 hours (each round is three setups,
   about 25 minutes), 1 clean pass, 17 commits across the three files. The first two rounds earned their keep (four real
   bugs). Since then the same failures come back every round and they are the test's: the ram row is scrolled out of
   view, so six shear asserts fall together; the walk-away check teleports a player whose run already ended; and the
   newest repair forces game speed 2/3 every tick to save time, which itself cuts the milking and shearing. The rules
   these scripts keep re-proving (which alert offers what, selection, run order, walk-away, feeding gates) are already
   217 checks in animals_quick_sim.lua, which runs in seconds. Keep one short in-game pass on a real farm at normal
   speed: milk one cow, shear one sheep, feed one hungry animal, take the pictures, check the pad landing. Drop _far,
   the speed hack and everything the sim owns.

2. **The drag strip as a farm.** zedqa_animals.lua builds a hen house, trough, hook and dead cow at the QA home and
   never takes them away, so each run adds more. That litter made the `_far` copy (whose first try landed animals in a
   stadium stand), zedqa_animals_restore and zedqa_animals_cleanup_far necessary. Pick one real farm on the map (barn,
   fenced pasture, a hen house, tilled ground; water for the trough) as a second QA home for every animal, hutch,
   trough, farm-plot and gallery-farm script, and let those scripts use what the farm already has. Then the restore
   and cleanup scripts go.

3. **zedqa_cov_objcards farm cards.** Brand new, three repairs in two hours, not yet through a complete run. It plows
   plots and stands a hen house on asphalt, and the hutch steps race the game's own clock (a hen sat on an egg 7 to 12
   seconds before the boxes are read; hens rounded up after they wander out in daylight). Restage on the farm, drop the
   timed hen steps, and split the farm cards out of the seven-card run.

4. **Fishing is mostly fine.** The live scripts go to a real shore and found real bugs that were fixed the same day.
   The one odd one is zedqa_fishing_tab, which tests the Fishing tab where there is no water by faking a bobber; fold
   its pictures into the shore run.

5. **Gaps, not waste.** Nothing tests butchering itself (hanging a carcass on the hook, the Butcher page's actions)
   or the full-bucket stop in game (QA_169: only the sim checks it). If they matter, they belong in the farm pass
   above, not in new scripts.
