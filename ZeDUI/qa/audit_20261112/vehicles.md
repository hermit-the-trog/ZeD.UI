# QA script audit: vehicles, dashes, car radios, mechanics, trailers, drag-strip staging (Nov 12 2026)

Read only. Sources: ZeDUI/tools/qa/zedqa_*.lua headers and staging code, the manifests (regression.txt and the
others), every results table in ZeDUI/qa/regression/*.md (about 1,280 reports), ZeDUI/qa/QA_177, the Nov 11 coverage
audit (AUDIT_DESIGN_AND_COVERAGE_20261111.md) and git history per script ("commits" = every commit that touched the
file, including the one that created it).

How to read the results column: the runner marks a script PASS when it reaches DONE with no ERR line. A script
that only takes screenshots has nothing that can fail, so it always "passes". The coverage audit already labels
these SHOT ONLY. Their pass counts below show that they ran, not that anything was checked.

Where things are staged: the prelude's ZEDQA_HOME moves the character to the Irvington Speedway drag strip
(925,13040, open asphalt, daylight). For cars this is the right kind of place. The trouble is not the place but
the spawn code. Ten scripts each carry their own copy of the same "find a free square, spawn, wait for the
physics, seat the driver" block. It checks one tile (isFree, no tree), not the whole footprint of the car. So every
timing fix (Oct 22, Oct 29, Oct 30, Nov 2, Nov 8) had to be made again in each copy.

In the beta 6 final run (Nov 6, 73 min) the vehicle block took about 16 min (22 % of the run). Since today,
cov_vehicles adds up to 10 more minutes.

## The worst offenders

1. **The 19-car dash tours, run four times over.** dash19, dashpx, dash_keys_edge and dash_brands each spawn and
   seat the same cars one by one. dashnight, dash_green, tour3 and r6_keys add more. A full regression seats about
   80 cars, and every WARN in this block (30-odd runs) came from that staging: "car not ready", "fell through the
   ground", "never reached 64 km/h", "key went nil", a key left in the ignition. **None of these WARNs was a ZeD
   bug.** dash_keys_edge alone has been repaired 8 times. The fix: one 19-car pass (spawn once, then per car record
   the key states, brand, rect and one picture). The logic itself (key sequence, badge choice, night plate, plain
   speed, fare, needle angle) goes into dash_odo_sim, which already loads the real ZeD_Dashboard.lua on stubs.
2. **The "drive" checks that push a car with no keys** (dash_green's fire pickup, r6_parts' taxi). They fail
   on physics, not on ZeD. Cut them, or keep one real drive in dash_odo where distance is the thing being tested.
3. **Shot-only scripts in the regression list** (car_radio_build, car_radio_cd, dash_props_smoke,
   seat_lightbar_vanilla, tm, tour3, dash19). About 2.5 min per run, and they can never fail. cov_vehicles now does
   real checks for the same surfaces. Once it completes one green run, take them out.
4. **cov_vehicles itself**: 1,582 lines, repaired twice on its first day, and it has never finished a regression
   run. Its rod, clock and sleep-clock sections have nothing to do with vehicles and draw on a recording canvas;
   move them to a sim. Its one known FAIL (CH down on channel 01) is a real bug, already shown by
   coverage_bugs_sim.lua. Fixing chStep in the mod is cheaper than running the game to see it again.
5. **Copy-pasted staging.** Ten scripts carry their own spawn-and-seat block with a one-tile free check. One
   shared helper in the prelude (beside ZEDQA_HOME) that spawns on a known clear stretch of the strip would stop the
   fix-it-ten-times loop. Old scripts that skip HOME (mech, mech2, tour, seats_build, trailer, compat_vehicles,
   car_radio_live/vanilla) still look for the save's old cars in the woods. They are the "car in the forest" cases;
   all of them are DROP above.

Real ZeD bugs this area has found: mech79 (step-before card on a brake, Nov 3, fixed), the Summer Car engine
window through the Vehicle tab (today, open) and the radio chStep wrap (today, open). The other failures on record
came from the tests themselves.
