## Fault for Build: opening the survival guide freezes the game

- `ZeD.Guide.open(0)` alone (b10_guide_open_repro.lua, manifest b10_gopen_manifest.txt) at 4K 1.75x: "open returned" prints, then no frame is drawn again. Reproduced three times (twice from the bubble's click, once direct).
- jstack of the game: MainThread is inside a Kahlua loop under `UIElement.render` (pcall'd Lua), about 2 cores busy, "Responding". An infinite loop in a render.
- New since beta 9 (beta 9's guide checks opened it). Reported to Build; the QA lead says Build is on it (wrap between letters). gbub steps 4 to 8 and any tooltip step inside the guide are held until Build sends a hash.
- gbub steps passed before the freeze: 1 (clock armed, guide not open at 20 s, no bubble before 30 s), 2 (bubble at about 30 s, exact wording with F1, long bare balloon, line unread in Messages, flag written, guide still closed), 3 (the balloon has a click).
- My script change: zedqa_guide_bubble.lua got an OnRenderTick unpause (the guide pauses the game and OnTick stops). It did not matter for this freeze and is harmless; keep or drop.
