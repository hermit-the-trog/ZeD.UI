## New since test 4

### 135. The belt's held-item card
At 1x and 1.75x:
- Hover the main hand with an item held, the off hand, and a belt slot with an item: ZeD's card stands above the box's
  hint at once (the item's name, condition, weight and its own figures), not the game's box.
- Hold Alt over one of them: the game's box there instead; let go: the card back.
- With a tooltip mod on (one that adds lines to the game's item box), its lines show under Alt.
- A right-click menu open on the belt: no card. Dragging an item off the belt: no card.

## Fixed after test 4

### 133. The strip's arrows, by hand
Test 4: a click on the right arrow opened "Move to"'s submenu, the left one equipped the hammer (a cell hidden under
each arrow took the click). Now the arrows win.
- A menu with 15 or more options, at 1x and 1.75x: real clicks on the right arrow 3 times, then the left 3 times. Each
  click pages (the next 10, none skipped or repeated), never confirms an option or closes the strip; you land back on
  the first page.
- Resting the pointer on an arrow doesn't change the selected cell.

### 126. The list as Health closes (1280 x 800)
Test 4: the list stayed put through the fold and while Health opened, but moved 53 px as Health closed, in two frames.
That move is the window going home; it now eases back with Health's slide.
- Equip's full card open, the pointer on the Character list; let the drawer fold, open Health, close it: as Health
  closes the list slides back with it (no jump) and ends where it was before the card opened; the inventory 279.
