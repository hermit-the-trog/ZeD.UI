# Simple versions: rules and list (for the director, Nov 2 2026)

You said: "the beta release is gonna have our vision and then a more simplified vision." Here are the rules every
simple version follows, then every surface ZeD draws as a prop or a graphic face, grouped by what I'd build for the
beta. Nothing in it is built yet.

## The rules

1. **A simple version does everything its full version does, and shows everything it shows.** Only the art goes. No
   button or reading is lost. A player who switches mid-game finds the same things in the same order.
2. **It is built from ZeD's own plain parts:** headings, labels with values, the segmented bars, buttons, steppers and
   on/off switches. It looks like the rest of ZeD's cards and windows, in every theme and accent, and never like the
   game's own screens.
3. **One layout for every device card.**
   - A readout block across the top shows the one or two values that matter most in large text: the frequency, the
     time left or the heat.
   - Rows of label, value and control follow, in the same order as the controls on the face.
   - The footer band holds the actions, as on every ZeD card.
4. **The controller moves through it in the same order as the full version:** top to bottom, then the footer. A, B, X
   and Y do the same jobs.
5. **The switches live in ZeD setup, in one "Look" group.** Each family has one row, Full or Simple, as the belt and
   the action bar have now. The four that exist move in beside the new ones. The Vanilla Access preset picks Simple
   everywhere, and Vanilla Plus keeps Full.
6. **Full stays the default.** The simple versions are there for players who want them, never pushed.

| Surface | Today | What the simple card shows |
