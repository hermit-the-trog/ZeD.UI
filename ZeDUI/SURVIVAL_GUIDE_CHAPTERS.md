# Survival guide: the five new chapters (draft for the director, Nov 4 2026)

Build's first draft, for you to rewrite. Change anything: words, order, what is in or out. When you are done, the
text is built into both looks (the book and the window) and translated.

How to read this draft:
- **Keys and buttons** are written like this: **E** / **A**. In the game each one is drawn as your own key (from
  your key bindings) or your own controller's button (Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo or Steam Deck, from the controller
  you hold). Switch devices and the page changes with you. The keys in this draft are stand-ins: in the game every
  one is read from the game's bindings and ZeD's controller map, and QA checks each line against the game.
- **"Two ways" boxes** close every page that teaches an action: what to do with a mouse and keyboard on the left, with
  a controller on the right, one short line each.
- **{Rules: ...}** marks a number the page shows from the game's own files or your world's settings, never typed in
  by us, so it is right for your version and your sandbox. A number some players would rather find out for themselves
  sits behind a **spoiler bar** ("Show the numbers").
- Chapters 16 to 20 follow the game's 15.

## Chapter 16: ZeD

*The screens of this computer, and how to work them.*

### Two windows

Everything you carry and everything around you sits in two windows. The **Character window** is you and your
things: what you carry, what you wear, your health, your skills and your journal. The **World window** is the world
and its things: the containers near you, the floor, crafting, building, your car and your maps. They open and close
together.

Two ways:
| Mouse and keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|
| **Tab** opens both windows; **I** too | **Y** opens or hides both |
| Click a window to work in it | **LB** goes to Character, **RB** to World |
| **Esc** or the close box closes them | Hold **Y** to close both |

### Moving things

Each open container is a column, side by side: your bag, the shelf, the floor. To move an item, drag it to another
column. Pick several first to move them together. The band at the foot of the column says what will move and where.

Two ways:
| Mouse and keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|
| Drag an item to another column | **A** on an item, then **Move** in its card |
| **Ctrl**-click picks several, **Shift**-click a run | Hold **A** marks a row; **Up** or **Down** while held marks more |
| Double-click uses an item | Hold **X** uses an item |

### Item cards

Click an item and its card opens beside the list: what it is, its condition, its weight and what you can do with
it. Food shows a nutrition label. Cards also open for things in the world: generators, campfires, farm plots,
troughs, padlocks and water. The card closes by itself when an action finishes.

Two ways:
| Mouse and keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|
| Click an item to open its card | **A** on an item opens its card |
| Click a button in the card | **Up** goes to the card's buttons |
| Click the item again to close it | **B** closes the card |

### The tabs

The tabs along the top of each window open its pages. Character: Inventory, Equipment, Health, Thermal, Skills and
Journal. World: the containers, Crafting, Building, Foraging, Furniture, Vehicle and Maps. A tab only shows when
there is something on it.

Two ways:
| Mouse and keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|
| Click a tab | **D-pad** to the tab, **A** to open it |

### The belt and your hands

The belt runs along the foot of the screen. Your two hands sit at its left end, each with a ring that shows the
condition of what it holds. The belt's slots hold what you can grab without opening a bag. A gun in your main hand
adds a tab for its rounds.

Two ways:
| Mouse and keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|
| Number keys **1** to **0** take from a slot | **D-pad** wheel: Character, then the belt |
| Drag an item onto a slot to keep it there | **X** on an item, then **Belt** |

### Menus and the action bar

Right-click something to see what you can do with it. The menu can be a strip of buttons or a list: choose in
Options, ZeD setup. While your character works, the action bar over their head shows what they are doing and how
long it has left, in words (Verbose) or ten segments (Simple).

Two ways:
| Mouse and keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|
| Right-click for the menu | **X** for the menu |
| **Esc** or a click away closes it | **B** closes it |

### Messages

Notes from the game and from other mods go into the Messages window instead of covering the screen, so a note you
missed can be read later. Questions that need an answer still ask at once.

### Your look

Every ZeD window has a window menu at its top left. It sets the theme (Dark, Light or High contrast), the accent
colour, the text size, how see-through windows are, and the controller highlight. A window can keep its own accent.

### ZeD setup and Full or Simple

Options opens on ZeD setup. It holds every ZeD setting, with a Find box and a help line that explains the setting
you point at. Many parts of ZeD come in two forms: **Full**, drawn as the thing itself (the watch on your wrist, the
car's dashboard, the radio's face, this book), and **Simple**, the same controls as a plain ZeD panel. Choose each in
the Full or simple group.

### On a controller

Every mouse action has a button. **A** is a left click, **X** a right click and **B** backs out. With no window
open, hold a **D-pad** direction for a wheel: Left for Character, Right for World, Up for Social, Down for Here and
now. Point with the right stick and let go to take. Hold **LB** or **RB** and press another button for one of twelve
quick actions you set in ZeD setup.

## Chapter 17: First Days

*What keeps a new survivor alive through the first week.*

### The first hour

Crouch, close the curtains and look around before you do anything else. Zombies see you through windows and hear
you through walls. A quiet house you have checked room by room is the safest place you will have for a while.

Two ways:
| Mouse and keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|
| **C** crouches and sneaks | Click **L3** (the left stick) to crouch |
| Right-click a window, **Close curtains** | **X** on the window, **Close curtains** |

### Water before it stops

The water will stop running and the power will go out, and nobody will tell you the day. Fill every bottle, pot and
bucket you find while the taps still run. Later you will collect rain.

{Rules: the water and power shutoff windows from your world's settings, behind a spoiler bar}

### Walk, don't run

Running tires you fast, and a tired survivor fights badly. Build 42 adds muscle strain: swinging, carrying and
climbing wear on your arms and legs until you rest. Walk between houses and save running for when you need it.

Two ways:
| Mouse and keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|
| Hold **Shift** to run, **Alt** to sprint | Hold **LB** to run, push the left stick fully to sprint |

### Fight at a door, never in the open

One zombie at a time is a fight. Three at once is a death. Make them come to you through a door, a window or a gap
in a fence, and push them over before you swing. If more come than you can take, leave.

Two ways:
| Mouse and keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|
| Right mouse held aims, left mouse swings | **LT** aims, **RT** swings |
| **Space** pushes | **RT** with no weapon ready pushes |

### A weapon and a bag

Your first two finds should be something to hit with and something to carry in. Put the weapon in your main hand and
the bag on your back. A two-handed weapon takes both hands.

### Keep it quiet

Guns, car alarms and house alarms bring every zombie in earshot. Smashing a car window can set off its alarm. If you
fire a gun or trip an alarm in town, leave at once.

### Two infections

Your health panel can say a wound is **Infected**. That is an ordinary infection from dirt, and a clean bandage and
disinfectant cure it. The zombie infection is different, and the game never tells you that you have it. Any wound a
zombie gives you can carry it: a bite always, a laceration or a scratch sometimes. Clean and bandage every wound, and
watch for the signs.

{Rules: the zombie infection's odds by wound and how long it takes, from your world's settings, behind a spoiler bar}

Two ways:
| Mouse and keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|
| Character window, Health tab | **LB**, then the Health tab |
| Click a wound, then **Disinfect** or **Bandage** | **A** on the wound, then the action |

### Food that keeps

Fresh food goes stale and then rotten, and rotten food makes you sick. Eat the fresh things first and keep the cans
for later. A fridge slows it down until the power goes.

{Rules: how long food keeps, from the game's item files}

### Learning

Skill books make you learn faster at what they teach, but only after you have read them and only at their levels.
The TV teaches too, at set hours. Read in daylight or by a lamp.

{Rules: Skill Books (already built)}

### Where you are

The map you start with shows only what you have seen. Maps you find fill it in. World window, Maps tab.

## Chapter 18: Coming from Build 41

*What changed, for survivors who learned the old ways.*

Build checks each of these against the game before it ships. Anything the game files do not confirm comes out.

### Traits and occupations

Several traits and point costs changed, and some are gone. Lucky and Unlucky no longer exist. Do not copy an old
build: read the costs on the character screen.

### Crafting and building were rebuilt

Crafting now happens at workstations, and many recipes must be learned first, from magazines or from your
occupation. The World window's Crafting and Building tabs list what you know and what you need.

### Carpentry

Taking furniture apart no longer teaches Carpentry. Build things to learn it.

### Weapons wear two ways

A weapon's head and its handle wear separately, and an edged weapon goes dull. Look after the one you rely on.

### Muscle strain

Swinging, carrying and climbing strain your arms and legs. Strained limbs hurt and work badly until you rest.

### New skills

Carving, Knapping, Pottery, Glassmaking, Masonry, Blacksmithing and Animal Care are new. Each has its own skill books.

{Rules: the skill list read from the game, so the names are right for your version}

### A bigger world

The map is about twice the size, with new towns (Brandenburg, Ekron and Irvington), basements and more buildings.
Nights are darker.

### Foraging finds materials

Foraging now turns up clay, flint and stone for the new crafts, as well as food.

### Animals

Farms keep animals now: chickens, cows, sheep, pigs and more. They need food, water and care.

## Chapter 19: Your Controls

*Every key and button, read from your own settings.*

This chapter is made from your key bindings and ZeD's controller map each time you open it, so it is always right
for you. One page per group, each a "Two ways" table:

- Moving: walk, run, sprint, crouch, climb, turn.
- Fighting: aim, swing, push, shove, stomp, reload, switch weapons.
- Using things: interact, open, pick up, the menu, the hands, the belt slots.
- Windows: the two windows, the tabs, the item card, Find, close.
- Driving: get in and out, start, gears, lights, horn, radio, the seat chooser, the dashboard.
- Talking and the map: chat, shout, the map, zoom.
- ZeD's controller extras: the four wheels, the ZeD wheel, the twelve quick actions as you set them.

A key you have changed shows your key, not the default. A controller shows your controller's buttons.

## Chapter 20: What's New in ZeD

*The changes in each version, newest first.*

Read from ZeD's own change notes, so it is always up to date. The first page shows this version's changes; older
versions follow. For the next beta it would open on:

- The survival guide: this book, or the plain window (Full or Simple in ZeD setup), with five new chapters, the
  game's rules as numbers and your own keys and buttons on every page.
- The Mods screen: mods that are on sit on a dark band and mods that are off dim; an Off tab; Hide incompatible; the
  tab you left is kept; a Simple view; colour labels for folders and separators.
- Overhead notes ("Too heavy", "+1 Carpentry") in ZeD's bubble, with every note kept in Messages.
- In-world hints for what is in front of you, on keyboard and controller (Always, First hours or Off).

## Rules pages (the encyclopedia), for your look

Each sits beside its chapter, in the book's grey-bar style, like Skill Books. Every number is read from the installed
game or your world's settings. If the game does not let ZeD read a number, it is left out rather than guessed.

- **Your body:** carry weight by Strength; endurance and muscle strain; tiredness, hunger and thirst over a day;
  temperature and clothing; panic.
- **Food and health:** calories and weight; how long food keeps fresh and stale; food sickness; each wound type and
  its healing time; the zombie infection's odds and timing (spoiler bar).
- **Skills and traits:** the XP each level takes; what each level gives; trait costs and effects; book, TV and
  occupation multipliers.
- **Things you run:** generators and their fuel; vehicles (fuel, battery, engine, keys and hotwiring); farming
  seasons and growth; the water and power shutoff (spoiler bar).

**The spoiler bar:** a grey bar that reads "Show the numbers". Click it (or **A**) and the numbers under it show for
as long as the page is open. ZeD setup could also have "Show spoilers: Ask (the default), Always, Never". Your
world's secrets (whether you are infected, the shutoff day, the helicopter's day, where loot is) are never shown,
spoiler bar or not.
