Seats and Lightbar

Two vanilla windows still sit behind the vehicle wheel. Switch Seat (also Enter Vehicle from outside, and the seat key) opens a black panel with the car drawn from above and a box on every seat; Lightbar opens a "Light controller" window with four siren buttons and a lights dial. The seat chooser becomes a small ZeD window on vanilla's own car art and seat places; the lightbar becomes the dash's own siren controller, brought up close above the dash so the car and its lights stay in view. Backdrops are real 1080p captures from the new QA save; Look: Vanilla shows the game's own windows, captured in the same places.

Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDThe seat chooser is a small ZeD window where vanilla's was (the middle of the screen, the same size), on vanilla's own car outline tinted to the text colour and vanilla's own seat places, so every car, van and a mod's vehicle lines up exactly as it does today.
  2. RECOMMENDSeats are the context strip's cells. An empty seat shows its number (the key that takes it); you are the seated figure in the accent; someone else is the seated figure in ink; items on a seat are a box. A seat you can't take from here (no seat fitted, items with nowhere to go, the back of a caged car) is stippled, like an off slice on the wheel. The pointed seat grows and turns the accent, as on the wheel.
  3. RECOMMENDThe band names the pointed seat and says why not. Vanilla prints the seat's name over the car and the reason in red text. Here the band under the title holds both: the name, and the reason with a status dot (red for "Must exit vehicle.", amber for "Item(s) on seat will be moved to another seat."). With nothing pointed it names your own seat. Vanilla's words are kept, so every language still works.
  4. RECOMMENDDoors are small cells beside the car where vanilla draws its faint exit arrows: one per door you could leave by. Pointing one says "Exit Vehicle" and which door. Holding Shift lights them and numbers them (Shift + number gets out there, as in vanilla); the footer says so, because vanilla never did.
  5. RECOMMENDThe lightbar is the siren controller already drawn on the police dash, up close. The same faceplate, the same two rotary switches (lights OFF 1 2 3; siren STBY YELP WAIL ALARM, vanilla's four modes), the same two pilot lamps, made larger as its own 1993 prop. It rises above the dash instead of covering the car, so you can watch the bar flash while you set it. Click a word to turn the switch there, or click the knob to step it; the wheel turns it too. A controller moves between the two switches with the D-pad and turns them with up and down; B puts it away. Any lightbar vehicle gets it, including the ones whose dash has no controller drawn (the lights pickups and vans).
  6. YOUR CALLOr a plain ZeD window (scene "Lightbar: plain window"): two rows of buttons in a small window where vanilla's sat. Clear, but it is software for a piece of hardware the dash already shows. A third way: no new surface at all, the wheel's slice just points you at the dash's own small switches (fiddly at 1080p).
  7. Kept from vanilla: the seat key and the wheel slice toggle it; clicking outside or walking more than six tiles away closes it; seat switching, moving items off a seat, the ambulance's front seats (get out and in) and every sound are vanilla's own calls. The lightbar sends vanilla's own commands, so it works in multiplayer the same way.
