Equip doll v4, loadouts and outfits, on today's window

Drawn on the window as the game draws it now. The doll: the figure stands straight on the panel with no frame, Arms sits above Belt, and the small slots fill the room either side. Loadouts and outfits are lists kept in the Journal (a new Loadouts page beside Journal, Notebooks, Papers and Recipes): a loadout is what you carry, gear and supplies, each line fixed or per day; an outfit is what you wear. Lists are built from what you carry or wear in one press, from the right-click menu (Save to loadout and Save to outfit list the lists by name), or from a new button on the item card's mark strip. The Equip tab applies them: pick an outfit, a loadout and the days, see what's missing on the doll, restock in one of three ways (hardcore, casual, accessible), and unpack when you're home.

Loadouts and outfits (director, Oct 29 2026: "Loadout lists should live in the journal tab. They can be applied/stocked in the equipment tab. Right click menu adds a save to loadout/outfit and menus populate based on the names of saved loadouts and outfits. item card also has a button on the mark for strip to add remove from loadout/outfit", "You can also save current inventory/equipped as a loadout/outfit")

  1. RECOMMENDTwo kinds of list. A loadout is what you carry: gear (the bag, the axe, the crowbar) and supplies (water, food, medicine, ammunition, light). An outfit is what you wear. You apply one of each, mixed freely (director, earlier: "Separate, mix freely").
  2. RECOMMENDThey live in the Journal, on a new Loadouts page beside Journal, Notebooks, Papers and Recipes, in the Recipes page's own pattern (a filter, a search, sections with counts). The lists on the left, the chosen list's lines on the right.
  3. RECOMMENDEach line is a recipe: "any knife above half condition", "a water bottle, full", "food, the soonest to spoil", or pinned to "this one" when you mean that exact item. A loadout's supply line is fixed or a day; the days you pick in the Equip tab multiply the per-day lines.
  4. RECOMMENDThree ways to build a list: save what you carry as a loadout or what you wear as an outfit in one press (in the Journal page and from the Equip tab's Save button); the right-click menu, where Save to loadout and Save to outfit open on the lists by name, a check on the ones it's already in, and New at the bottom; and the item card's mark strip, a fourth button beside Favorite, No crafting and Dispose that opens the same lists with checkboxes. Outfits only offer wearable things.
  5. RECOMMENDThe Equip tab applies them. The bar under Equipped: the outfit, the loadout, the days, how ready you are, Restock with its time, and Save. Where the outfit or loadout wants something you aren't wearing or carrying, the doll shows its picture faintly in a dashed outline. "Ready 4 of 9" opens the to-do in the drawer, the item card's place.
  6. RECOMMENDDays (director: "Days number only"). Past what you can carry, it says by how much, what's heaviest, and the most days that fit.
  7. RECOMMENDRestock runs the game's own actions at their own speed, in one of three ways chosen in ZeD's settings, casual by default: hardcore, only the containers in reach; casual, the list pinned, taking from each container you pass; accessible, it walks the building for you. Only the building you're in, only containers you could open yourself; it asks before taking the last of anything, and stops for any key or button, a zombie in view, or getting hurt. Smart picks: what spoils first, the best condition, the fullest bottle.
  8. RECOMMENDUnpack at home: loot goes where its kind already lives, leftover supplies stay packed for the next run unless they'd spoil, the kit and the outfit stay on you. A thing with no home yet asks once.
  9. RECOMMENDOn a controller: the D-pad wheel gets a Loadouts ring; in the to-do, A restocks and B goes back.
  10. Not in v1: lists shared between players; packing a car's trunk.
