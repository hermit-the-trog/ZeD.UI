Hutch, feeding trough, butcher hook and tracks

Five vanilla windows still float over the game when you keep animals: the hutch's two tabs of nest boxes and perches, a read-only trough panel, the butcher hook's workstation, a tiny tracks readout and a debug genome editor. Here the first four become cards in the World window's card drawer, like the generator and fluid cards, and the Animals tab links to them: a zone lists its hutches and troughs, and the Butcher view lists your hooks. The genome editor stays where vanilla keeps it, behind animal cheats, as a debug section of the animal view. Switch the scenes above.

Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDCards in the card drawer. The hutch, the trough, the butcher hook and an inspected track each open a card in the World window, over whatever tab is up, with a leash like the other cards. Every vanilla way in lands on the card: the world menu, the interact key (Shift + interact on a hutch, interact on a hook), Inspect Track from search mode or the inventory. The vanilla windows never show.
  2. RECOMMENDThe hutch is one card, not two tabs. The doors as two lamps; the four EGG LAYING BOXES with their eggs (vanilla's ten egg slots) or the hen sitting in them; COOP ROOSTING with every bird (GRAB, INFO; a dead one is grabbed as a corpse); the FLOOR's dirtiness with CLEAN and what cleaning takes. Vanilla's rules stay: no eggs while a hen sits on them, the boxes are out of reach with both doors shut, the perches with the main door shut. Added: GRAB EGGS for every box at once (vanilla's per-box grab, queued), PUT IN when you carry a bird (vanilla has it only in the world menu), and a line when every box is full, because the hens then lay outside.
  3. RECOMMENDThe trough says food or water. Vanilla's panel is a few numbers and three lines of advice. The card shows what is in it (the feed by kind, or the water in a gauge with its colour and Tainted), which zone it feeds and how many animals use it, and a warning when it sits outside a zone, where animals cannot use it. ADD FOOD moves feed from your bags into the trough, as the loot window does; ADD WATER opens the fluid card aimed at the trough; EMPTY pours the water out (footer confirm). The rule that a trough holds one or the other uses vanilla's own unused words as the NEEDS line.
  4. RECOMMENDThe butcher hook is a work card. Empty: the dead animals within reach or in your bags, HANG on the ones the hook takes and vanilla's reason on the ones it does not. Loaded: vanilla's 3D carcass stays (it changes as parts come off) beside a checklist of LEATHER, BLOOD, HEAD and MEAT with GATHER each; BLOOD gathers into a container you pick or BLEEDs onto the ground; the knife is the NEEDS line; a progress band with STOP while you work; REMOVE CORPSE. One line warns that taking the head or the meat first spills the blood still in it, since vanilla does that silently.
  5. RECOMMENDTracks get a small card. What it is, the species (or only its size below Tracking 5, as vanilla), how fresh it is, and the direction on a compass. Vanilla's window has no controller support and never closes by distance; the card does both.
  6. RECOMMENDThe Animals tab links in. A zone lists its HUTCHES and FEEDING TROUGHS with OPEN, and the zone's FOOD and WATER bars become real (today they always read 0). The "In hutch" tag on an animal opens its hutch. The Butcher view gains HOOKS, as the approved v2 animals mockup drew them, and its roster rows open the animal (today that click does nothing).
  7. YOUR CALLGenome stays a debug tool. Vanilla offers Modify Genome only with animal cheats on (an admin power or the debug menu); players never see genes. Kept that way: a GENOME section at the end of the animal view, shown only with animal cheats on, with each gene's two alleles (which one is used, dominant, any disorder) and EDIT, SWITCH and DISORDER. Players keep what they have now: disorders revealed by Animal Care level.
  8. On a controller. Every card works from the pad (A on a box, a bird, a part or a list row; B closes the card). Vanilla's trough and tracks windows have no controller support at all.
