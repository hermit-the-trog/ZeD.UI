Quick actions on the bumpers, and ZeD's own button pictures

In the world, hold LB or RB and press A, B, X, Y, L3 or R3: twelve quick actions, each one any action from the four D-pad wheels. A slice that opens a list (Eat, Drink, Smoke) does its best pick at once on a chord. You set them on the ZeD setup page, or from a wheel: point at a slice, hold a bumper and press the button. The same page has the switch for putting things back after an action (off for a new player). Every button is drawn as ZeD's own pixel picture, in the pad's own colours, for the controller you play with.

1. The button pictures
One set per controller. ZeD already has a setting for this, ZeD setup, Controls, "Gamepad glyph display" (Auto, Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo, Keyboard/mouse, Off): it was saved but never drawn. These pictures are what it will show. Auto follows the game's controller button style, which the game switches to PlayStation by itself when it sees one; Steam Deck is added to the choices. Face buttons in the pad's colours (Steam Deck and Nintendo pads have none); the shape or letter alone still tells them apart. Nintendo pictures name the button in that place on the pad: B at the bottom. Each picture at 1080p (left) and three times that (right).
2. ZeD setup: Quick actions and Put things back
Two new sections at the end of the ZeD setup page. Each chord is a row: A opens the list of the wheels' actions, X clears it. The band at the bottom says what the focused chord does right now. The defaults are a proposal: change any of them, or tell me your old Steam layout and I'll start from that.
3. Assigning from a wheel
Point at a slice (click a cell), then press a chord below as if you held the bumper and pressed the button: the slice is assigned and the bubble says so; a chord that held something else says what it replaced. A slice that has a chord shows it in its bubble.

What happens in play
  • LB + X with a flashlight in your bag: the game takes it out, puts it in a free hand and turns it on (its own timed actions, the same time as by hand). Press it again: off.
  • LB + Y (Smoke): the first cigarette you carry and a flame to light it; the game finds the lighter. With Put things back on, the lighter and the pack go back to the bag they came from.
  • A chord that can't run does nothing and your character says why over their head, with the wheel's reason: "Nothing to drink on you.", "In a vehicle."
  • The button pressed with a bumper does only the chord: A doesn't interact, Y doesn't open the windows. A bumper alone still does nothing in the world, and LB + RB is still zoom. No chords while a window has the pad or the game is paused.
  • Put things back (off for a new player): after changing a tire, the jack and the wrench go back into the trunk and your weapon back into your hands. Not after an interruption: you never stand there tidying while a zombie closes in.
