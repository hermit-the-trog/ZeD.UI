Pin what you are watching to the Character window's title bar

Right-click a skill, a book, a bottle, a recipe or your car and choose Pin to title bar (X on a controller, then Pin). The pin sits in the title bar beside your health and weight, so you can watch it without opening a tab. Every pin is a number the game already shows you. A pinned row carries a small pin mark. Three pins at most; a fourth replaces the oldest, and the help line says which.
