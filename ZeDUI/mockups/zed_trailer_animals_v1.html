Animals in trailers

Vanilla's "Check Animal(s) in Trailer" is a separate window of 3D figures with nothing but their names, a bare "Space Available" number and an Add Animal button that opens a pop-up. Hunger, thirst, each animal's share of the space, the food and egg containers and the trunk lid all live elsewhere or nowhere. Here the trailer's page in the Vehicle tab carries it all: the space as a floor bar shared by the animals, the lid and the two containers in the summary, every animal as a row with its needs, a card per animal (the 3D figure, WATER and FEED from your bags, REMOVE, GRAB, INFO), and what you can load from where you stand. Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDThe trailer's own page in the Vehicle tab. A trailer is already one of the vehicles you browse there; an animal trailer's page puts its animals first, above the parts (the mechanics inspection stays as it is, with INSPECT in the footer). Vanilla's "Check Animal(s) in Trailer" (world menu and the vehicle wheel) opens the Vehicle tab on that trailer instead of its window, without the walk when you are already within reach.
  2. RECOMMENDSPACE is a floor bar. Vanilla shows a bare number. The bar is the trailer's 500, shared out: each animal's segment (an adult cow takes 400, a sheep 100, a hen 30), what is left, and the overflow hatched red when a birth inside fills it past the limit (vanilla lets that happen).
  3. RECOMMENDThe summary holds the trailer. The trunk lid (OPEN / CLOSE: vanilla shuts every loading option behind it), the Food Container the animals eat from (fill and what is in it, OPEN shows it in the Loot pane), the Egg Container where hens lay, and how many animals are thirsty, since the trailer has no water and they must be watered by hand.
  4. RECOMMENDA list instead of the 3D grid. Each animal is a row: portrait, name, the Animals tab's three dots (health, hunger, thirst), species, sex and age, tags (DEAD, HUNGRY, THIRSTY, STRESSED), and its share of the space. Vanilla's 3D figure moves to the animal's card.
  5. RECOMMENDAn animal's card. The Vehicle tab's card: back, name, REMOVE, GRAB and INFO in the header (INFO opens the animal in the Animals tab); vanilla's 3D figure beside its vitals and its share of the space; GIVE WATER and FEED as lists of what you carry, shown when vanilla offers them (thirst or hunger past 10%); NEEDS in vanilla's words: the shut lid, "Animal is too heavy to be picked up." for GRAB. A dead animal's card offers REMOVE (the carcass drops at the back) and GRAB (the carcass in your hands).
  6. RECOMMENDLoading is a list, not a pop-up. LOAD FROM HERE lists what vanilla's Add Animal offers: what you hold, the animals and carcasses near the back, each with its size, distance and either PUT IN or vanilla's reason ("Too far from back of the trailer.", "Not enough room in the trailer."). An animal on your leash reaches from 5 tiles instead of 3.
  7. RECOMMENDThe car and its trailer point at each other. A car towing a trailer shows TOWING with the trailer's name and how many animals ride in it; the trailer shows TOWED BY. One press switches pages.
  8. RECOMMENDTwo vanilla bugs fixed on the way. The world menu's "Remove Animal from Trailer" does nothing in vanilla (it never passes the animal); it removes the one you pick. "Put Animal in Trailer" disappears in vanilla when a carcass in your hands is the only thing to load; it stays.
  9. On a controller. The rows are the focus path (A opens an animal, A on PUT IN loads), the card's verbs and lists take A, B goes back. Vanilla's grid of four per row and its pop-ups go.
