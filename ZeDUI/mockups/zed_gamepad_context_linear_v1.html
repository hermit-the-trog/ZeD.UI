Dense icon strip

Cells are icon-only, 52px square. Fits 10+ actions in ~530px. No text crammed into tiny tiles — the icon IS the tile. Truncation at both edges is pixel-hard, no fade.

Players scan by glyph, not by reading. Muscle memory kicks in fast because cell shapes are identical — position alone differentiates.

Label above, info below

Floating label above selected icon: the name. Big, bold, tail-pointing down. Gives the "what is this" answer at a glance.

Balloon below: category tag, description, state-aware reason (why available or unavailable). The full Balloon Help payload — but only on selection, so 11 cells don't scream at once.

Anchored bumpers

LB and RB are visual hardware affordances flanking the strip — they look like shoulder buttons because they are shoulder buttons. Jump to previous/next category.

Chevrons ◂ / ▸ between bumpers and strip are the scroll/truncation indicators — pure visual, always present, reinforce directionality.
