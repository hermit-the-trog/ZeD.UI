Info cards: generator, campfire, barbecue, farm plot

Vanilla answers "Info" on these four objects with a small read-only window, and keeps every action in the right-click menu. ZeD gives them one object card in the World window's card drawer, the same slot the item card uses. The card shows the object's state the way the object would show it, and carries its actions as header verbs, like the Vehicle tab's part cards. Switch the objects above the window.

Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDOne object card for all four, in the World window's card drawer. It sits where the item card sits and opens from vanilla's Info option, in the right-click menu or the controller's Interact menu. It replaces the four vanilla info windows. The back arrow, or B, closes it and shows the tab underneath again.
  2. RECOMMENDThe card carries the object's actions, with vanilla's rules. Vanilla's info windows only read out numbers; every action sits in the right-click menu. On the card, those actions are header verbs, as on the Vehicle tab's part cards, and they follow vanilla's rules: a running generator can only be turned off, a connected one cannot be taken, a fire cannot be lit twice. A greyed verb gets a short NEEDS line. The right-click menu keeps working as before.
  3. RECOMMENDThe generator is hardware. As with the oven and microwave, it gets a control panel instead of a list of numbers: a fuel needle, run, low-fuel and line lamps, the hours left at the current draw, and the on / off rocker, which works as the on / off control. Below it, everything it powers and what each item draws, in vanilla's litres per hour.
  4. RECOMMENDFires are measured in time. Vanilla already counts fire fuel in minutes. The card shows hours and minutes on a strip that ends at the sandbox maximum, and ADD FUEL lists what you carry that burns and how much time each piece adds. One fire card serves campfires, charcoal barbecues and fireplaces.
  5. RECOMMENDFarming skill still decides what you can read. Vanilla hides pests until Farming 3, the water bar until 4 and the next-stage timing until 6, and never shows health or fertilizer. The card keeps those gates; a locked reading shows the level that unlocks it. Levels stay vanilla's words (Light, Moderate, Heavy; Parched to Well watered), never raw numbers.
  6. RECOMMENDPlots step through the garden. The header arrows and the garden strip go through every plot in reach, each with the dot of its most urgent need. This replaces vanilla's click-another-square info cursor. Plowed land gets a SOW card from the strip, listing the seeds you carry and their season.
  7. RECOMMENDOne distance rule. Vanilla's generator and barbecue windows never close by distance, the campfire's closes past 2 tiles and the plant's past 6. The card stays open within 6 tiles for all four and closes when the object is gone.
  8. Confirmations stay in the footer. TAKE APART and DIG UP ask in the footer, as the Vehicle tab does. Otherwise the footer is empty; the card carries the state.
