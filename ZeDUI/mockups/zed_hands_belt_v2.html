Hands and the belt

Vanilla shows what you hold as two grey circles in the top-left corner and what hangs on you as a bar of numbered squares at the bottom; a controller player gets no bar at all. Here both become one strip in ZeD's own chrome at the bottom centre: HANDS (primary and secondary, one wide cell for a two-handed weapon) and BELT (every slot your gear gives you, named for where it hangs, with the key that draws it and the item's condition). The belt is only a name and a faint strap line. The Character window's Equip tab manages it. Switch the scenes above.

Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDOne strip on the HUD for the hands and the hotbar. Vanilla splits them: two hand circles in the top-left corner and a plain bar at the bottom. Here they become one ZeD strip at the bottom centre, where the hotbar sat: HANDS on the left, then BELT. In single player the vanilla left-edge column goes entirely (its window buttons already went).
  2. RECOMMENDCells named for where they hang. Each belt cell carries the slot's name under it (BACK, BELT LEFT, BELT RIGHT, HOLSTER, SHOULDER, WEBBING, BACKPACK BOTTOM; vanilla shows it only on hover) and the key that draws it in an accent badge: the key you bound, not the slot's position as vanilla prints. Slots past the eighth have no key, as in vanilla. Slots come and go with what you wear and keep their numbers (vanilla's rule). The belt itself is only the group's name and a faint strap line behind its cells.
  3. RECOMMENDHands: PRIMARY and SECONDARY. The catalog's compact hands (M30), one cell each; a two-handed weapon fills one wide cell under BOTH HANDS (vanilla draws it twice). An item drawn from the belt leaves its cell dashed and faded with IN HAND; a flashlight or walkie that is on carries a lamp dot.
  4. RECOMMENDEvery item shows its condition. A fill bar under each item, in a hand or on the belt, in the inventory rows' colours (green, amber below 60%, red below 30%): you see the knife going blunt before you draw it. Vanilla shows condition only in the tooltip.
  5. RECOMMENDVanilla's controls, plus the missing ones. A number key tapped draws the item or puts it back; held, it opens what fits that slot (instead of vanilla's radial), with REMOVE. Clicking a cell does what its key does; right-click opens the item's menu with Attach. Dragging from your bags onto a cell attaches it: the cells that fit light up, the rest dim, and an occupied one says what it replaces. Dragging onto PRIMARY or SECONDARY equips it in that hand. New: dragging an item off the belt into your bags detaches it, and dragging it from a belt cell to a hand draws it.
  6. RECOMMENDControllers get the strip. Vanilla hides the hotbar from controller players and gives them the D-pad Left wheel (every weapon in the bags and the best light). Here D-pad Left focuses the strip: left and right walk the cells, A draws or puts back, X opens the slot's list, B leaves. Above it, IN YOUR BAGS carries the rest of the old wheel: the other weapons and the best light.
  7. RECOMMENDThe Character window manages it. The Equip tab's HELD + BELT group lists both hands and every belt slot with its key, what fills it and which worn item gives the slot, with ATTACH (what fits) and DETACH. Items on the belt carry a BELT n tag in the inventory lists, where vanilla puts its small hotbar icon.
  8. YOUR CALLA round counter for the gun in hand. Vanilla shows the rounds only in the gun's tooltip; hardcore players count their shots. Option: a small counter in the corner of the PRIMARY cell (11+1/15). Leaving it out keeps vanilla's stakes. Try it with the Round counter control.
  9. RECOMMENDWhere it shows. Hidden while you sit in a vehicle, because the dashboard takes the bottom centre (vanilla hides the bar only for the driver); the number keys keep working. Toggle UI hides it too. In split screen it belongs to player one, as in vanilla. In multiplayer the vanilla column keeps only its server buttons (safety, user, admin, war) until a multiplayer pass; Debug stays under -debug. It follows the ZeD base and accent like the windows.
  10. Vanilla bugs fixed on the way. The bar prints the slot's position, so rebound keys show the wrong numbers. A two-handed weapon is drawn in both hand circles. Controller players get no bar. The hand slots' "Primary" and "Secondary" labels never show. Nothing can be dragged off the bar.
