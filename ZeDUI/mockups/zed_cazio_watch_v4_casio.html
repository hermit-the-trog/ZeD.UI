ZED-UI · CAZIO WATCH v4 · THREE CONTROL DESIGNS · LUA-FLAT PREVIEW · S123 · FOR DIRECTOR REVIEW

Three watch families, three control designs, the same four actions on every one (play/pause, speed step, alarm on/off, alarm set) so the gamepad pattern holds. Digital CaZio (A120 style): rounded-square case, black dial plate with the CaZio mark, WR badge and ALARM CHRONO print, LCD with the top indicator row (P, speed chevrons, ALM), a label strip, then two big coloured front keys and two metal keys on the case bottom. Keys: ALARM (arm) · SET (alarm time) · SPEED (1x 2x 3x) · START/STOP (play, pause). The catalog had four speed keys; this merges them into SPEED + START/STOP, which is what the reference watches do. Classic ana-digi (AQ style): square dial with applied indices, hands, a small LCD window in the dial for the day and date plus the speed chevrons, four side pushers with engraved case labels and a crown. Expensive: round fluted bezel, oyster bracelet, ivory dial, gold hands, date window with cyclops, crown plus two chrono pushers. No watch: only a plain ZeD speed strip at the clock position, no case.
