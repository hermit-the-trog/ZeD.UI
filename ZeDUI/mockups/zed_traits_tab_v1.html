Traits: a tab beside Skills, for Traits As Skills

Traits As Skills (153,861 subscribers) turns traits into ladders that level up and down with play: read enough and Slow Reader becomes no trait, then Fast Reader; skip smoking long enough and Smoker goes. It adds a Traits tab to the game's Character window, which ZeD never shows, so with ZeD on the tab is out of reach. This is that tab, built from ZeD's parts and the mod's own data: each ladder drawn like a Skills row, the trait you have now at the right, and the details for the focused row at the bottom. The numbers are the mod's: its 42 ladders, ranges, XP table and Gain and Lose lists. Click rows, hover the small steps, try Find.

Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDA Traits tab right after Skills, there only while Traits As Skills is on. One row per ladder, drawn like a Skills row: the level in the square, the name, the progress to the next level as the row's fill. Then ten small steps showing the level and which ranges hold a trait (a green line under a step: a good trait there, red: a bad one), and at the right the trait you have now, or "No trait". A trait gained by levelling up carries NEW until the tab is opened. Categories as the mod has them (Skills, Physical, Mind, Social, Behavior), each folds.
  2. RECOMMENDThe details at the bottom follow the focused row, as the Skills tab's pins do: the whole ladder with each trait's range and where you stand, the XP to the next level (or the skill it follows, or the weight in kg), the mod's Gain and Lose lists in its own words ("Reading books or magazines +10 XP per 50 pages"), what the next level up or down changes, and the trait's description from the game.
  3. RECOMMENDDrop the mod's "See all traits" tick. Its job (lighting up every trait's icon on every bar) is done by the steps, which always show the ranges, and hovering a step names its trait and level.
  4. RECOMMENDFind, and a switch between All and With a trait, because there are 42 ladders before other mods add theirs. Traits As Skills is open to other mods: a ladder they register lands under its category (or Other) with no work on ZeD's side.
  5. RECOMMENDNo Traits tab without the mod. The Identity tab already lists the character's traits, good and bad; a second list would repeat it. What the no-mod scene shows (each trait with its description) belongs in Identity instead: the description on hover and on controller focus of each trait there.
  6. RECOMMENDController: the tab sits in the D-pad wheel's Tabs slice like the others. Down from the tab strip lands on Find, then All and With a trait, then the first category. Up and Down walk categories and rows; the details change on focus alone, with no press. A on a category folds or opens it; A on a row does nothing (there is nothing to do with a trait). B goes back to the tab strip.
  7. YOUR CALLKeep the tab without the mod? Recommended no (above). The scene "No mod, if the tab stayed" shows what it would hold: the same traits as Identity, each with its description.
  8. YOUR CALLCategory order. The mod puts Skills first: 26 ladders that follow skill levels (Repair follows Maintenance, Hunting follows Aiming), so they move only when the Skills tab does. Physical and Mind move on their own and are the ones a player watches. Recommend Mind, Physical, Behavior, Social, then Skills; or keep the mod's order.
  9. LIMITIn multiplayer the numbers come from the server about once a second (the mod asks for them), so a level can show a moment late. The mod's own admin screen (Traits XP, under the game's player stats) stays the mod's.
