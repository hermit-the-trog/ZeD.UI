A home for moodles

Vanilla draws its moodles as a column of round icons at the right edge, under where its clock was; the CaZio watch now sits over the top of that column. Here the moodles become a ZeD column under the watch: vanilla's own moodle art in ZeD boxes, the level as four pips and the box's border, the worst moodle on top. Hover reads it; a click opens the Character window where you can do something about it. The Health tab gets their home: HOW YOU FEEL, every moodle with vanilla's words, readable on a controller. The stage is a whole 1080p screen; switch the scenes above it.

Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDUnder the watch, on the right edge. Vanilla's place, so muscle memory holds; the watch and the moodles make one corner that answers "how am I doing"; the bottom centre already belongs to the belt on foot and the dashboard in a car, and the ZeD windows open on the left. The column hangs from the watch: boxes right under it, as many as are on, growing down. Try "Above the belt" with the Where control (in a car it has to sit above the dashboard).
  2. RECOMMENDWorst on top. The workshop plan's "smart gravity": the column sorts by how bad each moodle is, so the most urgent one sits right under the watch, level 4 first and good ones (Well Fed) last; ties keep vanilla's order. A moodle that changes slides to its new place and its box flashes the accent once (vanilla wiggles it in place). See "Just got worse".
  3. RECOMMENDVanilla's art in ZeD boxes. The faces and body icons every player reads at a glance, without vanilla's round tinted backing; the ZeD box carries the level instead: four pips along its foot, a border that turns amber at level 2 and red at 3 and 4, and at level 4 a slow red pulse (ZeD's danger pulse; vanilla pulses too). Good moodles get green pips and border. Try Ink (the art in greyscale) with the Art control.
  4. RECOMMENDThe temperature trend, as vanilla shows it. Overheated and Cold carry vanilla's chevrons (one to three small arrows, up or down) from the same thermoregulator, red while it is getting worse and green while it is getting better. Nothing else gets a trend: vanilla has none.
  5. RECOMMENDHover reads it, in vanilla's words. A chip beside the box: a status dot, the moodle's name for its level (Peckish, Hungry, Very Hungry...) and vanilla's description, plus the trend in words for the two temperature moodles. No numbers vanilla keeps hidden (no hunger percentage): the stakes stay vanilla's.
  6. RECOMMENDA click goes where you can act on it. Wet, Overheated, Cold and Windchill open the Thermal tab (layers, insulation, wetness); Weighed Down, Restricted Movement and Uncomfortable open the Equip tab; every other moodle opens the Health tab's HOW YOU FEEL list with its row marked. Vanilla's moodles do nothing when clicked.
  7. RECOMMENDTheir home in the Character window. The Health tab gets HOW YOU FEEL under the body figure: every moodle that is on, in the same order, with its art, name, pips and vanilla's description, and a THERMAL or EQUIP button where that tab is the place to fix it. This is where a controller player reads moodles (vanilla gives them the icons and no words): LB opens the window, the rows join the D-pad list, A on a row with a button opens that tab. See the Health tab scene with Gamepad input.
  8. YOUR CALLA one-line summary. The Visual Catalog's Compound feeling (M29) as a line above the list: "Hungry and hurting, a bit sleepy, damp and chilly." It reads well but repeats the rows under it, so I would leave it out. Try it with the Summary line control.
  9. RECOMMENDWhen and where it shows. Like the watch: each split-screen player's own right edge, hidden with Toggle UI; the box size follows the ZeD scale and text size, and vanilla's Moodle size option still steps it smaller or larger. Death shows Deceased, as vanilla does. Vanilla's column is parked off-screen (it never accepts being hidden, as the clock did not).
