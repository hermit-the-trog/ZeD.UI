Hair and beard: haircuts, ties and trims in the Identity tab

The game keeps haircuts and beard trims behind the Hair and Beard buttons on its Character screen, which ZeD never shows, so today a ZeD player cannot cut, tie or trim at all. This adds a Hair and Beard section to the Identity tab, under the license: what the character has now, then every choice the game allows from here, each with the tool it needs. The choices come from the game's own rules (the style's length and what it can be cut into), so hair mods that change those rules show in the same list. Click a row, then the button; hover a row for the game's own hint. Change what the character carries and the rows follow.

Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDA Hair and Beard section in Identity, under the license, as a list. Not a pop-up menu like the game's: the current hair and beard sit at the top (style, length, colour), then Hair and Beard as two halves of one switch (a woman has Hair only, as in the game). The license's Hair style and Facial hair lines change as soon as a cut is done.
  2. RECOMMENDGroup the choices by what they need: Tie (no tool), Cut (Scissors; Shave Hair takes a Razor or Scissors), Style (Hair Gel or Hair Spray), and for the beard Shave and Trim (Razor or Scissors). The tool sits at the right of each row with a dot: filled when it is in your inventory or bags, empty when it is not. Wording is the game's: "Cut Hair into Buzz Cut", "Tie Hair into Pony Tail", "Trim Beard into Mustache".
  3. RECOMMENDGreyed rows stay in place and say why. A row you cannot do now is dimmed but can still be picked: the footer and the hover give the game's reason ("Requires Scissors.", "Requires Hair Gel, or Hair Spray.") and the button is greyed. Styles the hair is too short for, or that this style cannot be cut into, sit folded under Out of reach with the reason (Hair too short: needs long hair), so a player learns what growing it out opens up.
  4. RECOMMENDOne list for the hair mods too. ZeD reads the choices the game's hair menu would offer, so when a hair mod changes that menu its choices land here. Spongie's Hairstyle Unlocker opens every style at or under the current length and words the gel ones "Style Hair into ..."; its extra rows carry a small tag with the mod's name. Improved Hair Menu and NeatUI Hairstyler show pictures in a window of their own: the list stays, and a Pictures button opens theirs. New hairstyles from any style pack show up with no extra work.
  5. RECOMMENDController: D-pad Down from the license lands on the Hair or Beard switch; Left and Right flip it. Down again walks the rows; the footer shows the focused row at once, with no press needed. A does it (as the game's menu does: one press), and on a greyed row A does nothing while the footer says why. A on Out of reach folds it open. B goes back to the tab strip, as everywhere in the window.
  6. YOUR CALLNo confirm before a cut. The game cuts on one press and hair grows back, so this has no "Are you sure?". Say if you want one for Shave Hair only.
  7. YOUR CALLThe Now and After heads are simple drawn shapes, enough to tell long from short and tied from loose. The real 3D head (the license photo's) is possible but a bigger job.
  8. LIMITHats come off first and the tool goes in your hand, as the game does it: a cut, tie or untie takes off what is on your head, then puts the Scissors (or Razor) in your main hand. The footer says so before you press.
