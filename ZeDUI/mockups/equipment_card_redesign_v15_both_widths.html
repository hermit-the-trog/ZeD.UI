▌ S119 v15 — ITEM TYPES × WIDTHS
Item Card — Both Drawer Widths Across All Item Types
Five item types, each at both drawer widths. 220w on the left — that's the canonical browsing view, used every time you open the inventory drawer. 480w on the right — the expanded detail view when the drawer is pulled wide. Same scaffold, same chrome rules from v13. The "Facts" panel adapts per item type. Clothing keeps its disclosure pattern at 220w (zones above compete for vertical space); non-clothing items get always-expanded Facts (more breathing room without zones).
