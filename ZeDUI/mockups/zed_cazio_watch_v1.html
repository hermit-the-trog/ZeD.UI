ZED-UI · CAZIO WATCH v1 · LUA-FLAT PREVIEW · S123 · DESIGN FOR DIRECTOR REVIEW

Replaces the Java clock (UIManager.getClock(), 156×62 px at the top-right). Visible only while a wrist watch is worn (vanilla rule). The face is chosen from the worn item: WristWatch_*_Digital* → CaZio LCD module (colour per variant); WristWatch_*_Classic* and Expensive → analog dial with the ZeD type for the readout. 24 h / 12 h follows the game's clock-format option. The date line appears on digital and expensive faces only (parity with isDateVisible). No watch worn = nothing drawn, exactly like vanilla.
