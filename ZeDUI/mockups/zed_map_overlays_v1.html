Maps: a key, drawing, mark size, other players and shared notes

ZeD's Maps tab is its own map, so the six map mods that draw on the game's map show nothing in it, and ZeD has no map key, not even the game's own. This puts each of them into the Maps tab: the key as a small panel over the map's corner, drawing and mark size on the toolbar, other players on the map and in a list at the side, learned notes from Share Annotations in the same list, and the area's name over the map. New pieces have a dashed outline. Click the toolbar, the key, the ticks and the x's; switch the map style on the toolbar or above.

Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDThe key is built into ZeD, mod or no mod. A Key button on the toolbar opens a small panel over the map's bottom-left corner with the game's own eight entries, words and colours (Community, Hospitality, Industrial, Medical, Parks, Residential, Restaurants & Entertainment, Retail & Commercial). It is the game's key that ZeD lost; Map Legend UI shows the same eight, so with it on ZeD shows nothing extra. The key belongs to the Survey map, whose buildings are coloured by use; on Color and Paper the button stays and the panel says those maps show the ground as it looks, not by use.
  2. RECOMMENDDrawing only when Draw On The Map is on. A pencil Draw button after the inks opens a second toolbar row with the mod's tools and words: Draw, Line, Erase, Thickness, Fill. The inks stay the ones in row one, so drawing needs a pen or pencil as every mark does, and Erase needs an eraser (the game's own "Requires an eraser."). Strokes are ordinary map marks, as the mod makes them, so they show on the game's map too. ZeD writes its own drawing tool for this (the mod's tools expect the game's map panel).
  3. RECOMMENDSymbol Size only when Map Symbol Size Slider is on, under the toolbar's disclosure beside the full mark grid (where the mod puts it on the game's map). It moves the size of every mark and note and saves to the mod's own setting, so ZeD's map and the game's agree. A tick marks the game's size; Reset goes back to it.
  4. RECOMMENDOther players: the game's own dots always; names and the list only with Players On Map. Without the mod ZeD's map shows other players the way the server's own setting allows. With it, ZeD draws the mod's green squares and names and follows its sandbox rules (names, own name, faction only, dead players, distance). A Players section in the side list gives each name and how far and which way; clicking a name centres the map on them.
  5. RECOMMENDLearned notes only when Share Annotations is on, in the side list: one row per set with the mod's own name for it (By Rick Harlan, July 9th 1993 at 14:20), a tick to show or hide it and an x to delete it. Delete asks in the list's footer, as ZeD confirms elsewhere. ZeD also applies the sets the player hid before when its map opens; today a set hidden on the game's map stays hidden for good under ZeD.
  6. RECOMMENDThe side list appears only when it has something in it (multiplayer with Players On Map, or learned notes), docked at the right of the unfolded map and as a panel over the map's right edge in the drawer, opened by a list button. In the drawer the new toolbar buttons show as icons only, with a hover hint, so the Survey / Color / Paper switch still fits on the row. The area's name from Where Am I? sits over the top of the map, only with that mod.
  7. YOUR CALLOther players' colour. Shown in the mod's own bright green with the name underneath, as players of the mod know it. The alternative is ZeD's accent, which matches the toolbar but can be confused with your own marks. Recommend the mod's green.
  8. YOUR CALLA key for Paper. The paper road map has its own printed look (roads, towns, water). A small key for those three could come later; not drawn here.
  9. LIMITDrawing is the big one (several days): a drag tool, erase by area, and an undo for strokes that can be thousands of small marks. The key, the size slider, players, learned notes and the area name are a few hours each.
  10. Controller: the new toolbar buttons join the toolbar's row of stops; the side list is its own zone (A ticks a set, X deletes, A on a player centres the map); in Draw, A held draws along the stick.
