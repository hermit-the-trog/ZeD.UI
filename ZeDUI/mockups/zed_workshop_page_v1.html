Update: Oct 2026 (example of the voice)
The bar over your head is ours now. It says what you're doing and how far the queue's got (or, in Options, just ten little lights). Moving a stack of stuff counts properly as one job. Fixed: nothing; it's new. Known: resting on a chair or a bed hasn't been seen yet; tell us if it looks wrong.

Every upload gets a short, plain note like this. Steam dates them; the description never carries a changelog.

Notes

  1. Stand-ins. Slots 1, 4, 5 and 6 use QA captures (crops, test saves, odd sizes). The real gallery is reshot at 1920 × 1080, UI scale 1, default theme, in one staged save. The QA bridge can stage most of them so they're repeatable after updates.
  2. Order is the story: the desk (what it is) → both windows (the core) → depth (cards, stations) → always-on (HUD, driving) → how you play (controller) → your colours → honest comparison → motion.
  3. Steam's frame is approximate. Sizes and colours are close enough to judge the layout, not pixel-exact.
