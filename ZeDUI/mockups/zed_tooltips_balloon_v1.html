Tooltips: Balloon Help v1

ZeD's tooltips as System 7 Balloon Help

Punch list 118 and 119, for beta 10. Every pop-up that explains a control becomes one balloon in the theme's colors, with a tail that points at the thing it explains, sized to its text at every scale and text size. The context strip's end arrows fill with the accent. Inputs: the Mac's tooltip survey on beta9-test2, the Visual Catalog v2.0 (A07 Tooltip), the tooltip research of Nov 8, and today's code.

1. The icon strip's balloon

The right-click menu as an icon strip; the balloon names the cell under the pointer or the controller's focus. Today it is a white box forced on every theme, the name in Fira Mono Bold, centered and never wrapped, so a long name runs out of both sides of a box that stops at 250. Try Russian with Large text.

  • The balloon takes the theme: surface 2, the strong border (2u in High contrast), System 7's hard shadow. The target card under the strip already does.
  • The tail starts at the balloon's lower left corner and its tip touches the cell; near the right edge it moves to the lower right corner, and with no room above, the balloon goes under the target card with the tail pointing up.
  • The name is in the heading face (Fira Sans Condensed SemiBold), wraps at 320u (368u at Large) and never leaves the box. In a submenu the path before it is in the accent.
  • Item 119: both end boxes fill with the accent, framed in the strip's ink, the arrow in the accent's own text color.
2. A disabled action: the requirement balloon

World menu, Disassemble, Mahogany Drawers, with no saw and the drawers still full. The game sends its requirements in two columns, green for what you have and red for what is missing. Today ZeD prints them twice, large and then small and grey, with every color gone, so nothing says the saws are missing or that the drawers must be emptied.

  • The game's picture of the thing, then two columns: the game's label, then its values one under another. A met requirement gets a check, a missing one a cross, in text-safe positive and danger colors (new tokens, at least 4.5:1 in every theme), so color is never the only sign.
  • A condition with no label (the game's "- Has items in container.") gets the cross and spans the width, without the dash.
  • The list is printed once. The game's Name row is left out: the title already names the thing.
  • Rows keep the game's order whether they are met or not, so the layout never jumps between options.
3. The list style

The same menu with Context menu set to List. Today's balloon sits beside the menu at a fixed 280 with no tail, and it turns the game's line breaks into spaces, so the labels and values run together in one paragraph.

  • The balloon sits beside the row, the tail on its left edge pointing at the row; with no room on the right it goes on the left with the tail on its right edge.
  • The same requirement layout as the strip: one look, one set of rules.
4. Hover hints and the moodle balloon

ZeD's other hover boxes: the one-line hint on icon-only controls and the detail card. Today they open below and right of the pointer, so they move with it and land on whatever comes next; a disabled control's hint is drawn as faint as the control.

  • One-line hints use the body face (Fira Sans) and wrap at 200u (230u at Large). A balloon with a title and lines uses the heading face for the title, as the detail card does.
  • The balloon is anchored to the control, not the pointer: above it for a row of controls (the belt), beside it for a column (a tab strip, the moodles). The pad's focus gets the same balloon at once; the mouse after ZeD's 450 ms rest.
  • A disabled control's balloon is drawn at full contrast.
  • The moodle hover is the game's own today (drawn by the game, not by ZeD); the balloon shown is what ZeD draws if Build can take it over.
  • The game's item box over the belt (ISToolTipInv) stays the game's: half the Workshop hooks its renderer, and replacing it is what makes tooltip mods fight.
5. Item 119: the strip's arrows in every accent

Today both end boxes are near-black on every theme. Now: the accent, framed in the strip's ink, the arrow in the accent's own text color. The ratio under each is the arrow against the box; a graphic needs 3:1.

6. Fit test: the longest strip name

"Put in Container (Driver's Seat)" in English, German and Russian at Small and Large text, at the scale chosen above. Red marks text outside its box. The numbers are the box width and the longest line, in screen pixels.

7. Spec for Build (beta 10)

One balloon

  1. Every pop-up that explains a control draws one balloon: the context strip's balloon, the context list's balloon, the hint (requestHint), the detail card (requestDetailCard) and the requirement balloon. ZeD_Tooltip in Atoms.lua becomes the same balloon.
  2. Box: T.surf2 at alpha 1 (hover boxes never go see-through), a frame of T.borderS at T.borderW hairlines, drawHardShadow. Square corners. The strip's balloon leaves its locked paper and ink: only the strip's cells, bumpers and arrows keep them.
  3. Padding 8u at the sides, 5u top and bottom. Line step: the face's height plus 2u. 3u between the title and what follows.
  4. Faces: a one-line hint in F2 T.text; a title in F4 T.textHead, a submenu path before it in F4 T.textSec with " > " between; lines in F2 T.text; numbers in F3. No F1 in balloons.
  5. Width: the longest unwrapped line plus the padding, up to 200u for a one-line hint and 320u for anything with a title, both times 1.15 at Large text. Every line wraps at word breaks to that width, the title too. A word longer than the width widens the box up to 1.5 times; past that the word breaks. Height follows the lines. Nothing is clipped and nothing spills.
  6. Measure with ZeD.TM in the live faces at the live scale and text size, every time the text, the scale, the text size or the language changes (ZeD.Text.measureEpoch).

The tail

Drawn with drawRect on the hairline grid (one cell = T.hair), as the strip's pointer is. Outer triangle in T.borderS, inner triangle in T.surf2 inset by the frame's width, and the frame under the tail's base filled with T.surf2 so the tail opens into the box. 10 cells deep. Its straight edge sits 6u in from the box's corner. The tip stops one hairline short of the thing it points at.

Placement

  1. Anchor to the control's rectangle, never to the pointer. The strip and a row of controls (the belt, a footer): above, tail at the lower left, tip over the control's left part (12u in, or its middle when narrower). A column (the tab strips, the moodles) and the list menu: beside, tail on the near edge, tip at the control's middle height.
  2. Keep 4u inside the screen: past the right edge, use the lower right corner; past the top, go under (for the strip: under the target card); past the right edge beside, go to the left side. A box that still does not fit slides inside the screen, and the tail stays on its target.
  3. Never cover the strip, the target card, the hovered row or the control.

Behavior (from the Nov 8 research)

  1. Mouse: after ZeD.HINT_DELAY_MS (450 ms) of rest; moving onto a neighbor while a balloon is up shows the neighbor's at once. Pad and keys: at once on focus, the same balloon.
  2. It stays while the pointer is on the control or on the balloon itself, with no timer (WCAG 1.4.13). Escape or B hides it until the pointer or focus moves on; in the context menu B still closes the menu.
  3. One balloon at a time. It appears and goes at once (no fades).
  4. A disabled control's balloon is at full contrast.

The requirement balloon

  1. Parse the option's tooltip description, which the game writes as ISToolTip rich text (ISDisassembleMenu builds it from the moveable's info table): <LINE> ends a line, <SETX:n> splits it into label and value, <RGB:r,g,b> colors what follows; drop the other tags (<INDENT> and the rest). A line with an empty label adds its value to the row above: that is how the game lists the tools that each do. A line with no <SETX> is a condition row.
  2. State from color: the game's good highlight color (getCore():getGoodHighlitedColor()) is met, its bad highlight color missing, anything else neutral. A description with no <SETX> anywhere keeps its lines as plain lines, with the same marks for good and bad lines.
  3. Columns: when the tooltip has a picture (toolTip.texture, the furniture's sprite), it comes first, 32u square on T.surf3 in a 1u T.borderS frame, drawn at its aspect, then 8u. Labels in F2 T.text, as wide as the widest label; 8u; a 7-cell mark and 4u; the values in F2, wrapping inside their column. A condition row spans the label and value columns with its mark, the leading "- " dropped. The rows block is at least as tall as the picture.
  4. Colors (new tokens, the director's call, Nov 8): marks and values use T.positiveText and T.dangerText, status colors safe for text. Dark: dangerText #DE7045 (5.3:1 on surface 2), positiveText = positive #7A9A34 (5.3:1). Light: positiveText #204A0C (5.1:1), dangerText = danger #7A2A11 (4.8:1). High contrast: both = its positive and danger. Neutral values in T.text. The crafting card's met and unmet lines move to the same tokens.
  5. Print it once (drop the state rows when the body is the requirement list), keep the game's row order, and leave out a Name row whose value repeats the title.
