The Reader

v2: two sizes. The full reader (a window of its own) for a whole newspaper or a page of writing, and the same reader in the Character window's Journal tab for quick looks and quick notes without covering the game: the tab's Flyers becomes Papers (newspapers too), and it gains Notebooks. The "Tab" screens are drawn over a live capture of the Character window at its default size.

The last two reading surfaces still vanilla: printed media (newspapers, flyers, brochures) and writing in a notebook or journal. One ZeD window for both, opened from the item card's Read or Write: the piece as the game prints it, its words on a clean page, the places it mentions on your map, and a real notebook page to write on. Reading and writing stay vanilla's actions (time, light, a pencil): only how they look changes. The newspaper and flyer are the game's own, captured on 42.21.0; the notebook's words are made up.

Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDA window of its own, not the drawer. The Character window's card drawer is about 220 px wide and a newspaper page is 740 x 850: in the drawer it would be a stamp. The reader opens like the map's pop-out: when the Read action finishes (or Write starts), centred over the game; B or Escape folds it back to the item's card. The card keeps Read / Write as today.
  2. RECOMMENDPaper and Words. Paper is the piece exactly as the game composes it (its layered pages, pictures and old-print faces), with zoom and drag. Words sets the same piece as a clean page in ZeD's faces at your text size: headlines as headings, stories as paragraphs, pictures as their captions. Vanilla's own text view runs every story together in one small block and shows stray line codes ("\n") as text (see "Vanilla today").
  3. RECOMMENDOn this page. The side column lists the piece's stories; a press jumps to it in Words. Flyers and brochures list their places instead, each with Show on map: ZeD's map opens on the spot (vanilla's "reveal on map" buttons, up to five). Whether reading marks them on its own stays your game option.
  4. RECOMMENDA notebook page to write on. Ruled paper with the binder holes and the red margin, the notebook's name at the top (edit it in place), one line per ruled line (the game's 15 lines a page, 80 letters a line), the pages down the left with their first words, the page and letter count under it. Erase page asks in the strip; the padlock locks it so only you can write in it. Done saves (vanilla's pages and name); Back leaves it as it was.
  5. RECOMMENDSomeone else's locked notebook opens to read only: a band says so, and the pages still turn.
  6. YOUR CALLThe Flyers gallery (Journal tab) could open any piece you have read, without the paper in hand (the character remembers it), or only the ones you still carry. Remembering is kinder; carrying is closer to vanilla.
  7. YOUR CALLThe page's paper. Shown here as light paper in every theme (it is paper). The other way: the dark ZeD panel with faint rules, matching the windows.

New in v2: fitted stories, and two sizes

  1. RECOMMENDStories fitted to their columns. The game sets every newspaper story at 40% size in a fixed spot, so the words sit as a small block in a mostly empty column (your Knox Knews note). The Paper view now refits each story to its column: it moves up under its headline and grows to the largest size that still ends above what comes next or the page foot, with small headlines growing alongside. Bylines and "see page" lines stay as printed, and a story running on across several columns keeps one size. Prototyped in the game on four papers (Knox Knews twice, the Kentucky Herald, the National Dispatch): ZeDUI/qa/S125_reader/print_fit_sheet.png.
  2. RECOMMENDOne reader, two sizes. The Journal tab holds the quick ones: a glance at a paper, a line in a notebook, without covering the game. The full reader is for a whole newspaper or a page of writing. Each opens the other at the same place: the tab's Open full pops the full reader out on the same story or page, and the full reader's Back returns to where you came from (the tab, or the item's card).
  3. RECOMMENDFlyers becomes Papers: the newspapers, flyers and brochures the character has found, by their real names, with filters (All, News, Flyers, On map). Today's tab lists all 135 flyers in the game, found or not, with names made from file names and blank tiles, and leaves newspapers out.
  4. RECOMMENDA Notebooks sub-tab: the notebooks, journals and diaries you carry, the page small with a quick-note field under it. Write adds the line to the page as vanilla's write action (a pencil or pen, and light).
  5. YOUR CALLThe Journal log's writing box. Today it saves notes with no notebook and no pencil, which vanilla never allows. Recommended: it writes into a notebook you carry (picked beside the box), so the log becomes the newest lines of your notebooks; old entries stay. The other way: keep it free, a player's notepad outside the game's rules.
  6. YOUR CALLFound but unread papers: listed dim (you know you have it, not what it says), or hidden until read.

Controller

  1. LB / RB: Paper and Words (in a notebook: the page before and after). D-pad: through the stories or places, or pans the paper when zoomed; the triggers zoom. A: a story jumps, a place shows on the map, a notebook line opens the ZeD keyboard. X: erase page (asks). Y: lock. B: back to the card. In the Journal tab the same buttons work on the small page; Y there is Open full.
