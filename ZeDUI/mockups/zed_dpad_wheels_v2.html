D-pad wheels

Four wheels, one per D-pad direction, in place of the game's D-pad wheels. Left is you (the Character window's things), Right is the world around you (the World window's things), Up is social, Down is here and now. Hold the direction with your left thumb, point with the right stick, let go of the D-pad to take. (The D-pad and the left stick both sit under the left thumb on PlayStation and Xbox pads, so the right stick points.) A slice that opens more (the corner triangle) swaps the ring in place, with Back as its last slice, as the game's emote wheel does. Every slice keeps its place; one that can't be done right now is stippled and the bubble says why. Point with the mouse to try it; click to take or to open. The arrow keys switch wheels; Escape backs out.

Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDEight slices on Left and Right, with wheels inside. Eight is what a thumb hits without looking (the four D-pad directions and the four between). Your Character list is twelve things, so four of them share a slice: Medicine and first aid is one slice, and Use holds smoking, reading, the watch and the radio. Switch Slices to 12 to see the other way: every thing one flick away, like a clock face, but each slice is a 30 degree target instead of 45.
  2. RECOMMENDPlaces never move. The game's wheels add a slice only when it applies, so Drink sits somewhere else every time. Here every slice keeps its place and reads as off when there's nothing to do ("Nothing to drink on you."). Muscle memory beats a shorter wheel.
  3. RECOMMENDThings you carry are picked in the second wheel, one slice per kind, the best first, "x4" when there are more, as the quick-use wheel does today (food spoiling soonest first, water before drinks with a downside). The pick runs the game's own action, with its rules.
  4. RECOMMENDLetting go on a slice with a triangle opens its wheel and keeps it open until you pick, or B backs out one level. Letting go anywhere else takes the slice. One rule for all four.
  5. SETTLEDPoint with the right stick. The left thumb holds the D-pad, so the right thumb points (director, Oct 23). While a wheel is open the right stick doesn't aim and doesn't hide the windows; aiming comes back once the stick is centred.
  6. SETTLEDIn a car, Vehicle is a World slice (director, Oct 23), live in and next to a car. The game put the vehicle wheel on Up, which is now Social.
  7. SETTLEDDown's eighth slice is Light (director, Oct 23): the brightest light you carry, on or off, without opening a wheel inside. The game had it on D-pad Left; it leaves Weapons. Down gathers what has no home today: the stick-click wheel (crouch, sit, auto-walk) can't be reached because ZeD never binds the stick clicks, the Back button's game speed and zoom, and the help.
  8. When the wheels open. Only when no window has focus, as the game does. With a window focused, the D-pad keeps moving focus (GAMEPAD_SPEC); nothing changes there. The Back button's wheel stays as it is.
