Decisions for you

  1. DECIDEDYour desk. The pixel-art desk you drew, shown at whole multiples so every pixel stays square: 4x at 1080p, 8x at 4K. In the game it is one 480 x 270 picture (about 11 KB). Its screen canvas (960 x 720 at 1080p) holds the menu.
  2. RECOMMENDThe main menu on the screen. A menu bar (ZeD, File, Multiplayer, Settings, Debug under -debug; the version and the time at the right) and one Project Zomboid window: Continue first with the last save's picture, name, character, mode, map and when you last played; New Game, Load Game, Join a Server, Host a Game, Tutorial as ZeD buttons; Options, Mods, Workshop, Credits and Quit in the footer. The menu bar repeats them; nothing lives only there.
  3. RECOMMENDLoad Game on the monitor. Your saves grouped by mode (vanilla's folders), newest first, each with its picture, character, map and last played; a search box. The selected save on the right: its picture, mode, map, last played, mods, version, and PLAYING AS when a save has more than one character (vanilla's player list). Footer: BACK, then DELETE, MORE (vanilla's Rename File, New Player, Choose Mods, Open File Location) and PLAY. DELETE asks in the footer. A save whose character died says so with a red dot, and PLAY becomes NEW PLAYER, as vanilla.
  4. RECOMMENDNew Game on the monitor, as a 1993 icon view. Vanilla's six playstyle pictures (Spiffo, from the game) in a row like a System 7 Finder or Program Manager window: the chosen one darkened with its name highlighted, vanilla's description in a plain box below; BACK, CHOOSE MODS, NEXT. NEXT goes on to vanilla's next screens (map and spawn, sandbox options, character creation) at first, full screen; each gets its own mockup later, character creation first.
  5. DECIDEDA flashback to the era, not today's design. Inside the monitor everything is 1993: windows, buttons, icon views, list boxes, plain labels. No side bars on selections, no small spaced-out tags, no step trails or striped placeholders; a selection is ZeD's accent fill, as in the game's lists.
  6. RECOMMENDReal ZeD windows. Every window, on the monitor and the pause window in game, is a ZeD window as the Character and World windows draw it: the pinstriped title bar with the close box and "name + section", the window's own footer strip (theme, accent, text size), ZeD's buttons (the double rounded outline with the mono label; accent for the main action; red, pulsing, only on the confirm step of Delete and Quit), and the action strip above the footer. The close box is Back on Load and New Game, Resume on the pause window, Quit on the main window (asking in the footer).
  7. RECOMMENDQuit and Delete ask in the footer. No pop-ups: the window's footer turns into the question with CANCEL and the red action. The power button on your monitor's case is Quit too (it shows nothing until you point at it).
  8. RECOMMENDController. Each window is one D-pad list (Continue, the rows, then the footer); in Load Game the list, then PLAY; LB / RB do nothing here; A presses, B goes back (closes a menu, cancels a question, leaves Load / New Game). Prompts sit under the window.
  9. RECOMMENDThe pause menu in game. Escape (or Start) dims the world and opens a ZeD window titled with your character: a Paused header with the game day and time, ZeD buttons for Resume (accent), Options, and Players and Invite Friends in multiplayer; the leaving actions sit in the action strip: Quit to Desktop and Save and Quit (vanilla's QUIT, back to the main menu). The title bar says which game. The close box, B and Escape resume.
  10. RECOMMENDOther screen shapes. 1080p and 4K are exact multiples. At 1440p the desk draws at 6x and trims a little from every edge, so pixels stay square; ultrawide screens trim the top and bottom (a wider version of the desk would avoid it). The screen canvas follows the desk, and the menu follows the screen.
  11. YOUR CALLSmall life on the desk. Optional: the case's green light blinks while a save loads, the boombox needle drifts, the lamp flickers once now and then. None of it does anything. Want any of it?
