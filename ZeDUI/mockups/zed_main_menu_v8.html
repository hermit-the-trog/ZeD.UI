Main menu: the desk, the computer, the menu on its screen

v7.1's room stays: a desk in Knox County at night in July 1993, the computer as the menu. v8 makes it usable at the game's real size: the monitor is a 1024 x 768 screen, so the menu on it reads at ZeD's normal text sizes, and everything you came for is on the screen at once in one Project Zomboid window: Continue with your last save's picture and details, New Game, Load, Join, Host, Tutorial, and Options, Mods, Workshop, Credits, Quit. Each item runs vanilla's own action. The pause menu in game is a small ZeD window over the world. The stage is a whole 1080p screen; switch the scenes above it.

Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDThe room from v7.1, with a bigger monitor. A desk at night: the computer in the middle, a window with the blinds half down, the Knox County poster, a July 1993 calendar, the desk lamp, a walkie, a soda, floppies, the keyboard and mouse. The monitor fills most of the screen so its screen is 1024 x 768 (at 1080p): the menu on it reads at ZeD's normal text sizes. v7.1's monitor was a third of this and its text was 6 to 9 px. The room and the monitor become plates (like the watch and the dashboard) and scale with the screen.
  2. RECOMMENDEverything on the screen at once. One Project Zomboid window: Continue first, with your last save's picture, its name, your character, mode and map and when you last played (all from vanilla's Load screen); then New Game, Load Game, Join a Server, Host a Game, Tutorial; the footer holds Options, Mods, Workshop, Credits and Quit. v7.1 started on an empty desktop with Continue inside the File menu.
  3. RECOMMENDThe menu bar repeats them. ZeD (About ZeD-UI), File (Continue, New Game, Load Game, Tutorial, Quit), Multiplayer (Join a Server, Host a Game), Settings (Options, Mods, Workshop), plus Debug under -debug; the version and the time at the right. It is there for the 1993 feel and for mouse habits: nothing lives only in the menu bar.
  4. RECOMMENDVanilla's actions, vanilla's screens first. Every item runs vanilla's own handler by its id (LATESTSAVE, SOLO, LOAD, MULTIPLAYER, COOP, TUTORIAL, OPTIONS, MODS, WORKSHOP, CREDITS, EXIT), so nothing about saves, mods or servers changes. At first the New Game, Load, Join, Host, Options and Mods screens are vanilla's (full screen, over the room). Then each gets a ZeD window on this screen, with its own mockup: Load and New Game first (mode, map, sandbox), character creation next, Options and Mods last (v7.1's sketches are the start).
  5. RECOMMENDQuit asks in the footer. Quit, File > Quit or the monitor's power button turn the window's footer into "Quit Project Zomboid?" with CANCEL and QUIT (ZeD's footer confirmation, no pop-up). Vanilla quits at once.
  6. RECOMMENDController. The window's buttons are one D-pad list starting on CONTINUE (on NEW GAME with no saves): up and down along Continue and the rows, then left and right along the footer; A presses; B cancels a quit or closes a menu. Prompts sit under the window like the ZeD windows' footers. The menu bar is for the mouse: everything in it is also in the window.
  7. RECOMMENDThe pause menu in game. Escape (or Start) dims the world and opens a small ZeD window: Return to Game, Options, Save and Quit to Menu, Quit to Desktop, plus Scoreboard and Invite Friends in multiplayer (vanilla's in-game list). A line on top says whose game it is and where. Vanilla's in-game menu is the same screen with a different list, so the same hook serves both.
  8. YOUR CALLThe props. The floppy on the desk wears your last save's name, the calendar is July 1993 with the first days crossed off, the window shows the night with the blinds half down, the lamp is lit. Pure decoration: they do nothing when clicked (v7.1's poster gnome is gone). Keep, change or add?
  9. No emoji, Fira on the screen, prop lettering is printed on the props. v7.1 used emoji for icons and a pixel face; the screen now uses ZeD's type and window chrome, so the main menu matches the rest of the mod.
