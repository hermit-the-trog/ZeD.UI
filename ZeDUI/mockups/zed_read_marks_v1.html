Read marks on books, magazines, maps, fliers and tapes

The game already remembers what your character has read: pages read in each skill book, the maps, fliers and brochures read, the tapes watched and the recipes known. Has Been Read shows it as marks on the game's own inventory rows, which ZeD does not draw, so today a bag of books in ZeD gives no hint until each one is opened in the item card. This puts a native ZeD mark on every row and grid cell that can be read, from the same game records, with no mod needed. Three ways to show it; a character with Carpentry 4, Mechanics 3, Cooking 2, Fishing 3 and no First Aid, looting a bookcase.

Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDThe status in the COND column (B) in the list, a dot in the cell's corner in the grid. Books, magazines, maps and tapes have no condition, so that column is empty for them today; it takes a dot and a word: Unread, 140 of 260, Read, Known, Watched. Words read at a glance on every theme and need no legend. Where the column is hidden (a narrow window), the dot moves to the end of the name. The corner mark (A) is the most compact and closest to Has Been Read; the page bar (C) shows progress best but cannot tell "not read" from "nothing to read" without the hover text.
  2. RECOMMENDShape carries the meaning, colour backs it up: a ring for unread, a half-filled dot for part read (or some recipes known), a full dot for read. Unread and part read take the accent (something left to do), read takes the green "good" colour. With the green accent the two greens sit close (try it); the shapes still tell them apart, so no colour rule is needed for that accent.
  3. RECOMMENDA NEXT LEVEL tag on skill books that fit your next level (Has Been Read's "next target"), after the name where the BELT tag goes; in the grid, a small accent arrow in the corner. Mechanics II here suits Mechanics 4, First Aid I suits First Aid 1; Cooking III (levels 5 to 6) does not get it at Cooking 2.
  4. RECOMMENDA skill book below your level counts as read (Fishing I at Fishing 3), as the game teaches nothing more from it and as Has Been Read does; the hover text says why.
  5. RECOMMENDHover text in the game's own words: "Unread.", "Already read.", "Already watched.", "Pages:", "Some recipes known from profession or traits.", then the recipes known and not known, and the next level a book is good for. The item card's existing page bar stays as it is.
  6. YOUR CALLDim what is read? Fading read items (the switch above) makes the unread ones jump out in a big bookcase, but a dimmed row can look unavailable. Recommend off, as an option in ZeD's settings.
  7. YOUR CALLYour own tick. Has Been Read also lets you tick an item by hand from its menu (to mark "keep" or "already have"). Recommend leaving it out: it is a second meaning on the same mark. If wanted later, it would be a separate flag, not the read mark.
  8. LIMITTapes and discs are all or nothing: the game only says whether every line has been heard, so a half-watched tape shows as not watched. Paperbacks, comics and newspapers are read or not read (no pages kept).
