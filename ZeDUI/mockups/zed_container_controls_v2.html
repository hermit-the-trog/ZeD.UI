Container Buttons v2

The buttons as built (one row at the bottom of each list, scrolling sideways when it is wider than the list), with your two asks: the container's own actions move into its heading, between the name and the weight, and common actions become icons, the unique ones stay words. Every icon button says what it does in a hover hint (ZeD's own hint box) after a short pause; on a controller the footer names it. The icons are the ZeD library's. Each slice is the World window's list (or the Character window's) at 150%.

Common and unique

Common: what every container has (the moves) and what many objects share. Unique: one kind of object's own, and anything a mod adds.

Every container

Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDIcons for the moves and for the actions many objects share (Take all, Transfer all, Same type, Same type to all nearby, To floor; power, settings, light, add fuel, put out, lock, delete all). Words for the rest: Wear all, Switch outfits, Mode: dryer, Insert tank, Remove tank, Take apart, Add corpse, Close trunk, and every mod's button (we cannot know a picture for those).
  2. RECOMMENDA hover hint on every icon button, in ZeD's hint box, after a short pause: the action in a few words ("Take all", "Take the kinds you already carry"). Word buttons need none. On a controller the footer's prompt names the focused icon ("A Take all").
  3. RECOMMENDThe container's own actions sit in the heading, between the name and the weight, words first, then icons; the moves stay at the bottom. With icons nearly every container fits; when one does not (the large text size, a campfire with a corpse to add) the heading's actions scroll like the bottom ribbon, between two small arrows, and the name never shortens.
  4. RECOMMENDPower shows its state: the icon lights in the accent while the thing runs (a stove on, a washer going), dim while it is off. The hint says what a press will do ("Turn on" / "Turn off").
  5. RECOMMENDDelete all's question takes over the bottom row ("Delete all 9 items for good?" Cancel / Delete all), where the other moves are, so the heading never changes height.
  6. YOUR CALLThe icons. Shown here from the library: inbox in (Take all), inbox out (Transfer all), two sheets (Same type), arrows spreading (Same type, all nearby), arrow to a line (To floor), power, gear, match, extinguisher, fire with logs (Add fuel), padlock, bin. Any can be swapped.
