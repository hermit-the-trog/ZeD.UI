Fluids: pour and container info

Vanilla opens a floating "Transfer Liquid" window: two boxes you fill by choosing an item from a pop-up menu, a Switch button, a slider and Pour. Its "Container Info" is a second window with the same box, read-only. ZeD folds both into one fluid card in the World window's card drawer, the slot the object cards use. Two tanks side by side show what pours where and how full each ends up, one amount control sets the pour, and your containers sit in a list under it, each with FROM and INTO. Switch the scenes above the window.

Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDOne fluid card for pouring and for Container Info, in the World window's card drawer. Every vanilla way in opens it: an item's Liquid menu (Container Info, Transfer Liquid), a rain barrel, sink, toilet, bathtub or well (Transfer, Info), and a fluid slot on a crafting station. The container you opened it from is the starting point. Info is the same card with nothing chosen to pour into yet. The floating vanilla windows never show.
  2. RECOMMENDTwo tanks, side by side. POUR FROM on the left, POUR INTO on the right, each a vertical fill gauge in the liquid's own colour with a white line where it will end up. The hatched part leaves the left tank and arrives in the right one, so you see before you pour how full both will be. Between them, the arrow and the amount.
  3. RECOMMENDYour containers are a list, not a pop-up menu. Vanilla fills each box by opening a menu of items. The card lists every liquid container you carry, with its fill and a tag saying what a pour would do (EMPTY, SAME, MIXES, FULL, WON'T MIX, WON'T TAKE, using vanilla's own reasons), and two buttons on each row: FROM and INTO. A world container you opened it from stays pinned at the top.
  4. RECOMMENDOne amount control. The catalog slider (M16) runs from nothing to the most that can pour (the smaller of what the left holds and the room in the right, vanilla's Max Pour), and starts at the most, as vanilla does. 1/4, 1/2 and ALL set it in one press. On a controller, left and right on the slider step it by 5% and hold repeats.
  5. RECOMMENDWhat is in it, as vanilla knows it. Under the tanks, LIQUIDS lists what the left container holds, each liquid with its share, in vanilla's words (Water, Bleach, Mixture). Tainted water and poison show the way vanilla's tooltip shows them (the tainted label follows the sandbox setting; poison reads Mild to Deadly), with the warning on the pill.
  6. RECOMMENDHeader verbs: POUR, SWITCH, EMPTY. POUR walks to a world container if needed and runs vanilla's pour; while it runs, the item card's progress band shows the litres moved with STOP. SWITCH swaps the two sides. EMPTY pours the left container out on the ground and asks in the footer first. When POUR cannot run, the NEEDS line carries vanilla's reason ("Cannot mix these liquids.").
  7. RECOMMENDOne distance rule, as the object cards. Vanilla closes its window past 2 tiles from a world container. The card stays open within 6 tiles, like the other object cards, and walks back when you pour.
  8. Litres everywhere. Amounts read in litres, vanilla's unit: two decimals under 10 L (0.25 L), one under 100 L (96.0 L), whole litres above (160 L). A container whose amount vanilla hides reads UNKNOWN.
