Belt hands together

The two hand circles move next to each other on the belt, so an item held in both hands can join them into one shape with one condition track. As in the vanilla game, the main hand is bigger than the off hand: 56 units against 44, the size of a belt slot (vanilla draws the off hand at three quarters of the main). Below: the belt as it is now, then two places for the pair. The strap, slots and item pictures are cut from a 4K capture of the game at 1.75x, the knife scaled down for the smaller hand. The resized hands and the joined shape are drawn with the belt's own pixel rules but aren't in the game yet.
