Belt hands together

The two hand circles move next to each other on the belt. As in the vanilla game, the main hand is bigger than the off hand: 56 units against 44, the size of a belt slot (vanilla draws the off hand at three quarters of the main; today both are 52). When one item is held in both hands, the main hand keeps its circle and its condition ring, and the off hand slides in behind it, peeking out 14 units. Below: the belt as it is now, then two places for the pair. The strap, slots and item pictures are cut from a 4K capture of the game at 1.75x, the knife scaled down for the smaller hand. The resized hands are drawn with the belt's own pixel rules but aren't in the game yet.

Either way

Seen from behind, as today. The off hand is the left circle of the pair and the main hand the right one.

One item in both hands. The off hand shows no picture and no ring of its own; the item and its condition are on the main hand. It slides back out when the item leaves a hand.

Dragging. Onto the main hand's circle puts the item in the main hand; onto the peeking off hand, in the off hand.

Controller. The belt still starts on the first slot. At the left end, Left from the first slot reaches the main hand, then the off hand. At the right end, Right from the last slot reaches the off hand, then the main hand. The peeking off hand is still a stop.

Sizes. The belt grows 4 units taller, to the main hand's 56; the slots and the strap stay centred on it. The off hand's picture is 28 units, as a slot's is.

Why the left end now. The belt is placed by its buckle, so at the left end the off hand can slide behind without moving anything else; the belt only ends sooner. At the right end, the main hand keeps its place but a gap opens beside the strap every time an item is held in both hands. Closing that gap would move the main hand, the ammo pouch and the watch instead.
