ZED-UI · VEHICLE DASHBOARD v2 · INSTRUMENT CLUSTER · LUA-FLAT PREVIEW · S123 · FOR DIRECTOR REVIEW

Replaces the S123 first cut (flat tiles). Direction: 1980s to 1993 instrument clusters, drawn the way the live mod draws (rects and bitmap text only: needles are rect runs, arcs are rect dots, bar graphs are drawSegBar, buttons are the radio chassis bevel atoms). The outer frame is the car's interior material (same cabin table as the code: walnut + tan, saddle leather, red leather, black plastic, slate cloth, patrol black). The bezel is always matte black plastic, like a real cluster. Two gauge styles per model era: digital (VFD or LCD segments: luxury and modern cars) and analog (needle dials: everything else). Hardware controls on the right follow Visual Catalog v2.0 O22 (LED push buttons, rocker, keyhole) and M38 to M41. Everything keeps the verified S123 plumbing: state readers, vanilla verbs, D-pad across the controls, A toggles, B back to driving, passengers read-only.
