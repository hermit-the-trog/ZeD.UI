Hands and the belt

Vanilla shows what you hold as two grey circles in the top-left corner and what hangs on you as a bar of numbered squares at the bottom; a controller player gets no bar at all. Here both become one HUD prop, the belt, a 1993 piece in the line of the CaZio watch and the dashboard: your hands as a pair of work gloves holding what you carry, a brass buckle, then the belt with every slot your gear gives you, each drawn as the thing it hangs on (a back sling, leather frogs, holsters, webbing), its key on a brass tag. The Character window's Equip tab manages it. Switch the scenes above.

Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDOne belt on the HUD for the hands and the hotbar. Vanilla splits them: two hand circles in the top-left corner and a plain bar at the bottom. Here they become one prop at the bottom centre, where the hotbar sat: your hands on the left, a brass buckle, then the belt. In single player the vanilla left-edge column goes entirely (its window buttons already went).
  2. RECOMMENDEach slot is the thing it hangs on. Back is a sling, Belt Left and Belt Right are leather frogs, the holsters (right, left, shoulder, ankle) are holsters, webbing is a canvas pouch, a backpack bottom is a pair of bedroll straps. Its name is stamped under it, always visible (vanilla shows it only on hover). The key that draws it sits on a brass tag above: the key you bound, not the slot's position as vanilla prints. Slots past the eighth have no key, as in vanilla. Slots come and go with what you wear and keep their numbers (vanilla's rule).
  3. RECOMMENDHands are a pair of work gloves. What you hold rests on the palm: PRIMARY on the right glove, SECONDARY on the left. A two-handed weapon lies across both under BOTH HANDS (vanilla draws it twice). An item drawn from the belt leaves its outline in the slot with IN HAND; a flashlight or walkie that is on shows a lit lamp. The Hands control compares plain leather pads.
  4. RECOMMENDEvery item shows its condition. A wear bar under each item, in a hand or on the belt, in the inventory rows' colours (green, amber below 60%, red below 30%): you see the knife going blunt before you draw it. Vanilla shows condition only in the tooltip.
  5. RECOMMENDVanilla's controls, plus the missing ones. A number key tapped draws the item or puts it back; held, it opens what fits that slot (instead of vanilla's radial), with REMOVE. Clicking a slot does what its key does; right-click opens the item's menu with Attach. Dragging from your bags onto a slot attaches it: the slots that fit light up, the rest dim, and an occupied one says what it replaces. Dragging onto a glove equips it in that hand; between the gloves equips a two-handed item in both. New: dragging an item off the belt into your bags detaches it, and dragging it from a slot to a glove draws it.
  6. RECOMMENDControllers get the belt. Vanilla hides the hotbar from controller players and gives them the D-pad Left wheel (every weapon in the bags and the best light). Here D-pad Left focuses the belt: left and right walk the gloves and slots, A draws or puts back, X opens the slot's list, B leaves. Above the belt, IN YOUR BAGS carries the rest of the old wheel: the other weapons and the best light.
  7. RECOMMENDThe Character window manages it. The Equip tab's HELD + BELT group lists both hands and every belt slot with its key, what fills it and which worn item gives the slot, with ATTACH (what fits) and DETACH. Items on the belt carry a BELT n tag in the inventory lists, where vanilla puts its small hotbar icon.
  8. YOUR CALLA round counter for the gun in hand. Vanilla shows the rounds only in the gun's tooltip; hardcore players count their shots. Option: a small brass counter beside the primary glove (11 + 1 of 15). Leaving it out keeps vanilla's stakes. Try it with the Round counter control.
  9. RECOMMENDWhere it shows. It is hidden while you sit in a vehicle, because the dashboard takes the bottom centre (vanilla hides the bar only for the driver); the number keys keep working. Toggle UI hides it too. In split screen it belongs to player one, as in vanilla. In multiplayer the vanilla column keeps only its server buttons (safety, user, admin, war) until a multiplayer pass; Debug stays under -debug.
  10. Vanilla bugs fixed on the way. The bar prints the slot's position, so rebound keys show the wrong numbers. A two-handed weapon is drawn in both hand circles. Controller players get no bar. The hand slots' "Primary" and "Secondary" labels never show. Nothing can be dragged off the bar.
