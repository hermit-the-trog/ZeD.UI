Skills: how much of each skill is kept in the journal

Skill Recovery Journal (4 million subscribers) draws a thin colour strip under each of the game's skill bars: the part of the skill that came from the character's job and traits, the part that cannot be written down (from TV and tapes), the part recorded in a journal, and the part not yet written. Its tooltip gives the numbers. ZeD's Skills tab draws its own rows, so none of this shows today. This puts the strip under each ZeD row, a short key at the top of the tab, and the numbers in the row's hover card. The numbers follow the mod's own sums; the character is a Carpenter, ten days in.

Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDOnly when the mod is on. Nothing changes in the Skills tab without Skill Recovery Journal. With it, ZeD reads the mod's own four numbers per skill (the same sums its strip uses) and draws them in ZeD's colours and type. ZeD writes nothing and keeps no copy; the journal item and its Transcribe Into Journal and Read entries keep working from the item menu as they do today.
  2. RECOMMENDA 3 px strip in its own lane under each row, not over the row's fill. The row's fill already means "progress in this level" (green, then yellow, then red near the next level); the strip sits below it so the two never mix. The row grows by 4 px. Skills the mod does not record (Fitness and Strength by default, and any group its sandbox options turn off) and skills at level 0 get no strip, as in the mod.
  3. RECOMMENDStrip length like the mod: how far the skill has come (level 5 and a bit fills just over half the row), split by share of XP. Full row, as the other button shows, reads more evenly but loses the sense of size: a level 1 skill and a level 9 skill look the same.
  4. RECOMMENDThe mod's words and colours. Starting Level, Unrecoverable XP, Recoverable XP, Untranscribed XP come from the mod's own text (translated into its 20 languages), in its grey, orange, green and red, so players who know the mod read the strip at once. One short key line at the top of the tab, in the tab's scroll (no strip of its own), and the numbers in the row's hover card.
  5. RECOMMENDThe key line says whether a Bound Journal is on the character, whose it is and where (main inventory or a bag). The strip itself does not change: the mod keeps the record with the character, not in the item, so what was written stays counted wherever the journal is. Only the "not yet written" part asks for the journal; with none on the character, the line says so.
  6. RECOMMENDPinned skills in the title bar stay text only (as now: Carpentry - Lv.5 - 600/3000). A 2 px strip under a pin is shown in the Pinned scene; at 1080p it is too small to read and crowds the title.
  7. YOUR CALLNot yet written: solid red like the mod, or hatched red. Solid red sits next to the red row fill that means "almost the next level" and can read as the same thing. Hatched keeps the mod's colour but looks unfinished, which is what it is. Recommend solid (the mod's own look) unless the red rows bother you in play.
  8. YOUR CALLA Transcribe button in the key line when a journal is on the character, running the mod's own Transcribe Into Journal. Saves opening the item menu, but it is a shortcut the mod itself puts only on the item. Not shown; recommend leaving it on the item.
  9. LIMITTwo places only. ZeD shows skills in two places: the Skills tab's two columns and the two pinned skills in the Character window's title bar. Collapsing the tab's header hides the grid and the strips with it, as today.
  10. Controller: each skill row is already a stop; on focus the hover card shows beside the row (the Controller scene). A still pins the skill.
