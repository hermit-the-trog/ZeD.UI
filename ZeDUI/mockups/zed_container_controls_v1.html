Container Buttons

Vanilla B42 put a row of buttons on its inventory windows: on yours, Transfer All and two picture buttons that move only the same type of thing (into the container on show, or into every nearby container that already holds that type); on the other side, Take All, Take Same Type, Move To Floor, and the container's own buttons (a stove's Turn On and Settings, a washer's switch, a mannequin's Wear All, a car trunk's Close and Lock, a campfire's Light and Put Out, a trash can's Delete All). None of them drops or splits a stack; they are bulk moves. Here they are in the ZeD Character and World windows: one row of worded buttons in each window's list, for the container that list shows. The backdrop is a real 1080p capture (the pickup's truck bed open in the World window, a few nails and a plank in your bag); Look: Vanilla shows vanilla's two windows on the same truck bed.

Every container

The World window's list for each kind of container vanilla gives buttons to, at the window's usual width (shown at 150%). The moves come first; the container's own buttons sit at the right after a divider, and drop to a second row when both will not fit.

Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDYes, ZeD gets them. The item menu moves one thing or a selection; these move a whole container, or just what matches, in one click, and players who learned them in vanilla will look for them.
  2. RECOMMENDWords, not pictures. Vanilla's two "same type" buttons are small arrow pictures that only explain themselves in a tooltip. ZeD names them: SAME TYPE (into the container on show, or out of it in the World window) and SAME TYPE, ALL NEARBY (each thing goes into whichever nearby container already holds its type). The rest keep vanilla's words: TRANSFER ALL, TAKE ALL, TO FLOOR.
  3. RECOMMENDThe row sits under the container's heading, across the list only (not the tab rail or the container strip), the same place the fire and fluid cards keep their buttons. It belongs to the container on show and changes with it.
  4. YOUR CALLOr at the bottom of the list, where vanilla has it (the Row control above). Closer to vanilla, but a long list's last rows end up beside the buttons.
  5. RECOMMENDPointing at a SAME TYPE button marks the rows it would move (the light accent fill). Vanilla does the same; it's the only way to know before you click.
  6. RECOMMENDThe container's own buttons run vanilla's own code, so every rule stays vanilla's: the stove's only with power, Delete All only where the server allows it, the trunk's only when you stand at its lid. Buttons that other mods add to vanilla's row show up here too, as plain worded buttons. Where ZeD already has a fuller view, the button opens it: the stove's SETTINGS opens the oven panel in the Craft tab, the campfire's ADD FUEL opens the fire card's fuel list.
  7. RECOMMENDDELETE ALL asks first, in the row itself. Vanilla asks with a pop-up; here the row becomes the question, with DELETE ALL where you just clicked it.
  8. Controller: the row's buttons are stops like any other; the D-pad reaches them from the list (up from the first row), A presses, and the footer's prompts name the button.
