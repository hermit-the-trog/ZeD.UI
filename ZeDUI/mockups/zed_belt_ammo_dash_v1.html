Ammo on the belt, condition on the dash lamps

Two small native pieces that replace what Common Sense and Condition On Dash draw on vanilla screens ZeD hides. On the belt: with a gun in the main hand, the rounds in the gun and the rounds or magazines you carry, shown three ways for you to pick from. On the dash: the battery, engine and heater lamps take the part's condition, and pointing at a lamp says the part and its condition. Both use only what the game already knows, so they work without either mod. Real art: the belt's strap, buckle and round pictures and the DASH ELITE plate with its own lamp pixels; the item pictures are the game's.

Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDAmmo shown as a box beside the main hand (B). It reads as one more pouch on the belt: the ammo's own picture (the magazine for a pistol, the shell for a shotgun), the rounds in the gun in large figures, what you carry under it, and a bar for how full the gun is. The counter on the hand (A) is smaller but crowds the condition ring; the pips (C) are the quickest read in a fight but cannot say how much you carry, and 30-round rifles would need a second row.
  2. RECOMMEND"Carried" counts what a reload can use. For a gun with magazines: the loaded magazines you carry, then the loose rounds. For a shotgun or a revolver: the loose rounds. Unopened boxes are named but not added in ("+1 box"), since the game will not reload from a box. Bags count as carried, as the game's own reload does. The full breakdown (each magazine's rounds) is in the hover text.
  3. RECOMMENDThe count takes the belt's wear colours: normal text over half full, amber over a quarter, red under, and the word EMPTY at nothing (Common Sense uses white, yellow, red and dark red). A chambered round shows as "+1", as gun owners write it.
  4. RECOMMENDDash lamps light only when something is wrong (Warning only). A real dash: dark while the part is fine, amber when it is worn (under 60%), red when poor (under 30%), red and blinking under 10%. It keeps the dash calm and the colours the belt already uses. The battery lamp follows whichever is weaker, its charge or its condition. The engine lamp moves from today's 40% to the same 60 and 30 steps. Always tinted (Common Sense's way: every lamp glows green, amber or red while the engine runs) is one switch away.
  5. RECOMMENDHover text as a ZeD chip above the lamp, the same chip the belt uses for a slot's name: the part, its condition and, for the battery, its charge. The other choice prints it in the dash's own pixel letters along the top edge, where the controller prompts go; that is more in the world but small, and it would fight the prompts.
  6. YOUR CALLHeadlights too? Condition On Dash also gives each headlight's condition. Pointing at the headlight lamp could say "Headlights | left 85% | right 40%" (shown in the worn car). Recommend yes, it costs nothing.
  7. YOUR CALLController: the lamps are not on the dash's controller path today. Recommend leaving them off it and adding the conditions to the Vehicle tab's part list instead, which the controller already reaches.
  8. LIMITThe heater lamp only shows while the heater runs, as in Common Sense: on most plates the heater has no lamp of its own, only the light on its button (on the DASH ELITE, the light over AUTO). Plates without a heater light get the hover text on the heater button only.
