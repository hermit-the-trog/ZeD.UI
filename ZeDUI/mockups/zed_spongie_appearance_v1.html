SPONGIE'S CHARACTER CUSTOMISATION | ZED SUPPORT V1

Spongie's faces and details become an Appearance tab in the Character window, and rows on the Looks screen for a new character. The mod's own lists, rules and saving run underneath; ZeD draws them. Click tiles, rows, Save, Shave, the arrows under the figure. With "Controller" on, the arrow keys move the focus and Enter is A.

Where it opens

  • Character window, Appearance tab, after Identity (a mirror). It is there only while Spongie's Character Customisation is on; without the mod ZeD runs exactly as now.
  • First time in an old save, the mod asks for a look: ZeD opens the Character window on this tab instead of the mod's window. The game is paused, as the mod does it, and only Save is offered. Closing the window un-pauses; the mod asks again next time the save loads.
  • New character: FACE and DETAILS rows on ZeD's Looks screen (the mod's Change Appearance button sits on the game's screen, which ZeD hides). Body hair shows for women too, as the mod has it. Random rolls a face and details, as the mod does.
  • The D-pad wheel's Tabs slice lists the tab like any other.

What stays the mod's

  • Which faces and details there are: the mod's own list plus every add-on pack (Spongie's Character Retexture add-on brings six faces for each sex). The picture under each tile is the mod's camera for that option.
  • The server's rules: what may change in game (the mod's default is nothing), the Appearance Changing Spiffo and the Appearance Unlocking Bear (a padlock marks options it unlocks), the eye mark on options that change vision.
  • Saving to the character, stubble and body hair growing back, the shave actions (they need a razor and take a hat, top or trousers off first), multiplayer.
  • Its words: Face, Details, Shave, "Changing faces is unavailable", "Requires a Razor".

Mouse and controller

  • Click a face to wear it, a detail to add or take it off. The figure shows the picks; Save puts them on the character, Cancel goes back.
  • Hovering or focusing a tile turns the figure's camera to it (a body scar shows the torso). Drag the figure or use the arrows to turn it; + and - zoom.
  • Controller: the D-pad moves between tiles, rows and buttons, A picks, B goes back to the tab strip. The picks wait until you Save or Cancel; leaving the tab keeps them.
  • Pictures or List: the switch in the tab's header, like the inventory's list and grid. List is quicker with many packs on.

For the next mod

  • Each supported mod gets one ZeD file, loaded only when that mod is on. The tab names the mod under its header, as here.
  • A line in the Compatibility post: "ZeD-GUI shows its faces and details in the Character window's Appearance tab and on the Looks screen."
