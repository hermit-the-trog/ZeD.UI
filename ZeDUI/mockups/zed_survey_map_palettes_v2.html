Label contrast

What ZeD can set

  • The fill of each of the eight building uses, and plain buildings.
  • Trails, small streets, main roads, the railway, forest, water and the ground.
  • Each label kind's color: streets, buildings, places, towns, forest and water names.
  • The game's own color blind patterns, already a ZeD setting (Options, Status colors).

What stays the game's

  • Where every road and building is, and every name. Those are the map's data.
  • Road widths: roads are filled shapes, not lines.
  • The printed legend sheets placed on the map itself (Legend.png) are pictures in the game's colors. With a ZeD palette they would be hidden, and ZeD's Key (right) shows the palette in use.

From your references

  • Carried over: paper white ground, woodland tint, blue water with blue names, red highways, a black railway with ties in the key, a symbol sheet for the key, and a quadrangle's margin block with a scale bar (drawn by ZeD over the map).
  • Patterns like the symbol sheet's marsh and orchard can be the game's texture fills, the same way its color blind patterns work.
  • Not possible: roads drawn as double lines or with an edge, and dashed trails. Roads are filled shapes, one color each. Contour lines: the game's map has no heights.

How to read this page

The block is drawn here in the browser to compare colors. The real map comes from the game's data. The patterns are stand-ins drawn here: in the game they are its own pattern pictures. The label contrast list measures each label against what it sits on; 4.5 to 1 is the floor for small text.
