Reading order. Top to bottom: label tells you the ACTION, strip shows the verb icons, target card names the SUBJECT (the item being acted on), balloon describes the CONSEQUENCE. The strip literally points at the item it's operating on. Balloon is static-centered under the target card — it doesn't chase selection horizontally anymore, only its contents update.

Action → Subject → Consequence

The layout reads like a sentence. Eat (verb, from the label). Beans (Canned) (object, from the target card). Opens portion submenu (outcome, from the balloon). Top-down flow matches how players think about actions — pick the verb, see what it's for, see what happens.

Static balloon

Previously the balloon tracked horizontally with selection. Now it's anchored under the target card and doesn't move — only its content updates. Less motion, less chrome chasing the eye around. The target card is the anchor that holds the whole assembly together visually.

Visual loop · unchanged

Triple-render carousel still operates when #items > 10. End to the left of start, start to the right of end. Silent settle after animation. All the scroll behavior rides underneath the new layout — nothing changed in the strip itself.
