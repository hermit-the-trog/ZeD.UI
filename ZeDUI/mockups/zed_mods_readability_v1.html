Mods: telling on from off, an Off tab, Hide incompatible, a Simple view and folder labels

A player with a long list said every row looks the same. Here mods that are on sit on a darker solid band, and an off mod's name and icon dim. The accent stays for the row you chose, hover and the controller. Click the tabs, Hide incompatible, the rows and the label swatches; they work.

Scene

What changes

  • On and off. On rows: a solid band, darker than the list, and the name in the heading colour. Off rows: the list's own ground, the name and icon dimmed. In Light the band is a shade darker than the page as well; in High Contrast it is black against the grey list. Not colour alone: the checkbox, the band and the name's brightness all say it.
  • Off tab beside On, with its count. Like the others it hides when it would be empty.
  • Hide incompatible by Find: mods the game can't run (made for another version, or needing a mod that isn't installed) leave the list. The count of hidden ones sits beside the box. Kept between visits.
  • The tab is remembered when the screen closes and reopens (not drawn: it opens on the tab you left).
  • Simple view (ZeD setup, Full or simple group, like the survival guide): one line a mod, the checkbox, the name and one red mark when something is wrong. No IDs, no chips; the pane shows About only. Folders, separators and the load order tools stay in Full.
  • Labels. A folder or separator takes a label colour. The folder's own picture is tinted (its outline in the colour, a light fill), and a separator's name and rule. Nothing on the row's edge. The picker is the Maps tab's swatch row, with None first.
