Crafting stations

Stations become part of the Craft tab. A Station selector sits beside the category picker: Hand is crafting as today, and every station within reach is listed under it. Most stations are benches (forges, furnaces, kilns, pottery wheel, looms, mills, charcoal burners), which are simply the recipe list for that station, with fuel as an ingredient. Drying racks are machines with slots, start and stop. The oven and microwave are appliances, drawn as physical 1993 control panels the way the radio drawer is. Switch the states above the window.

Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDStations live in the Craft tab, not in a new tab. They are the same recipe list and card with a different source, so the Station selector switches the source. The selector lists every usable station within four tiles (vanilla's reach) with its distance. Choosing one binds the tab to it, and walking away drops back to Hand.
  2. RECOMMENDEvery vanilla way in lands here. These are the station option in the world right-click menu, the controller Interact prompt, and clicking a big multi-tile station. Each opens the World window's Craft tab already bound to that station. The vanilla station window never shows. Only one player can use a station at a time, as in vanilla; a station in use by someone else shows a lock.
  3. RECOMMENDHand mode tells you when a recipe needs a station. Today the Craft tab can show a kiln recipe as craftable by hand, which is wrong. In Hand mode, station recipes keep their place in the list with the station's name as a tag and a hollow dot. Their card says which station they need and how far away the nearest one is, with a button that binds the tab to it.
  4. RECOMMENDMachines get a full-width panel. A drying rack needs room for its 20 input slots, the drying progress, start and stop, its output slots and the items you can add. So the recipe list folds into a recipe dropdown and the slots take the drawer. Each slot shows its own progress bar.
  5. RECOMMENDAppliances are hardware. The oven and microwave are 1993 objects, so their controls are a physical panel: knobs for temperature and timer, a green readout, a power lamp and an ON / OFF key. Below the panel, "In the oven" lists what is cooking with the item card's cooking band, including the burning warning. The oven also gets the °F / °C switch vanilla has, and its knobs move one stop per D-pad press. The alternative is plain steppers like the rest of the window.
  6. Fuel. Forges, furnaces and kilns have no fuel gauge in vanilla; fuel is an ingredient of each recipe. The card marks those ingredients with a FUEL tag rather than inventing a fuel system.
  7. Also folded in. The vanilla hand-craft window (its keybind and the HUD) and the build window already have ZeD tabs. Their remaining entry points get hooked to open the Craft and Build tabs, so no vanilla crafting window can appear.
