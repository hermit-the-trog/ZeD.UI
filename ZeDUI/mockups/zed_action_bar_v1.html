Action Bar v1

Every timed action (reading, eating, ripping sheets, moving items, chopping) puts a progress bar over your character's head. Vanilla's is a plain bar the engine draws, sized by Options > Action Progress Bar Size (100 to 400%), and it restarts for every item in a queue. ZeD would switch vanilla's off for each action as it starts (vanilla already does this for foraging and exercise) and draw its own in the same spot. Four options below, each over a real game frame at three times its size, square pixels, so you are looking at what a 1080p screen shows, magnified. The True size row shows them unmagnified.

The four options, running

Each one loops the same sequence: walk to the book, read it, finish. Then a queue of six rips, then resting until you get up.

Every state

The same six moments in each option. Frozen at an interesting point; the patterns that move, move.

True size

Unmagnified, at vanilla's default 100% and at 200%, a queue of 12 at the 4th. B is shown in both faces: the HUD's 5 × 7 pixel letters and the card's F1. If your screen is high density this shows them as a 1080p screen would.

Try it

Pick an option, an action, a theme, an accent and vanilla's size setting. START runs it; STOP interrupts it.

Decisions for you

  1. RECOMMENDB, the tag, in the 5 × 7 pixel letters. It is the item card's Progress Band in small (label in the accent, number on the right, fill under it), lettered like the dash and the watch, so it reads as part of the HUD. The label answers what your character is doing when a long queue runs while you look elsewhere. Where it could fail: it is still the biggest of the four (about 52 × 17 at 100%, twice that at 200%), and words over a fight may feel heavy. If it does, C, the dial, is the fallback: no words, the action's icon, a smaller footprint. The tag in the card's own F1 face is out: 87 to 112 wide at 100% (True size).
  2. RECOMMENDA queue reads as one job. Ripping twelve sheets fills one bar from empty to full with "4/12" (B), or one tick per item (A, C, D), instead of vanilla's bar restarting twelve times.
  3. RECOMMENDNo made-up numbers. Actions with no end (resting, sitting, chopping until the tree falls, walking there first) get a moving pattern, never a percentage.
  4. RECOMMENDFinishing flashes the positive colour for a moment; stopping goes grey, not red. Stopping is nearly always your own choice (you moved, you pressed a key); red would cry wolf. Colour carries the change; the shape stays still.
  5. YOUR CALLSize. Follow vanilla's Action Progress Bar Size option on top of ZeD's UI scale (shown here), or give ZeD its own setting in the settings work.
  6. YOUR CALLOther players in multiplayer (vanilla's "Show overhead progress bars for all player actions"): the same design, dimmed, or the bare bar (A) for others so only your own carries words.
  7. YOUR CALLThe 5 × 7 face has no lowercase and no %: the queue reads "4/12", and this mockup adds a % in the face's style. Approve the glyph or swap it for the 3 × 5 face's own.
  8. TO PROVEIn game before building: switching vanilla's bar off as each action starts (the call exists; vanilla uses it for foraging and exercise, which keep no bar in ZeD too), placing ours on the head as the camera zooms and the character moves, and a label and icon for every action. ZeD has labels for some actions and icons for about 530 menu entries; an action with no match (a mod's, say) falls back to "WORKING" and a plain gear.
