# This Is Your Life: what it adds, and what support would take (Nov 2 2026)

This is a short read for the director's decision (punch list item 64). It comes from the mod's code (Workshop 3773911887, version 0.1.0); it was not run with ZeD.

## What it adds

1. **Hometown postcards.** At the start of a new game it replaces the game's town list with a pile of postcards, or a map board, showing 11 Kentucky towns. You pick a town and start in a house there.
2. **Origin traits.** Each town gives one free trait. Examples:
   - more XP in one skill: Riverside fishing, Muldraugh mechanics, Dixie cooking
   - Rosewood's unhappiness builds slower
   - less stress at home

   Your home area also starts already seen on the map.
3. **Scripted starts, in two kinds.**
   - **Partner starts** are off by default. You start married. Your partner is a zombie nearby wearing a wedding ring, with a shovel by a grave spot, and your unhappiness starts at its peak and fades over a month.
   - **Five "poster stories"** are not behind a switch and are single player only. Each sets your name, traits and outfit, places zombies, bodies and notes, and hands you items. Two of them give you a repaired car.
4. **Its own creator pages**: occupation, traits and looks, drawn on diner-table and newspaper art.
5. **In-game extras:** a survivor's journal that is kept after death, milestone pop-ups and a health log.

## How it meets ZeD

ZeD's own Spawn, Occupation and Looks windows drive the game's creator directly. This Is Your Life changes the game's creator screens in place and puts its own on top. Under ZeD none of its screens appear, so no hometown is picked, and the origin trait and its effects never start. ZeD would keep its own creator pages in any case; the mod's art pages can't be matched.

## What support would take

- **Small to medium (half a day to a day): a ZeD-style hometown picker.** It would list the 11 towns with their trait and blurb. Picking one would hand the choice to the mod, and its traits, map reveal, journal and pop-ups would then work as written. The partner-start switch would pass through as well.
- **Large (days): the mod's own look inside ZeD.** That would mean the postcards and map board, or the poster stories.
- **Multiplayer** needs its own pass.

## Against ZeD's standing rules

- The origin traits are mild.
- Partner starts give only a few small story items.
- The poster stories hand out a weapon, food and, in two of them, a working car. A free working car is the one part close to "makes the game easier", so it's the director's call whether ZeD would carry the poster stories at all.

## For the director to decide

- (a) Support the hometowns and origin traits only, with a ZeD-style picker: about a day.
- (b) Leave it listed as not working with ZeD.

Either way the poster stories stay out unless the director says otherwise.
