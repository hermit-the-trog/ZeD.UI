78 pictures in upload order, drafted from the research: the first five sell the mod, then the book follows a run from the main menu to the long haul. Each card sketches the proposed spread: wider crops, and a full-page picture on about one spread in five.

Shots30 re-crop as they are, 31 re-crop but sit in a plain test place, 13 retakes, 4 new
Capture standard4K, UI scale 1.75x, Dark + PZ Teal, list view, font 19px, unless a card says otherwise
Notes today303 ink notes and 101 sticky notes across the 63 chapters; 58 sticky notes repeat the ink or caption beside them
Crops today191 pictures; the middle one shows 22% of the screen across, and 173 show under 40%. One shows the whole screen.
Crop ruleA picture shows the window with the game around it and the character in frame: at least 45% of the screen wide, printed up to 800 px. All 160 captures are full 4K frames, and most already have the game open under the windows, so this is mostly a re-crop. A tight crop is allowed only as a small closer look beside a wide one.
Feature spreads13 of the 63 chapters, about one in five: text and at most one small picture on one page, one picture filling the facing page (half the 4K screen at full height).
Notes ruleCaption says what the figure is. Ink points at one detail, four a page at most. Sticky gives one takeaway and never repeats the ink.
