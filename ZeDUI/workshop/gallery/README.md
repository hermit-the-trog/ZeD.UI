# Workshop gallery: "Bitchin' znix Programs"

The gallery pictures are pages from a used 1993-style programming book, marked up by the man who wrote the kernel:
struck-out print, his handwriting, tape, post-its, dog-ears. Drawn in the mod's pixel art: a 480 x 270 grid, fixed
palette, ordered dither, scaled 4x (nearest) to 1920 x 1080 (16:9, director's call, Oct 30 2026). The pictures carry
no captions; the handwritten notes do the explaining. The director supplies the jokes and where they go; the printed
text stays dry. Headings on every slide are minimal: one plain heading, no eyebrows, accents or marks.
