# Gallery review, Nov 1 2026 (QA)

The posted set is the book (`upload/00_cover.jpg`, `01_hero.jpg` to `60_machine_plates.jpg`, built Oct 31 from the
t14 captures). Every page was checked for three faults the director named: an arrow that does not land on the thing
its note names; a named thing that is out of frame; and words that defer ("scrolls for the rest", "below") instead of
showing. Six reviewers, ten pages each, judged by the picture. Findings only; nothing was changed.

The old flat cards in `shots/out` are from Oct 28 and no longer match the captures. Ignore them.

## Two causes behind most of it

1. **The book builder stops every arrow short and drops short ones.** In `book_shots.py` (the arrow step, about line
   210): the arrowhead is drawn 14 px before its point, and a note that sits within 24 px of its point gets no arrow at
   all. So a correct point still leaves the head floating on the window border or the paper beside it (pages 1, 5, 12,
   15, 31, 32, 51-53, 57, 60 and more), and pages 2, 5, 14, 21, 25 and 49 have notes with no arrow. Two numbers to
   change, then every page is nearer right before any point is moved. Design's file (work account).
2. **The points were set on the t13 captures; the book uses t14.** Where a window's layout changed in between, the
   arrows land on what used to be there: Foraging (29: the Search button and stance marks moved to the bottom row,
   the category pop-up to the top), Journal (3), Thermal (24: the view pop-up replaced the radio buttons), Health
   (23: the exercise pop-up), the car radio (35: a different radio in the capture), the controller highlight (17:
   grid view, mark out of the crop), the map inset (4: still the t13 file). Every one of those needs its points set
   again on the t14 picture, or a retake.
