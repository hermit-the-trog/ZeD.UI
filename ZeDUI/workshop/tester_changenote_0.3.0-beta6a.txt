0.3.0-beta6a: hotfix for 0.3.0-beta6.

Servers
- A dedicated server no longer writes a font error to its log every few seconds.
- Safehouse invitations stay until they're answered, and one sent while you were offline now shows.
- A muted player can no longer talk through /all, /s, /y or /w. A mute also stops /roll and /card.
- In safe mode, safehouse and faction invitations can be answered in the game's own pop-ups.
- Others can't tow now works on 42.21 as well.
- Check stats on a Player card opens the game's stats window, and a ticket opened from a Player card no longer wipes the ticket you came from.
- Role names and ban targets take one line. The ticket queue keeps a Refresh if an answer is lost.
- Answering a second safehouse invitation while the first is on its way now waits, so it can't trip the game's anti-cheat.
- Tag colors show only to the faction's owner, the one person the game takes them from.

Cards
- Clothing cards show the real bite, scratch and bullet protection, neck, water, wind and condition.
- Book cards show Read and Progress, and the Reading band follows the book you're reading.
- The Surface badge is back on Crafting and Building. Journal recipes show their description again.
- Kills by weapon count in the Journal again, one for each kill.
- Pens show their roof percentage.

Windows
- The Animals tab's Ranch page keeps its tab icons and footer.
- Bags and columns: Main on Loot after viewing a bag from another tab shows the main list. Shrinking a window with main hidden keeps a column.
- A picked chat line keeps its buttons while any of the line is in view.
- Vanilla Access: anyone coming straight from beta 5 stays on the preset.

Controller
- With Hold and Flick-release, a slice no longer fires without being chosen. The stick has to start centered.
- A pad button aborts a Restock run.
- The light switch search looks through windows and glass doors.

Mods screen
- A mod switched off on the default list stops running through ZeD Picks at Accept.
- Load orders and chords are checked as they're written, with a spare kept. ZeD Picks now requires ZeD.

Known issues
- Spongie's Character Customization: the folds on the Appearance tab haven't been played with the mod on yet. The Looks screen has. If the tab misbehaves, say so in Bug reports.
- The join rules and timed bans are enforced by the player's own game, so a modified game can get past them.
- Split-screen: a second local player shares the first player's windows.
- Death then Restart uses the game's character screens.
