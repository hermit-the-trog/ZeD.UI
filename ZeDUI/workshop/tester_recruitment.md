# Tester recruitment (0.2.0-t1)

## Post (Discord, Reddit, forums)

Title: Looking for testers: ZeD-GUI, an interface mod for Build 42

ZeD-GUI replaces the inventory, the character and loot windows, the HUD and the menus for Build 42. It works with a controller and with a mouse and keyboard. A private test build is on the Workshop, and I am looking for a small group of testers before the public release.

What testers do:
- Play Build 42 (42.21) with ZeD-GUI on, in a new game or a copy of a save.
- Report problems in the pinned Bug reports discussion on the Workshop page, with console.txt.
- Do not share the link, the files or videos until the public release.

I am looking for a mix of:
- Controller players and mouse and keyboard players.
- Screens at 1080p, 1440p and 4K.
- Players who run other mods, and players who play without mods.
- A few people who can try it on a multiplayer server.

To apply, reply or send a message with:
1. Your Steam name.
2. Your screen resolution.
3. Controller, or mouse and keyboard.
4. The mods you usually play with.
5. About how much you have played Build 42.

## Message to accepted testers

Thanks for testing ZeD-GUI. The test build is here: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3824968485

1. Open the link while signed in to Steam and press Subscribe.
2. Start Project Zomboid (Build 42). Main menu, Mods: turn on ZeD-GUI. Then quit the game and start it again: ZeD-GUI loads its fonts when the game starts.
3. Start a new game, or play a copy of a save.
4. Report problems in the pinned Bug reports discussion on the Workshop page. Include the version (0.2.0-t1), your mod list, what you did, what you expected and what happened, and your console.txt (C:\Users\(you)\Zomboid\console.txt on Windows). The game replaces console.txt each time it starts, so copy it before you restart.
5. If ZeD-GUI stops you from playing, turn on Options, ZeD setup, Safe mode, and say so in your report.

This is a private test build. Please do not share the link, the files or videos before the public release.
