TITLE: Compatibility with other mods

[h1]Compatibility with other mods[/h1]
This thread keeps the list of mods tested with ZeD-GUI, graded by how well they work together.

[h2]How the mods were tested[/h2]
[list]
[*]Oct 24 2026, ZeD-GUI 0.2.0-t3 and the fixes below (0.2.0-t4), Project Zomboid 42.21, single player, a 1920x1080 window.
[*]124 Workshop items (128 mods) on at once, ZeD-GUI loaded after them: a save loaded with all of them, then ZeD-GUI's own tests ran (66 of 73 before the game ran out of memory). 62 of those 66 passed as they do without other mods.
[*]Every error in the game's log was checked for the mod that made it.
[*]Each mod's own features were [b]not[/b] tried one by one. A note marked [i](expected)[/i] comes from how the mod works, not from a test.
[/list]

[h2]Grades[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]A[/b] | Works. Loads with ZeD-GUI, no errors of its own, ZeD-GUI's tests pass with it on.
[*][b]B[/b] | Works, part of it does not show: it adds to a game screen that ZeD-GUI replaces.
[*][b]C[/b] | Does the same job as ZeD-GUI. Keep one of the two.
[*][b]D[/b] | Clashes: with both on, something happens twice or one undoes the other.
[*][b]F[/b] | Does not work with ZeD-GUI.
[*][b]X[/b] | Broken on 42.21 by itself, with or without ZeD-GUI.
[/list]
No mod tested so far is D or F.

[h2]Found and fixed, in the next test build (0.2.0-t4)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Notes that mods open at game start (Horse Mod, Bandits, Companion Dogs) go into ZeD-GUI's new Messages window instead of covering the game, and a mod that pauses the game for its note no longer leaves it paused.
[*]Another mod's error while the main screen builds (Change Sandbox Options has two on 42.21) left the game without the options screen or ZeD-GUI's pause window. ZeD-GUI now sets such a key binding to none, catches a mod's options page that fails, and builds its menu even when another mod's step fails.
[*]A watch worn in a mod's own watch slot (Pack Mule) did not show on the belt. The belt now finds the watch wherever it is worn.
[/list]

[h2]Broken on 42.21 by themselves (X)[/h2]
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2894296454]Change Sandbox Options[/url] by star: Two errors of its own on 42.21: a key binding with no key (it stopped the game's options screen), and a main-screen step that looks for a quit button the game no longer makes (it stopped ZeD's pause window). ZeD-GUI 0.2.0-t4 works around both. ZeD-GUI 0.2.0-t4 also has its own Sandbox options in the pause window (Options, ZeD setup, Sandbox; off by default): use one.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2875848298]Common Sense[/url] by Braven: Stops the world from loading on 42.21: its recipes ask for a fluid, Alcohol, that the game no longer has. Not ZeD.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2737665235]Ladders!?[/url] by co`: Its ladder code errors as the world loads (setLadderClimbingFlags). Not ZeD.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3403180543]Week One NPC[/url] by Slayer: Its scheduler errors once a game minute in an existing save (generateSchedule). ZeD is not in the error.
[/list]

[b]To add a mod to the list[/b], reply with the mod's name, its Workshop link, and what happened. The grades are in the next posts; mods ZeD-GUI does the job of, and their makers, are in the last one.
