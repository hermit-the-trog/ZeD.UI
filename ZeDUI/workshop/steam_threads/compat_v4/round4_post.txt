Round 4 (Oct 29 2026, build 0.2.0-t11): new support, and six more mods tested.

Supported (ZeD has code for the mod):
- Easy Drop'n'Loot: its Transfer category and Loot category buttons are in ZeD's container buttons row, in the Character and World windows. They move the same items the mod's own buttons would.
- Moodle Quarters: the mod draws the moodles in whichever place ZeD puts them (right edge, left edge, or the row above the belt), with its own plates and order. ZeD's hover note follows the mod's order.
- Exp Recovery for Skill Books: the saved XP shows as a strip under each skill in ZeD's Skills tab (grey for where the skill is, blue for what the saved XP would add), and the numbers per book stage are in the row's hover card.
- Nested Containers: a bag carried inside a bag gets its own column on ZeD's container strip, in the Character window and the World window, following the mod's depth and filter options.
- Evolving Traits World: its trait progress page shows as rows in ZeD's Traits tab, with the mod's bars, its gain and lose marks, the trait you have now and its own numbers and words at the foot. With Traits As Skills on, both share the one tab.
- Container Capacity Indicator: every container button on ZeD's strips carries a fullness bar in the mod's bands (green, amber, red).
- Wound Expansion: its wounds and conditions show in ZeD's Health tab, in the part's detail and in the injury list, in the mod's own words and colours; its treatments are in the part's menu.

Compatible (loads and works):
- Building Craft
- Neat Crafting XP and Neat Building XP (with Neat Crafting, Neat Building and the NeatUI Framework)
- The Only Cure, and The Only Cure+ (Field Surgery). The Only Cure+ logs its own "duplicate object" errors at load when both are on; the game carries on.
- Kilometers Per Hour: loads; ZeD's dashboard draws its own speed, so its reading is not used.
- Knox Packs: Upgradable Backpacks

Also compatible (loads and works, tested the same day): Viewpoint Clean UI (only its update notice showed), Better World Map, Locksmith, Survivor PDA, Loot Tracker, Vital Signs, Item Details Panel (one error of its own at load), Disable Welcome Message, Better Building Plus with RC's Structures Framework, SideWinder's Placement+, Mod Load Order Sorter Extension.

Does not work with ZeD-GUI: Xen's Enhanced GUI. Both are whole-interface overhauls; with both on, its HUD draws over ZeD's and its inventory opens beside ZeD's windows. Run one or the other.

Still to test: This Is Your Life (the character creator).

How this was tested: each mod on a copy of the test save at 4K, ZeD loaded last, through ZeD's QA scripts (a census of the visible UI, the tab walk, the grid, the item menu, the card on the controller, the dashboard, the Health tab), plus a script per supported mod that checks its feature in ZeD's windows.
