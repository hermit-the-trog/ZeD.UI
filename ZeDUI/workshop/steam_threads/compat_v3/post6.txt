[h2]Not needed with ZeD[/h2]
ZeD-GUI already does the job of these mods, so use one of the two. Most still load with ZeD-GUI on; the part that does the same job does not show.

ZeD-GUI does not take donations. If ZeD-GUI does the job of a mod you used and you want to give something back, give it to the person who made that mod. They worked these problems out first, and some of them do this to put food on the table. Where no support link is listed, the maker's Steam page is linked where known; many keep theirs there.

[h2]Does not work[/h2]
No mod tested so far clashes with ZeD-GUI (something happening twice, or one undoing the other). These are broken on Project Zomboid 42.21 by themselves, with or without ZeD-GUI. Found a mod that clashes? Reply with its name, its Workshop link and what happened.
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2696986935]Barricaded World - Extended Erosion [b41/b42][/url]: Errors as new parts of the map load (it calls PropertyContainer.Is, which 42.21 removed). Not ZeD.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2875848298]Common Sense[/url] by Braven: Stops the world from loading: its recipes ask for a fluid, Alcohol, that 42.21 no longer has. Not ZeD.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2835852387]Disassemble Container With Items[/url]: Replaces the game's furniture-moving code with an older copy that calls functions 42.21 removed, so picking up, placing and taking apart furniture error. Not ZeD.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2737665235]Ladders!?[/url] by co`: Its ladder code errors as the world loads (setLadderClimbingFlags). Not ZeD.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2845952197]Read While Walking[/url]: Errors every frame while you read: it asks for fatigue in a way 42.21 removed (getFatigue). Not ZeD.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3403180543]Week One NPC[/url] by Slayer: Its scheduler errors once a game minute in an existing save (generateSchedule). Not ZeD.
[/list]
