===== 1. New section at the top of the Translations thread's first post =====

[b]Machine translations (from 0.3.0-beta2)[/b]
ZeD-GUI now ships a draft in every language the game has. They were made by machine and are marked "(machine translation, ZeD-GUI)" in the game's language list. Japanese, Korean, Thai, Traditional Chinese, Russian and Ukrainian had a second, careful pass by machine; none has been checked by a native speaker yet. Expect mistakes: words that are wrong for the game, phrases too long for their space, sentences that read stiffly.

If you speak one of these languages, please help fix them:
[list]
[*]Post in your language's reply below with the phrase as it shows, where you saw it (a screenshot helps), and what it should say.
[*]Or send a corrected UI.json for your language (Translate/<code> in the mod's folder). The section below says how.
[/list]
Each language has a short list of the choices its translator was least sure of, the best place to start: [url=https://github.com/hermit-the-trog/ZeD.UI/tree/main/ZeDUI/i18n/mt_notes]ZeDUI/i18n/mt_notes[/url]. When players have checked a language, its machine-translation mark comes off.
