0.2.0-t7: changes since 0.2.0-t6.

Every window
- The whole interface now draws from one set of parts, so windows, cards, menus and the HUD look and behave the same everywhere.
- A box at the right of each title bar opens the window menu: Theme, Accent, Text size, See-through, Controller highlight, Reset and ZeD setup. It replaces the small squares in the footer.
- The footer is a help line: plain words for what the pointer or the controller is on, and the controller's buttons.
- Resize a window from its bottom edge, its bottom corners, or its sides (hold still for a moment on a side).
- One push button style everywhere. An action's verbs sit under the item's name; questions and answers appear in the band at the foot.
- Anything that can't be undone asks first (Cancel, or the action's own word).
- Radio buttons, checkboxes and pop-up menus in the 1993 style replace switches and pill buttons. Tags are outlined, with square status marks.
- Pop-ups cast a hard one-unit shadow. Window text is in sentence case; hardware and pixel faces keep their capitals.
- Status bars are framed strips with a flat fill: belt slots, the hands' condition ring, the ammo tab and the action bar.

Options
- See-through: how much of the world shows through a window, 100 to 60 (90 by default), with Solid while in use.
- Controller highlight: a pointing hand (the default), corner marks, a wash, or marching ants.
- Hidden actions: Folded (the default) gathers actions you can't do yet into one row that says what they need; Show all lists them greyed; Hide leaves them out.

Belt
- The hands sit side by side at the left end of the belt. The main hand is bigger than the off hand, as in the game.
- With one item in both hands, the main hand keeps the circle and the off hand peeks out from behind it.
- With a gun in the main hand, its rounds show on a tab that comes out from under the hand, instead of a pouch on the belt.
- The buckle splits the slots evenly, half on each side in key order (an odd one goes on the right).
- A mod's own belt slot shows the game's name for it.

Maps
- The Maps tab uses the same parts as the rest. Flyer pins are never drawn past the map's edge.

Working with other mods
- Tooltip lines other mods add to items show in ZeD's tooltip.
- Smart Radial Menu and other mods with their own wheels keep their wheel.
- Option pages other mods add appear after the Mods page, under their own title.
- Dialogs another mod sets up after ZeD-GUI loads get ZeD's look.
- Project Writing: its notebook pauses the game. That is the mod's behaviour, not a ZeD-GUI fault.

Translations
- The translators' guide has a note for every one of ZeD-GUI's 3,434 strings.
