0.3.0-beta9a

Fixes
- An item dragged over a rolled half brings that half down at once, even with the other half pinned.
- Dragging a closed stack moves the whole stack, to the other window or to the floor.
- Resting on the inventory's part of a rolled title bar brings back only the inventory.
- Picking up an item brings back a drawer that rolled in, and both windows stay open until you drop it.
- A bat or other weapon dropped on the Equip tab's Back goes on your back.
- The tab drawer rolls in with its tab still selected instead of switching to Loot.
- The item card scrolls far enough for its last rows to clear the buttons at its foot.
- Pin to title bar and Unpin from title bar show the pin icon in the right-click menu.
- The roll boxes are gone from the Character and World title bars. The pins stay.
