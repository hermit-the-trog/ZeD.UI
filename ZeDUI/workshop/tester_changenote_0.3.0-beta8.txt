0.3.0-beta8

Survival guide
- A copyright page opens the book, and 32 more Knox County ads fill its spaces.
- Facts the game keeps hidden open with a find: the TV schedule from TV Monthly, and infection odds. Vanilla Access shows them.
- Recipes show what each one is used to make, each skill level shows what it unlocks, and skills list the recipes they teach.

Servers
- Timed bans are the game's own ban, and the join rules turn away or kick a player who doesn't agree.
- An optional host helper lets a server carry out ZeD's bans and kicks.
- The game's own unban wins over ZeD's.
- Admins get player stats, inventories and claims, plus user, PvP log, climate, sandbox and zone tools.
- Trading and the medical check open in ZeD.
- The kicked screen says why, with a way back to the menu.

Windows
- Every window the game opened on its own now opens in ZeD: the main menu's mod download, invites, world select, terms, controller map and advanced sounds; TV and radio; animal info; paper maps in ZeD's map.
- After a death, Restart goes to the desk.
- Every notice, controller plugged in or unplugged included, is a bubble out of the Messages icon.
- Make-up sits with hair.
- One phrase for the two looks everywhere: Immersive or Simple, Detailed or Simple.
- The game pauses again when you switch away from it, as it does without ZeD.
- A radio in a bag opens in the Radio tab.

Desk
- CletusWorks keeps its notes in a Documents folder, and ProtoPOP3 Mail sends and receives.
- The debug Lua console is drawn by ZeD (debug mode).

Mods
- Seven more mods are supported, among them hotbar, icon and menu mods, and Wounds Overhaul works with the Health tab.
- When another mod replaces one of ZeD's screens, ZeD steps aside and hands that screen back.

Speed
- Restock, Craft and Build, the inventory list, Animals, the item card, moodles, the watch and the World window do far less work each frame. The once-a-second stutters are gone.

Fixes
- Holding A on a locked container opens the lock instead of the container.
- An item card closes when its item is gone.
- The Equipment tab shows the outermost layer: a jacket over a sweater shows the jacket.
- Mail shows the real reason a letter was refused.
- The controller map closes on B.
- ZeD picks comes back with ZeD-GUI whichever way it's turned off and on.
- Split-screen: a second local player gets the game's own windows plus their own belt and dash, and dash miles save on a server.
- French and Hungarian month names are right.
