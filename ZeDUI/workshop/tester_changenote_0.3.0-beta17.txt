0.3.0-beta17

Add-ons and fixes.

Add-ons
- Other mods can now lay out an inventory column their own way, set rules for where items may go, and put their own settings on the ZeD setup page as a group.

Controller
- Every question opens on Cancel, so a second A can't delete or empty anything: the trash can's Remove all, a Journal entry's delete, digging up a crop, taking apart a grill, emptying a container.
- The belt is back: the Character wheel's last slice, Belt, puts the controller on it. On the belt's watch, A opens the full watch, X starts it, Y lights it.
- The full watch works by controller: A grabs the alarm setting, the crown or the speed, the D-pad turns it, A or B lets go.
- Start pauses from every pop-out, the reader, the car dash, the seat picker and chat.
- Every text box takes the on-screen keyboard: Journal writing and searches, Craft and Build search, the main menu's forms and Find boxes.
- Long item cards, letters, messages and main menu panes read to the end before the controller moves on.
- Machine faces scroll back to the top, so the TV's antenna and a card's art always show.
- Animal zones can be renamed and removed, also from a new zone menu.
- A second L3 reverses a sort. Category headings fold and unfold.
- After deleting, unequipping or opening a card in the other window, the controller lands where you'd expect.
- The car's volume is a knob: A grabs it, the D-pad turns it both ways.
- The chat window's menu can be reached, and players 2 to 4 can open chat.
- "Keep these display settings?": B answers No.

Mouse and keyboard
- The Load order pane and the Workshop page's folder list scroll, so every button and folder can be reached.
- Bug report is in every ZeD window's menu.
- The server join screen takes Enter, Tab and the arrow keys.
