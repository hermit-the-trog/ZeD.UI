0.3.0-beta14

A bug fix update, almost all of it for the controller, from a day of playtesting.

Controller: the windows
- LB cycles the Character window's tabs and RB the World window's. Tap for the next tab, hold to go back.
- X moves the selected item, a stack or every marked item to the open container in the other window, as the game's own inventory does. Hold X is still the quick use.
- A opens an item's card. A again on that row opens its menu.
- Eat, drink, smoke and take pills by holding A, on the row or on the card. A tap never eats or drinks.
- The item card's buttons are Grab (for things in a container or on the floor), the main action, then More, which opens the full menu.
- Resting on an item row or anything with a tooltip shows that tooltip after a moment, as the mouse does.
- B with nothing left to back out of cancels the action you are doing.
- Down from the list's header over an empty container reaches the buttons at the window's foot (an oven's or a microwave's).
- Right from the Character window's bags goes straight to the World window's Ground, and crossing between the windows lands where the move points.
- The title bar's menu button can be reached with the D-pad.
- The ID card's photo and studio buttons, the skill books and the item card's buttons can all be reached.
- Y no longer opens the windows over the keyboard, a menu, the oven's controls, the reader or a dialog, and aiming no longer takes the controller from them.
- A station opened from the world (the A prompt on a coffee maker, a menu option) gets the controller.
- Start pauses the game with the windows open, and the pause menu keeps the controller.
- The hint strip sits above the moodles.

Controller: knobs and keyboards
- Every knob works the same way: A grabs it, the D-pad turns it, A or B lets go. Radios, TVs, two-ways, car radios, lock dials, the siren and the oven and microwave. A volume knob that also switches a set on does that with X.
- B on the on-screen keyboard is Back: it closes the keyboard and keeps what you typed.
- The keyboard takes the controller when it opens, so the inventory's Filter and the desk's notes can be typed in.
- Rename opens one box, and it has the controller.

Controller: other screens
- The main menu desk: push the D-pad past a window's top for the menu bar, past its side for the desktop icons. B closes a window, never the main one.
- A places items with the game's Place item cursor.
- The menu opened with X closes when you walk away, and B always closes it.
- The photo studio shows its buttons.
- After a death, the controller works on the death screen.

Radios and TVs
- The full card shows the full-size faces, and the CD player takes the half card like the walkie-talkies.
- A television's tab and title say Television, with a TV icon.

Sounds
- ZeD setup > Windows has UI sounds: Full, Quiet or Off.
- Switching between the windows plays one click instead of two.

Bug fixes
- Food cooking shows a bar on its row, Cooking then Burning, read from the food's own heat: an oven switched on with no power or gas no longer says Cooking.
- Turn on from an oven's menu opens its controls once you reach it.
- The Equip tab's rows and figure show the item card on hover.
- Disassemble's furniture pictures fill their slots.
- Read on the Papers page reads newspapers and fliers.
- The craft and build band's lists draw over the recipe panes.
- Tooltips near a window's foot open upward instead of running under it.
