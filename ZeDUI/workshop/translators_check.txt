Translators wanted, after beta 2: the 24 replies rewritten (Nov 3 2026)
Beta 2 (0.3.0-beta2) ships a machine draft of ZeD's text in every language the game has. The 24 replies in the
Translations thread asked for a translation; these ask native speakers to check the draft instead, as the Turkish,
Chinese and Portuguese (Brazil) posts already do. Each post quotes the label the game's language list shows for its
draft (UI_ZeD_Options_MachineTranslation in that language's UI.json) and points to the drafter's notes
(ZeDUI/i18n/mt_notes/<code>.txt). The director edits the posted replies by hand.

ENGLISH (the master for the posts below)
========================================================================================================
Title: Translators wanted: <language>

Since beta 2, ZeD-GUI comes in <language> as a machine translation. No native speaker has checked it yet. In the game's language list it is marked "<label>".

I'm looking for native speakers to check it and correct what is wrong.

How to help
- Set the game to <language> and play with ZeD-GUI on.
- Post in this thread what reads wrong, is cut off or is not translated: what the screen says and what it should say. A screenshot helps.
- Or work in the file: ZeD's text is in 42/media/lua/shared/Translate/<code>/UI.json in the source code (linked at the bottom of the mod page), about 4,200 lines.
- The drafter's notes on the choices it was unsure of are in ZeDUI/i18n/mt_notes/<code>.txt in the same source (in English). They are a good place to start.
- Only ZeD's own text needs checking. Words the game already translates come from the game.

Credit
Everyone who helps is named in the credits on the Workshop page, with the language they worked on. Tell me the name you want used.

Judul: Dicari penerjemah: Bahasa Indonesia

Sejak beta 2, ZeD-GUI tersedia dalam Bahasa Indonesia sebagai terjemahan mesin. Belum ada penutur asli yang memeriksanya. Di daftar bahasa game, terjemahan ini ditandai "(terjemahan mesin, ZeD-GUI)".

Cara membantu
- Atur game ke Bahasa Indonesia dan mainkan dengan ZeD-GUI aktif.
- Tulis di utas ini apa yang terbaca janggal, terpotong, atau belum diterjemahkan: apa yang tertulis di layar dan seharusnya tertulis apa. Tangkapan layar sangat membantu.
- Atau kerjakan langsung di file: teks ZeD ada di kode sumber, di 42/media/lua/shared/Translate/ID/UI.json (tautan di bagian bawah halaman mod), sekitar 4.200 baris.
- Catatan penerjemah mesin tentang pilihan yang tidak ia yakini ada di kode sumber yang sama, di ZeDUI/i18n/mt_notes/ID.txt (dalam bahasa Inggris). Ini tempat yang baik untuk mulai.
- Hanya teks milik ZeD sendiri yang perlu diperiksa. Kata-kata yang sudah diterjemahkan oleh game berasal dari game.
