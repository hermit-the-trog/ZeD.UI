## Suggestions for the punch list (yours to pick)

1. **The Sandbox page list scrolls.** The list under Find takes the wheel, clips to its box and follows the
   controller's choice of page (main menu's Sandbox step and the in-game Sandbox window, which share it). Kable's
   report; a 1,500-mod list makes it a blocker for big-list players. Worth checking the Options window's page list the
   same way (many mods add Options pages too).
2. **Ente's Auto Sorter's bar over the belt.** Its button bar sits bottom centre over the top half of ZeD's belt.
   Either the belt moves up to make room for a known mod bar, or the Compatibility thread just notes it ("drag its bar
   aside"); a design call.
3. **This Is Your Life (14,000+ subscribers).** ZeD's Looks screen replaces the creator it changes, so it does not
   work with ZeD. Support would mean ZeD's Looks reading its extra options; worth a look at what it adds before deciding.
4. **More Simple Context Menu Icons and Simple Context Menu Icons.** ZeD's menus draw their own icons, so these mods'
   icons never show. Fine as "expected"; support would mean ZeD's strip taking a mod's icon when ZeD has none for the
   action.
5. **The 720 vehicle cluster (test rig, punch list 47).** Still owed its longer bisect; not a product item.
6. **Immersive Moodles (2,100 subscribers, two days old).** Its animated moodle column draws top right while ZeD's
   moodles still show, so each moodle shows twice. Support as for Moodle Quarters: when it is on, ZeD leaves the
   moodles to it.
7. **A ZeD error with round 5's nine mods on.** On the Identity tab, Hair and beard's tool lookup (the inventory's
   search for a razor, scissors and so on, inside a protected call) makes the game throw an internal error twice a
   run. It repeats with all nine on and does not with either half alone; Build would need to bisect further. Players
   would not see it unless they launch with -debug, but it fails our tests.
8. **The marks check's column case (punch list 44).** The one-list rule for a column is still unexercised; the
   script's staging needs the column to hold its second crate. A rig item.
