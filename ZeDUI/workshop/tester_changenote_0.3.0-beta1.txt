0.3.0-beta1: the beta. Changes since 0.2.0-t16.

Full or simple
- A new group in ZeD setup, Full or simple, right after Look: the belt, the action bar, the context menu, the Equip view, the devices, the watch and the dashboard, each drawn as the real thing (Full) or as a plain ZeD card with the same buttons and readings (Simple). Full stays the default; the Vanilla Access preset picks Simple.
- Devices, Simple: every radio, CD player, TV, walkie-talkie, ManPack and ham set, the car radio up close, the ovens, microwaves and fryer, the generator, the propane grill and the washers and dryers are plain cards, with every reading and every button in the face's own order.
- Watch, Simple: a plain clock box with the time, the weekday and date, the temperature, the game's speed and the alarm, and the same buttons; a plain time box on the belt.
- Dashboard, Simple: a plain strip with the speed, RPM, fuel, temperature, gear and odometer, the lamps as dots and every switch as a button, the taxi meter and the police siren included.

Controller
- The controller map: the quick wheel's Help, Controller map opens a page with the controller and what each button does, drawn in your own button pictures.

Bug reports
- Bug report in the ZeD wheel copies every error with ZeD-GUI's version, your game version and your mod list, ready to paste into Bug reports.
- Messages shows each kept error on its own card with its own Copy.

Languages
- Machine drafts of Turkish, Simplified Chinese and Brazilian Portuguese, marked as machine translations in the language list. Corrections welcome in the Translations discussion.

This Is Your Life
- Supported: pick its town in Spawn and a Hometown page lists the 11 hometowns with their origin traits, then the poster stories. A hometown goes on through ZeD's own pages; a poster story sets itself up as the mod writes it.

Windows and cards
- Containers with no picture of their own show the game's box instead of a letter; a car's trunk, truck bed, glove box and seats show the game's own pictures.
- The washer, dryer and washer-dryer are redrawn: the washer a top-loader seen from above, with the water rising through the clothes; a machine on the house pipes reads On the water pipes, and a dryer has no water row.
- The walkie-talkie and ham radio faces lose their printed brand foot.
- Radios: Stop takes at once, a station between lines still shows its signal, and a disc's time runs.
- About shows ZeD-GUI's version.

Options
- 0.5x is no longer offered in UI scale; a saved 0.5x draws at 0.75x.
- Dimmed rows no longer light up under the pointer, and settings that do nothing with ZeD-GUI are skipped by the controller.

Main menu
- The desk has the Kentucky Instruments controller on it.
