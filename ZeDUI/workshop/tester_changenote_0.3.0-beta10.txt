0.3.0-beta10

Balloons
- Every hover box is now one Balloon Help balloon in the theme's colors, with a tail that points at its control, sized to its words at every text size.
- The requirement balloon on a greyed-out menu option lists what you have in green and what's missing in red, in two columns, once.
- The context strip's arrow boxes are in your accent color.
- A submenu opens when the pointer rests on its row, the first row included.

Survival guide
- The guide no longer opens by itself when a game starts. A bubble from the Messages icon offers it instead, during your first hour of play.

Windows
- An item dragged over a rolled half brings that half down, even with the other half pinned.
- Picking up an item brings back a drawer that rolled in, and both windows stay open until you drop it.
- A rolled drawer keeps its tab selected instead of switching to Loot. A drag started on its tab brings it back; on a controller, A on that tab does too.
- A fully rolled title bar splits evenly between its two halves.
- The roll boxes are gone from the Character and World title bars. The pins stay.
- The Equipment, Thermal, Identity and Skills tabs draw with far fewer calls.
- Paper is dimmer: the survival guide, the reader, notebooks, the sticky note and paper maps no longer glare.

Inventory
- Dragging a closed stack moves the whole stack.
- A bat or other weapon dropped on a Back slot goes on your back.
- The item card scrolls far enough for its last rows to clear the buttons at its foot.
- Pin to title bar and Unpin from title bar show the pin icon.

Servers
- The Ban button says when a role can't be banned because the game protects it.

Mods
- Ellie's Tattoo Parlor: Make Up no longer asks for a mirror you already have.
- Custom Map Labels: its labels show on ZeD's map.
