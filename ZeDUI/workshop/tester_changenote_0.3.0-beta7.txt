0.3.0-beta7

Survival guide
- Ads from Knox County shops fill the book's empty spaces in the Full look, in 1987 print: no two alike in a book, and NOTES once they run out.
- The contents runs onto a second page when it needs one, instead of off the bottom. The publisher's own ad faces the introduction.
- The First Days checklist prints at every text size.
- The publisher's ad keeps every line inside its frame, in every language.

Dashboards
- Real odometers and trip odometers, saved on the car. Reset the trip on the dash; the Simple dash has a Trip cell.
- The key has an ACC position, left of OFF: a key in with the engine off sits at ACC, and ON while the engine runs.
- On a server the odometer reaches every seat.
- The Dash Elite's unlit odometer marks are whole at night.

Kills
- The Identity tab, the Journal and the death screen show the same number, fire kills included.
- Only real bombs count as explosions. Bats and guns count as what they are.
- Older characters' kill rows are rebuilt from the game's own count, once, so they add up to the total.

Cards
- Padlocks and combination locks open in the half card.
- Firearm cards show their ammo type again.
- Book cards show your progress through that book.

Servers
- A safehouse claim that's waiting says so. Releasing and claiming again shows as two lines in the Log.
- In safe mode, a safehouse note that arrives while you're joining waits until you're in.
- An offline player's card shows only what the server keeps: no made-up role and no role change.
- Spawn items reads as whole sentences in every language.

Mods screen
- ZeD picks comes back on when ZeD-GUI is turned back on.
- The Z menu shows the right version.

Windows
- Tabs and cards no longer clip the next thing drawn.
- Dates follow each language's own order.

Known issues
- ZeD picks comes back only when ZeD-GUI is turned on by its own row, not through a folder box or Turn all off.
- Split-screen: a second local player shares the first player's windows, and doesn't save dash miles on a server.
- The join rules and timed bans are enforced by the player's own game, so a modified game can get past them.
- Death then Restart uses the game's character screens.
