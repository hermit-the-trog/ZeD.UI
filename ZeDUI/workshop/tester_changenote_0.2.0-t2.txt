0.2.0-t2: changes since 0.2.0-t1.

Controller wheels (new)
- With no window open, each D-pad direction holds open a wheel: Character (Left), World (Right), Social (Up), Here and now (Down). Point with the right stick and let go of the D-pad to take. A slice with more opens in place: A takes, B goes back.
- Character: tabs, weapons, eat, drink, medicine and first aid, sleep, clothing, and smoke / read / watch / radio. World: tabs, map, vehicle, animal, craft and build, search, lights and power, designated zones. Social: the gestures, shout, and on a server chat, safety and players. Here and now: sit, crouch, auto-walk, game speed, zoom, view, help, your light.
- A slice that can't be used right now stays in its place and says why.
- Hold Select for the ZeD wheel: theme, accent color, text size, UI scale, moodles, action bar, reset windows, ZeD setup, safe mode.
- These take the place of the game's D-pad and Select wheels. Everything those offered is on the new wheels.
- While the game is paused, only Here and now and the ZeD wheel open.
- In a window, click the left stick to sort the list and the right stick to switch it between List and Grid.

Controller
- After an action, focus goes back to where you were.
- The D-pad reaches containers in the direction they sit on the screen.
- A on an item opens its card with focus on its main action (Take, for an item in a container). Press A again to do it. B goes back to the list.
- Hold A on an item in a container to move it to your inventory, as a double-click does. On an item you carry, hold A does its basic action (eat, wear, equip).
- An object card (generator, padlock, trough and the rest) opens with focus on its main action.
- The belt no longer holds on to the controller. B, or a direction with nothing further that way, leaves it.
- The bottoms of the Health and Skills tabs can be reached.
- Fixes to where the D-pad goes at the Large text size.

Moodles
- Right edge and Left edge: the column is centered on the middle of the screen. It stays under the watch and above the belt.

Character
- Looks: "Presets" in place of "Outfits", the game's own word for saved looks.

Workshop window
- The upload folder and the preview are checked again while the window is open, so a folder made after you opened it is found.

First start
- After you turn on ZeD-GUI in Mods, quit the game and start it again. ZeD-GUI loads its fonts when the game starts.
